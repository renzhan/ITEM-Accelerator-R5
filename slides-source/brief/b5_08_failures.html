iCEO 与其他实践：成功条件和失败教训iCEO and other practices: success conditions and lessons

iCEO 先展示查事、草稿与能力边界，再用供应商选择与设仓判断说明决策所需的证据。iCEO shows lookup, drafts and capability boundaries, then compares the evidence behind supplier selection and warehouse assessment.

iCEO：功能与案例iCEO: capabilities and cases

  • 员工数字分身统一对外访问，跨平台查事与办事，以邮件演示并验证边界。An employee digital twin offers unified access across platforms, with email illustrating lookup, action and boundaries.
  • 成功：在预算和交付约束下选择供应商 A。Success: select supplier A under budget and delivery constraints.
  • 历史教训：复杂决策未达预期，有限知识无法补全经营背景。Historical lesson: complex decisions fell short. Limited knowledge cannot supply full business context.
  • 当前演示：识别设仓证据缺口，建议先验证、暂缓批准。Current demo: identify warehouse evidence gaps and recommend validation before approval.

其他踩坑：变成可执行门槛Other traps: turn them into gates

  • 行业知识很多但无来源、有效期、审核人和真实任务：知识多不等于可用。Large knowledge without source, validity, reviewer and real task is not usability.
  • 检索、权限、规则和评测未稳定就自建模型：先找清缺口，再决定投资。Building a model before retrieval, access, rules and evaluation are stable: identify the gap first.
  • HR、敏感信息、高风险 Action 不能以试验绕过法规、审批或人的责任。HR, sensitive data and high-risk actions cannot bypass law, approval or human accountability.
  • 只检索 API、缺业务语义：用本体把规则和动作连起来。API search without business semantics: link rules and actions with an ontology.
