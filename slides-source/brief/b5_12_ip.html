D2.4 · 90 分钟 · 自主能力、IP 与商业化D2.4 · 90 MIN · OWNED CAPABILITY, IP AND COMMERCIALIZATION

把内部有效变成可授权、可复用、可交付的方案Turn internal value into authorized, reusable and deliverable offerings

自主 IP 不等于所有代码自写，也不等于自训练模型；差异化可能来自领域语义、规则流程、审核 Skill、评测反馈、工程与交付方法。Owned IP does not mean writing every line or training every model; differentiation may come from domain semantics, rules, reviewed skills, evaluation feedback, engineering and delivery.

先把资产边界说清Clarify asset boundaries first

  • 已有背景资产：ITEM、COO AI Team/Hollis 的方法、底座、模型或工具，按授权和合同确认。Background assets: ITEM and COO AI Team/Hollis methods, foundations, models or tools, subject to access and contract.
  • 联合新增资产：三方共同开发的流程模型、Skill、评测、接口、配置和交付包，权利逐项确认。Jointly created assets: process models, skills, evaluations, interfaces, configurations and delivery packages, with rights confirmed item by item.
  • ISS 业务资产：经授权的领域知识、规则、反馈、语义与运营机制；客户数据不默认可自由商业化。ISS business assets: authorized domain knowledge, rules, feedback, semantics and operating mechanisms; customer data is not automatically commercializable.
  • 第三方资产：模型、软件、数据、品牌、接口与许可证，明确可用范围、留存、转授与退出。Third-party assets: models, software, data, brands, interfaces and licenses, with use, retention, sublicensing and exit defined.
