平台不是功能清单：让一次验证变成可重复交付The platform is not a feature list: make validation repeatable

业务需求会变化，模型会升级，规则会修订；平台的价值在于让语义、接口、评测、版本、回写与运营一起演进。Business needs change, models upgrade and rules evolve; the platform value is evolving semantics, interfaces, evaluation, versions, write-back and operations together.

一条受控链路One governed chain

输入 → 语义/规则 → Data/Model/Connector/Tools → Agent/Workflow/页面 → 人确认 → 业务与能力回写Input → semantics/rules → Data/Model/Connector/Tools → Agent/Workflow/page → human confirmation → business and capability write-back
  • Runtime 面向业务使用；Factory 面向专业建设、测试、版本和日志；Ontology Studio 共建对象、关系、规则与 Action。Runtime serves business users; Factory supports professional build, test, versions and logs; Ontology Studio co-builds objects, relations, rules and actions.
  • Marketplace 属于技术能力层，管理发现、接口、版本、权限、评测与质量；Dashboard 面向人，观察采用、质量、成本和结果。Marketplace is the technical capability layer for discovery, interfaces, versions, access, evaluation and quality; Dashboard serves people with adoption, quality, cost and outcomes.
  • 不要求所有案例迁到同一平台；先登记、评测、标准接入，再按成熟度服务化。Do not force every case onto one platform; register, evaluate and standardize first, then service by maturity.
