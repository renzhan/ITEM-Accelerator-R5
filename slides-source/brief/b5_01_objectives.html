工作坊结束时，要留下什么What the workshop must leave behind

战略成果回答“ISS 要变成什么”；执行字段回答“下一步谁做什么、凭什么验收”。两层共用一份记录。Strategic outcomes answer “what ISS should become”; execution fields answer “who does what next and how it will be accepted.” One shared record connects both.

四项战略成果Four strategic outcomes

  1. 目标运营蓝图Target operating blueprint：未来两到三年的业务工作方式、人机分工、组织运行与共用能力。: how work, human–AI roles, operating model and shared capabilities evolve over two to three years.
  2. 现状差距与突破主线Current gaps and breakthrough themes：事实、优势、影响、缺口、优先级、任务与 Owner。: facts, strengths, impact, gaps, priorities, tasks and owners.
  3. 分层实施路线图Layered implementation roadmap：业务、组织、平台、产品四条泳道与阶段门。: business, organization, platform and product lanes with stage gates.
  4. 自主能力与商业化方案Owned capability and commercialization：候选资产/产品、客户价值、权利边界、交付责任与启动条件。: candidate assets/products, customer value, rights, delivery responsibility and start conditions.

四个执行字段Four execution fields

  1. 首个共建场景与验收指标First co-build scenario and acceptance：问题、用户、范围、排除项、基线、独立样本和验收。: problem, users, scope, exclusions, baseline, independent samples and acceptance.
  2. 联合团队与 OwnerJoint team and owners：ISS Biz、COO AI、ITEM、FDE、专家、审批、运营与 DT 按需角色。: ISS Biz, COO AI, ITEM, FDE, experts, approvers, operations and DT as needed.
  3. 能力输入与执行边界Inputs and execution boundary：数据、知识、代码、模型、接口、权限、日志；真实执行、模拟或人工确认。: data, knowledge, code, models, interfaces, access and logs; live, simulated or human-confirmed execution.
  4. 推进方式与下一决策门Delivery mode and next gate：阶段、交付物、资源、时间、上线条件与继续/调整/停止。: phase, deliverables, resources, timing, release conditions and continue/change/stop decision.
