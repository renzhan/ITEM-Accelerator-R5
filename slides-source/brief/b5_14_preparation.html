会前准备 · 给业务人员的最小输入PREPARATION · MINIMUM INPUT FOR BUSINESS PARTICIPANTS

带材料、基线、Owner 与权限来Bring materials, baselines, owners and access

准备的目标不是证明“已经成熟”，而是让会中能判断目标、断点、依赖和下一门；未确认内容直接标为待补证。The goal is not to prove maturity, but to make goals, breaks, dependencies and the next gate discussable; mark unknowns as evidence pending.

目标与用户Goal and users
一个真实业务结果、服务对象和当前流程。One real outcome, service user and current flow.
最好实践与断点Best practice and break
已有有效做法、等待、重复判断、交接与例外。Existing good practice, waits, repeated judgment, hand-offs and exceptions.
知识与数据Knowledge and data
SOP/WIKI/工单/样本、来源、权限和有效期。SOP/WIKI/tickets/samples, source, access and validity.
基线与 OwnerBaseline and owner
周期、质量、成本、人工介入、业务签认人与更新人。Cycle, quality, cost, human intervention, business sign-off and updater.

至少一个真实或脱敏例子；没有材料的域只形成问题/依赖，不强行给成熟度结论。Bring at least one real or sanitized example; a domain without material yields questions/dependencies, not a forced maturity conclusion.

共建与动手所需Needed for co-build and hands-on

  • 一份已授权 SOP、工单/规则或流程样本，含正常、缺失、异常与边界。One authorized SOP, ticket/rule or process sample with normal, missing, exception and boundary cases.
  • 沙盒账号、预配置环境、角色、记录模板，以及离线样例/录制回放备选。Sandbox accounts, prepared environment, roles, record template and offline/replay fallback.
  • 如涉及源码、系统接口、个人信息、录屏或生产数据，先确认授权、脱敏、留存与停止条件。For source, interfaces, personal data, recordings or production data, confirm access, sanitization, retention and stop conditions first.
  • 明确谁可以改规则、谁审核、谁评测、谁发布/回滚、谁看运行质量。Name who changes rules, reviews, evaluates, releases/rolls back and monitors quality.
