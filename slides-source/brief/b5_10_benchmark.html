六业务域是事实来源，不做互不连接的六段汇报；用同一模板写目标、现状、最好实践、缺口、Owner 与补证任务。The six domains are evidence sources, not six disconnected reports; use one template for goals, current state, best practice, gaps, owners and evidence tasks.

会中共同提问Shared questions in the room

  • 知识、规则、数据、代码、模型、接口与权限分别由谁负责？Who owns knowledge, rules, data, code, models, interfaces and access?
  • 基线、独立样本、人工边界、评测、成本与运营反馈是什么？What are the baseline, independent samples, human boundary, evaluation, cost and operating feedback?
  • 缺的是知识、数据、接口、评测，还是组织协作机制？下一证据是什么？Is the gap knowledge, data, interface, evaluation or operating mechanism? What is the next evidence?

状态写法：四类，不做成熟度排名Status language: four categories, no maturity ranking

已常态运行Operating routinely
有材料、Owner、周期和可复核证据。Materials, owner, cadence and reviewable evidence exist.
局部试点Local pilot
范围、样本或流程有限，需说明边界。Scope, samples or workflow are limited; state the boundary.
尚未形成Not yet formed
存在需求或方向，但机制/材料还未成形。A need or direction exists, but the mechanism/material is not formed.
待补证Evidence pending
不凭印象判断，指定材料与责任人。Do not infer; name the evidence and owner.

每个差距写成任务：目标事实｜现状证据｜影响｜可复用成果｜缺口｜Owner｜补证/建设动作｜下一门。Write each gap as a task: target fact | current evidence | impact | reusable result | gap | owner | evidence/build action | next gate.

会前准备　PREPARE BEFORE THE ROOM　每域一页真实材料，不只准备痛点列表；未知就标待补证，不以部门或团队做排名。Bring one page of real material per domain, not only pain points; label unknowns as evidence pending and do not rank teams.
