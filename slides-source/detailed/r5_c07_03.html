实施：先用授权样本做小步闭环Implementation: close a small loop with authorized samples

输入必须可追溯；困难先作为验证项，不把未证实阻力写成历史事实。Inputs must be traceable. Treat difficulties as validation items, not as unverified history.

输入与建设Inputs and build

  • 已授权 SOP、脱敏任务样本、角色/权限、表单/数据字典。Authorized SOP, de-identified samples, roles/permissions, forms and data dictionary.
  • 允许执行的动作、异常案例、流程版本和回写要求。Allowed actions, exception cases, versions and write-back requirements.
  • 先配置最小 Workflow，以只读/建议模式检查状态与回写。Configure the minimum workflow; check states and write-back in read-only/suggestion mode.

先定采集范围、保存期限、脱敏、复核人和不可推断事项。Define scope, retention, de-identification, reviewer and non-inferable matters first.

六步落地Six steps

  1. ISS Biz 确认术语、边界、Owner、验收。ISS Biz confirms terms, boundary, owner and acceptance.
  2. SOP 拆成状态、分支、异常和证据字段。Break the SOP into states, branches, exceptions and evidence.
  3. 配置最小 Workflow，申请受控动作。Configure the minimum workflow; request controlled actions.
  4. 演示一次自然语言变更、回归、发布和回滚。Demonstrate one natural-language change, regression, release and rollback.
  5. 覆盖正常、缺失、异常、未完成和回滚用例。Cover normal, missing, exception, unfinished and rollback cases.
  6. 运行后由 Owner 审核事件、修正与知识更新。After runtime, the owner reviews events, fixes and knowledge updates.

采用与困难Adoption and risks

  • 建议用业务 Owner＋领域专家共同审查首条流程。Use a business owner and domain expert to review the first flow.
  • 可能困难：SOP 不细、权限不足、异常未记录、并行任务兼容未知、视频信号受遮挡。Possible issues: coarse SOP, limited permissions, unrecorded exceptions, unknown task compatibility, occluded video.
  • 建议用术语会、权限沙盒、异常剧本、双人复核、灰度发布和一键回滚。Suggested controls: terminology review, permission sandbox, exception drills, dual review, phased release and rollback.
  • 明确“建议/待确认/已发布/已回滚”，失败进入下一轮评测。Label “suggested/pending/released/rolled back”; feed failures into the next evaluation.
