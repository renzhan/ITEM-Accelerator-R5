思考：自然语言是入口，不是授权替代品Reflection: natural language is an entry point, not authorization

把每次业务变更变成可审核、可回归、可发布或回滚的链。Turn every business change into a chain that can be reviewed, regressed, released or rolled back.

建议变更链Suggested change chain

业务提规则Business proposes
→
专家审核Expert review
→
FDE 改FDE changes
→
回归Regression
→
发布/回滚Release/rollback
自然语言只负责表达意图；系统必须展示差异、影响、测试和回滚点。Natural language expresses intent; the system must show diff, impact, tests and rollback point.
视频观察仍需单独验证：误报/漏报、复核人、能否影响业务状态。Video observation needs separate validation: false positives/negatives, reviewer and whether it may affect business state.

可沉淀 vs 必须重验证Reuse vs re-validate

可沉淀核心Reusable core

  • SOP-to-Workflow 模板SOP-to-workflow template
  • 状态/权限/证据字典State/permission/evidence dictionary
  • 异常、接管、回滚 runbookException/handoff/rollback runbook
  • 回归用例、失败案例、运营台账Regression, failures, operations ledger

必须重验证Re-validate

  • 术语、SOP、角色权限Terms, SOP, role permissions
  • 接口、回写语义、兼容Interfaces, write-back semantics, compatibility
  • 异常分布、视频隐私边界Exception distribution, video privacy
  • 验收分母与业务结果Acceptance denominator and outcome
讨论Discussion哪些审核知识可以授权沉淀？哪些只能留在当前流程的受控边界内？Which review knowledge can be authorized for reuse, and what must stay bounded to this flow?
