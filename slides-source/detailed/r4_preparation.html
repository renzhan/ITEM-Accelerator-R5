两日准备：新 MD/R5、真实业务、能力清单与会前资料表Two-day preparation: new MD/R5, real business, capability inventory and pre-read table

会前不预设结果；按新 MD/R5 的章节顺序准备可授权、可核验的材料，并在资料表中标注状态、Owner、版本和下一步。Do not pre-set outcomes. Prepare authorized, verifiable material in the new MD/R5 order, and mark status, Owner, version and next step in the table.

A｜八案例与视频证据A | Eight cases and video evidence

  • 每例准备业务结果、前/后流程、实施路径与失败边界。
  • 视频/录屏只放在授权范围；标注真实、脱敏或模拟，不写假结果。
  • Prepare outcome, before/after flow, implementation path and failure boundary for each case.
  • Use video/recording only within authorization; mark real, redacted or simulated.

B｜新 MD/R5 平台与风险输入B | New MD/R5 platform and risk inputs

  • Data/Model Hub、Connector、Tools、Skill、Runtime、Factory、Ontology、Marketplace、Dashboard 的已知与待核验状态。
  • 准确性、权限、Action、回写、成本、价值、复用与审批证据。
  • Known and unverified status of Hubs, Connectors, Tools, Skills, Runtime, Factory, Ontology, Marketplace and Dashboard.
  • Evidence for accuracy, access, Actions, writeback, cost, value, reuse and approval.

C｜ISS 真实业务资料C | ISS real-business material

  • 候选流程、用户、断点、SOP/WIKI/UKM、工单、代码、数据或已有 Agent。
  • 每份材料附来源、脱敏方式、版本、专家与分享边界。
  • Candidate flows, users, breakpoints, SOP/WIKI/UKM, tickets, code, data or existing Agents.
  • Attach provenance, redaction, version, expert and sharing boundary.
