实施不是“接一个模型”：FDE 把知识变成可运行闭环Implementation is more than calling a model: FDE makes knowledge runnable

按版本、样本、专家审核和独立评测推进；困难先作为风险管理，不把未证实阻力写成历史事实。Work through versions, samples, expert review, and independent evaluation; treat unverified obstacles as risks, not history.

建议实施链Suggested build chain

  1. 从 WIKI/已有知识确定范围、主要规则与当前版本。Set scope, key rules, and current version from WIKI and existing knowledge.
  2. 建立小本体，补齐正常、例外、缺失和边界样本。Build a small ontology and add normal, exception, missing, and boundary samples.
  3. 专家确认规则、结果和可接受动作。Experts confirm rules, results, and acceptable actions.
  4. 独立评测拆分回答、规则判断、预测、建议和 Action。Independently evaluate answers, rule judgments, forecasts, recommendations, and Actions.

可能困难与克服机制Possible difficulties and countermeasures

  • 术语/版本不一致 → 版本化词表与审核记录。Term/version inconsistency → versioned glossary and review log.
  • 字段缺失或例外难枚举 → 字段契约、分层样本与风险分级。Missing fields or hard-to-enumerate exceptions → field contract, stratified samples, risk tiers.
  • 专家时间有限 → 短时审核、待确认队列与截止时间。Limited expert time → short reviews, a question queue, and deadlines.
  • 调试样本混入评测集 → 训练/调试与独立评测严格分开。Debug samples leaking into evaluation → strict separation.

采用建议：现场演示一条可追踪链，让专家先改一个规则或边界样本，再形成责任人和回归项。Adoption suggestion: demo one traceable chain; let an expert change one rule or boundary sample, then assign an owner and regression item.
