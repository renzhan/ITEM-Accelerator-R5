FDE 现场实施：从业务需求到可运行能力FDE in the field: from business requirements to runnable capability

FDE 与业务共同完成需求梳理、知识整理、能力构建、测试验证和交付。FDE and the business work together from requirements, materials and rules to ontology, Agent, validation and delivery.

五步实施链Five-step delivery path

  1. 定义问题：明确用户、业务目标、范围和验收标准。Define the problem: user, business goal, scope and acceptance criteria.
  2. 整理资料：收集 WIKI、SOP、案例、数据、规则和例外。Structure the inputs: collect WIKI, SOPs, cases, data, rules and exceptions.
  3. 构建能力：构建 Ontology，设计Agents、必要接口和人工审核确认点。Build the capability: build the ontology and configure the Agent, Workflow, interfaces and human approval points.
  4. 测试验证：用正常、缺失、异常和边界样本测试，专家复核并修复问题。Validate: test normal, missing, exception and boundary samples; experts review and issues are fixed.
  5. 交付运行：固化版本、权限、日志、回写、Owner 和回滚方式。Deliver and operate: fix the version, access, logs, write-back, Owner and rollback path.

每一步都有证据Each step leaves evidence

  • 需求范围与验收标准Scope and acceptance criteria
  • 资料、规则与 OntologyMaterials, rules and ontology
  • Agent 流程与人工边界Agent flow and human boundaries
  • 测试、复核与修复记录Tests, review and fix records
  • 版本、日志、回写与交接Version, logs, write-back and handoff

FDE 的价值：把业务经验转成可理解、可执行、可验证的业务能力。FDE value: turn business experience into capability people can understand, systems can execute and results can verify.

现场原则Field principle从业务结果出发，每一步都留下可复核的交付物。Start from the business outcome and leave a reviewable artifact at every step.
