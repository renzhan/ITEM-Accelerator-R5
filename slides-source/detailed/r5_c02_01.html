WMS 动态表单：需求不再层层转述，现场说出来就能试用WMS Dynamic Forms: say it on the floor, try it right away

仓库现场每天都有新需求：异常登记、状态采集、任务确认、临时看板。难的不是做出一个页面，而是让需求原样送达，做出来就符合 WMS 的字段、接口和规则。New floor needs arrive daily: exception logs, status capture, task checks, ad-hoc dashboards. The hard part is not the page but getting the need through intact and building it to WMS rules.

改造前：需求层层转述Before: the need is relayed hop by hop

01 · FIELD现场提出Floor asks
02 · RELAY支持/产品转述Support/PM relay
03 · BUILD开发/测试Build/test
04 · RELEASE上线再改重走Release, redo

每转述一次，背景、痛点和优先级就淡一分；周期长，上线后改一处又要重走整条链路。Each relay fades context, pain and priority; cycles are long, and one change after release reruns the whole chain.

改造后：说出来，当场就能用After: say it, use it on the spot

01 · SAY说出需求Say the need
02 · DRAFT生成初版First version
03 · TRY现场试用Try on the floor
04 · REVISE对话里修改Revise in chat

提出需求的人直接和懂 WMS 的 Agent 对话，一轮迭代在一次对话里完成；权限、校验和发布仍由 WMS 与人把关。The requester talks directly to an Agent that knows WMS, and one iteration fits in one conversation; WMS and people still hold access, validation and release.
