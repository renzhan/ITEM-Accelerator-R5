改造后闭环：每个状态都有证据与接管人After: every state has evidence and an owner

建议闭环：SOP → Workflow → 自然语言调整 → 验证与受控发布 → 运行反馈。Suggested loop: SOP → workflow → natural-language change → verified release → runtime feedback.

运行路径Runtime path

1
建议·正常任务SUGGESTED · NORMAL
从已确认 SOP 拆出步骤、角色、状态、校验、分支与异常；记录流程版本和状态事件。Decompose the confirmed SOP into steps, roles, states, checks, branches and exceptions; record version and events.
2
建议·缺失/异常SUGGESTED · MISSING/EXCEPTION
缺字段进入补件或人工队列；超时、冲突、未授权动作暂停并保留上下文。Missing fields go to completion or human queue; timeout, conflict or unauthorized action pauses with context retained.
3
建议·人确认/接管SUGGESTED · HUMAN REVIEW
业务审核变更，专家处理高风险分支和视频观察结果；确认后才继续。Business reviews changes; experts handle high-risk branches and video observations before continuation.

回写与最终结果Write-back and final result

4
建议·状态回写SUGGESTED · WRITE-BACK
把完成、未完成、异常、人工修正和回滚回写到 ISS Biz 确认的系统/表单；不假定已有集成。Write completion, unfinished, exception, manual fix and rollback to the ISS Biz-approved system/form; integration is not assumed.
5
建议·业务验收SUGGESTED · ACCEPTANCE
以任务完成、证据齐全、异常关闭、回写成功作为字段；不把流程图或视频识别当质量证明。Use task completion, evidence completeness, exception closure and successful write-back as fields; a diagram or video signal is not proof.
待补证据：状态字典、版本/回滚、未完成任务兼容、权限矩阵、回写字段、人工接管 SLA。Missing evidence: state dictionary, version/rollback, unfinished-task compatibility, permissions, write-back fields and human-handoff SLA.
