DAY 1 / MD §D1.2.8.4 / 小模型训练复盘：样本、规则与可解释性的边界Small-model training review: limits of samples, rules and explainability

如何选型：小模型、大模型，还是两者配合Choosing small models, large models or both

先看任务是否稳定、是否要实时、是否必须解释，再选择模型分工。Choose the division of work by task stability, response time and the need for explanations.

小模型Small model

目标固定、要实时、调用量大Fixed targets, real-time response, high volume

  • 园区车辆车牌识别Vehicle plate recognition
  • 月台有没有车停靠Dock occupancy detection
  • 安全帽、反光衣佩戴检测Hard-hat and reflective-vest detection
  • 卡车停靠后是否放置轮挡Wheel chocks after docking

识别对象长期不变，样本好采集，需要实时响应。Targets stay stable, samples are easy to collect and response must be immediate.

大模型Large model

低频、开放、结果要给人看Lower frequency, open-ended tasks, human-readable results

  • 读各家承运商的提单Read BOLs from different carriers
  • 退货能不能再卖、为什么Explain whether returns can be resold
  • 按货主要求查装柜打托Check loading and palletising rules
  • 查危险品标签贴得对不对Check hazardous-goods labelling

样本攒不起来，规则写在文档里，结果需要讲清原因。Samples are sparse, rules live in documents and decisions need an explanation.

小模型 + 大模型Small + large models

小模型盯全程，大模型做判断Continuous detection, contextual judgement

  • 人车靠太近时报警Alerts when people and vehicles get too close
  • 丢了货，翻录像找去向Trace missing goods through video
  • 巡检拍照盘点库位Inventory locations from inspection photos
  • 月台装卸时有没有抛扔货物Detect goods being thrown during handling

全部交给大模型太慢太贵；只靠小模型，误报多、说不清。Using only a large model is slow and costly; using only a small model can leave false alarms unexplained.

一句话判断：要反复标注、反复改规则，或最终总要人看一眼，就别让小模型独自硬扛。Rule of thumb: If labels or rules keep changing, or a person always needs to review the result, do not rely on a small model alone.
