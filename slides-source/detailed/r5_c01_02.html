01 · DO

DO读取与录入DO reading and entry

  • 读取 DO，检查所需字段是否齐全。
  • 数据完整、校验正常，直接录入系统。
  • 缺少字段或无法确认，转入“Need Help”，由人工核实补充。
  • Read the DO and check all required fields.
  • Enter complete, validated data directly into the system.
  • Missing or uncertain fields go to “Need Help” for human verification and completion.
02 · TERMINAL

码头网站查询Terminal website queries

  • 不同网站有不同操作方式与箱号输入规则，例如部分网站要求去掉箱号首位。
  • 正常查询后，读取实际返回的集装箱信息。
  • 遇到反机器人限制、页面异常或查询失败，转入“Need Help”，由人工接管，不猜结果。
  • Websites differ in workflow and container-number format; some require removing the first character.
  • Read the container information actually returned by a successful query.
  • Anti-bot restrictions, page errors or failed queries go to “Need Help” for human takeover. Never guess results.
03 · DISPATCH

动态派单建议Dynamic dispatch recommendations

  • 每完成一个任务，根据当前限制与实时路况，建议下一个任务。
  • 人工可接受建议，也可拒绝并手动调整。
  • 人工调整时，记录调整原因，保留决策依据。
  • After each task, recommend the next one using current constraints and live traffic.
  • People may accept the recommendation, reject it or adjust the assignment manually.
  • Record the reason for each manual adjustment to preserve the decision rationale.
