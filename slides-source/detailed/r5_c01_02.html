改造后闭环：正常运行，异常接管The target loop: run normally, take over safely

无头 Agent 的价值在于事件驱动的后台协作；安全边界在于缺失、冲突、高风险和失败都能停下来并交给人。A headless Agent coordinates work from events in the background; safety comes from stopping and handing off on missing, conflicting, risky or failed cases.

01 · NORMAL · 建议路径

一条正常运输任务One normal transport task

  • 事件、工单或状态变化触发。
  • 读取授权输入，校验身份、字段和当前状态。
  • 拆分子任务，调用必要 Connector/Tools。
  • 形成下一步 Action，完成后回写状态与上下文。
  • Trigger from an event, ticket or status change.
  • Read authorised inputs; validate identity, fields and current state.
  • Split subtasks and call required Connectors/Tools.
  • Propose or complete the next Action, then write back state and context.
02 · EXCEPTION · 建议路径

缺失、冲突与工具失败Missing, conflicting or failed

  • 缺失信息：暂停并列出缺口，不猜测。
  • 状态冲突：不覆盖，转人工核对真值。
  • 高风险 Action：人工确认后才允许继续。
  • 超时或工具失败：记录错误、重试和最后状态并升级。
  • Missing data: pause and list the gap; do not guess.
  • State conflict: do not overwrite; ask a human to verify.
  • High-risk Action: continue only after human confirmation.
  • Timeout or tool failure: log error, retries and last state, then escalate.
03 · WRITE-BACK · 已知边界＋待验证

接管、回写与最终结果Takeover, write-back and outcome

  • 已知边界：正常路径后台运行；异常保留完整上下文。
  • 建议记录：触发、输入、子任务、错误、人工决定、回写和关闭理由。
  • 订单、监控、派车、状态、结算的接口、权限和写回对象待验证。
  • 本案例没有已证实的自动决策执行或运营结果。
  • Known boundary: normal work may run in the background; exceptions keep full context.
  • Log trigger, inputs, subtasks, errors, human decision, write-back and closure reason.
  • Interfaces, access and write-back objects for order, monitoring, dispatch, status and settlement need verification.
  • No proven automated decision execution or operating result is claimed.
