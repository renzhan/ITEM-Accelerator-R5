从能力资产到可重复交付的业务产品From Capability Assets to Repeatable Business Products

讨论候选产品包，不宣布已售产品：客户问题、差异化、通用核心与配置必须和评测、部署维护、成本及 SLA 一起成立。Discuss candidate product packages, not sold products: customer problem, differentiation, core/configuration, evaluation, operations, cost and SLA must fit together.

候选产品必须回答Every candidate must answer

  • 客户/问题：面向谁，解决哪个可观察业务问题，首个范围与排除项是什么。
  • 差异化：哪些领域语义、规则、流程、评测、反馈或交付方法可复用。
  • 通用核心 + 配置：接口、编排、权限、日志、评测和运维是核心；规则、数据映射、工作流、角色和界面按客户配置。
  • Customer/problem: who is served, which observable problem, and what is in/out of scope.
  • Differentiation: reusable domain semantics, rules, workflows, evaluation, feedback or delivery method.
  • Core + configuration: interfaces, orchestration, access, logs, evaluation and operations are core; rules, mappings, flows, roles and UI are configured.

交付与产品化证据Delivery and product evidence

  • 评测：正常/异常/边界样本、专家判定、基线、质量、风险、成本和人工介入。
  • 部署维护：环境、接口、版本、回滚、告警、培训、支持、退出和责任人。
  • 成本/SLA：模型、算力、接口、实施、运维与人工成本；响应、可用性、异常和不适用边界。
  • 第二个受控场景必须记录复用项、客户差异、权限、语义和重新评测，不用一次演示证明泛化。
  • Evaluation: normal, exception and boundary samples, expert judgment, baseline, quality, risk, cost and intervention.
  • Deploy/maintain: environment, interfaces, versions, rollback, alerts, training, support, exit and owners.
  • Cost/SLA: model, compute, interface, implementation, operations and human cost; response, availability and limits.
  • A second controlled scene must record reuse, customer differences, access, semantics and re-evaluation.
候选方向：服务智能、Back Order/Customer Promise、CEC/Genesys、Service Supply Chain Decision、WMS/Warehouse Business Tooling；名称、客户、成熟度、价格和商业责任待验证。Candidate directions: service intelligence, Back Order/Customer Promise, CEC/Genesys, Service Supply Chain Decision, WMS/Warehouse Business Tooling; name, customer, maturity, price and commercial ownership remain to be validated.
