自主 IP：背景资产、联合新增与授权边界Own IP: Background, Joint Additions and Rights

自主 IP 是可持续复用的业务能力组合，不等于自建模型；权利与商业化边界必须由合同和法务确认。Own IP is a reusable business capability set, not a requirement to build a model; rights and commercialization require contract and legal confirmation.

四类资产先分开Separate four asset classes

  • ITEM/COO 背景资产：既有代码、模型、框架、方法、底座、接口与品牌；记录来源、版本、授权和限制。
  • ISS 业务资产：经授权的流程、规则、领域语义、运营机制、反馈与验收方法。
  • 联合新增：共同实现的配置、Skill、Workflow、评测、连接、产品工程与运行改进。
  • 第三方授权：模型、软件、开源组件、数据、接口、客户材料与品牌条款。
  • ITEM/COO background: existing code, models, frameworks, methods, base, interfaces and brands.
  • ISS business assets: authorized processes, rules, domain semantics, operations, feedback and acceptance methods.
  • Joint additions: co-created configuration, Skills, Workflows, evaluation, connectors, product engineering and run improvements.
  • Third-party rights: models, software, open source, data, interfaces, customer materials and brands.

边界与会中记录Boundaries and meeting record

  • 共同讨论、调用或演示不自动转移背景资产所有权。
  • 客户/员工数据不自动成为可自由商业化的 IP。
  • 能技术调用不等于可转售、再许可、训练或对外发布。
  • 法务/合同确认用途、地域、期限、保密、再许可、维护、退出与收益安排。
  • Discussion, invocation or demo does not transfer background ownership.
  • Customer or employee data is not automatically commercializable IP.
  • Technical access does not mean resale, sublicensing, training or publication rights.
  • Legal/contracts confirm use, territory, term, confidentiality, sublicensing, maintenance, exit and revenue terms.
