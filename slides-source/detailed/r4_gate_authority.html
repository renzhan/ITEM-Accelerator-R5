MD §D2.6.4 + D2.6.6 · Day 2

会议共识不等于上线、预算或权限批准Consensus Is Not Formal Approval

会议完成的是建议、验证范围、责任与下一决策门；企业正式权力仍由对应流程和责任人行使。The meeting produces recommendations, validation scope, ownership and a next gate; formal enterprise authority remains with the relevant process owners.

MEETING CAN RECORD

可以当场收敛What the meeting can converge

  • 首场景、验收证据与执行边界建议
  • 四种决策状态、Owner、日期与下一评审
  • 补证、缩小、停止、回滚或转交条件
  • Recommended first scenario, evidence and execution bounds
  • Four states, Owner, date and next review
  • Conditions for evidence, narrowing, stop, rollback or transfer

FORMAL PATHS STILL REQUIRED

不能由会议替代What still needs formal paths

  • 安全、EA、BIA、数据/模型授权与采购
  • 预算、生产变更、正式上线与权限授予
  • 正式责任人对风险、架构和业务结果签认
  • Security, EA, BIA, data/model rights and procurement
  • Budget, production change, launch and access grants
  • Formal owners sign off on risk, architecture and outcomes

LANGUAGE DISCIPLINE

未确认就保持未确认Keep unknowns explicit

未来方向、目标架构、模型、接口、Marketplace、服务化或上线，只能写“建议/待确认”，不能写成完成。

Future direction, target architecture, models, interfaces, Marketplace, service or launch remain “recommended / pending confirmation,” never complete.

HANDOFF

转交要有入口Transfer with an entry point

每一项暂缓都记录原因、转交对象、所需材料、后续入口和复审条件；不让模糊结论离开会议。

For every deferral, record reason, recipient, required material, next entry and review condition; no vague conclusion leaves the room.

底线：不因共建获得生产权限；不把业务规则判断交给 IT；不把会议记录当作安全、架构、预算或业务审批。Bottom line: co-build does not grant production access; business judgment stays with business; meeting records are not Security, architecture, budget or business approval.
