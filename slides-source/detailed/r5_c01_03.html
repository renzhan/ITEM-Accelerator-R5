先选适合的任务，再把规则、异常和人工反馈跑通Choose suitable tasks, then validate rules, exceptions and human feedback

01 · INPUT → FILTER

选对切入点Choose the right starting point

  • 从 CSR ticket 找出耗人力、规则清楚、容易验证的任务，不只按数量排序。
  • DO录入、码头查询先承接重复操作；复杂派单先提供建议。
  • 明确输入、结果与人工介入的位置。
  • Find labour-intensive tasks with clear rules and verifiable results in CSR tickets—not simply the most frequent.
  • Automate repetitive DO entry and terminal queries; start complex dispatch with recommendations.
  • Define inputs, outcomes and human intervention points.
02 · BUILD

把业务规则说清楚Make business rules explicit

  • DO需要哪些字段，什么情况可直接录入、什么情况转 Need Help。
  • 各码头网站的操作差异、箱号格式与查询异常。
  • 派单需要遵守的限制、路况依据，以及人工调整权限。
  • Define required DO fields, direct-entry conditions and when to route to Need Help.
  • Document terminal workflows, container-number formats and query exceptions.
  • Specify dispatch constraints, traffic inputs and human override authority.
03 · VALIDATE

用真实场景验证Validate with real scenarios

  • 用授权、脱敏的业务样本验证正常与异常路径。
  • 核对录入、查询结果是否准确，Need Help能否有效接管。
  • 检查派单建议是否符合限制、是否便于人工判断与调整。
  • Use authorised, redacted business samples to test normal and exception paths.
  • Check entry and query accuracy, and whether Need Help enables effective takeover.
  • Check that dispatch recommendations meet constraints and are easy to assess and adjust.
04 · ADOPT → IMPROVE

即时算成本，每日复盘改规则Compare costs now, refine rules daily

  • 每次人工调整，立即显示前后预计成本差异，由人工确认；每天结合调整原因复盘。
  • 人工方案成本更低，或成本更高但确有必要：查明原因，补齐规则，经验证后更新。
  • 成本更高且仅因习惯：写入提醒规则，下次提示额外成本，保留人工决定权。
  • Show the estimated cost difference immediately after each manual adjustment for human confirmation; review adjustments and their reasons daily.
  • If the human plan costs less, or costs more for a valid reason, identify why, fill rule gaps and validate before updating.
  • If higher cost comes only from habit, add a reminder rule to flag the extra cost next time, while retaining human authority.
