先选适合的任务，再把规则、异常和人工反馈跑通Choose suitable tasks, then validate rules, exceptions and human feedback

01 · INPUT → FILTER

选对切入点Choose the right starting point

  • 从 CSR ticket 找出耗人力、规则清楚、容易验证的任务，不只按数量排序。
  • DO录入、码头查询先承接重复操作；复杂派单先提供建议。
  • 先确认本体能否描述关键对象、关系和结果，再决定哪些环节适合交给 Agent。
  • Find labour-intensive tasks with clear rules and verifiable results in CSR tickets—not simply the most frequent.
  • Automate repetitive DO entry and terminal queries; start complex dispatch with recommendations.
  • First confirm that the ontology can describe the key objects, relationships and outcomes before assigning work to an Agent.
02 · BUILD

用本体把业务规则连起来Connect business rules through ontology

  • 定义 DO、箱号、任务、司机、码头等对象，以及它们之间的关系。
  • 把司机能力、意愿、班次、路线经验，任务时效、优先级和道路情况纳入同一业务语境。
  • 明确什么条件可直接处理，什么情况需要查询、改派、人工调整或转 Need Help。
  • Define objects such as DOs, containers, tasks, drivers and terminals, and the relationships between them.
  • Put driver capabilities, willingness, shifts and route experience alongside task deadlines, priorities and road conditions in one business context.
  • Specify when the Agent can act directly, and when it must query, reassign, accept human adjustment or route to Need Help.
03 · VALIDATE

验证本体能否支撑真实判断Validate ontology-backed decisions

  • 用授权、脱敏的真实样本验证正常与异常路径。
  • 检查 Agent 是否理解对象关系和约束，而不只是成功调用接口。
  • 核对派单建议是否符合限制，人工能否看懂依据、调整结果并有效接管。
  • Use authorised, redacted business samples to test normal and exception paths.
  • Check whether the Agent understands object relationships and constraints—not merely whether it can call APIs.
  • Check that dispatch recommendations meet constraints and that people can understand, adjust and take over effectively.
04 · ADOPT → IMPROVE

基于本体比较成本，持续补齐规则Compare costs through ontology, then refine

  • 每次派单或人工调整，都基于同一组对象、关系和约束，立即比较前后预计成本。
  • 成本差异反映出规则、数据或本体关系的缺口；每天结合调整原因复盘。
  • 经业务确认和真实场景验证后，更新本体、规则或人工接管条件，保留人工决定权。
  • Compare the estimated cost of each dispatch or human adjustment using the same objects, relationships and constraints.
  • Cost differences reveal gaps in rules, data or ontology relationships; review the reasons for adjustments daily.
  • After business review and real-scenario validation, update the ontology, rules or escalation conditions while retaining human authority.
