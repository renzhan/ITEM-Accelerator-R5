实施不是先接接口，而是先形成可审核的能力卡Implementation starts with a reviewable capability card

从样本到小范围验证，再到采用与持续改进；以下困难是实施风险和建议机制，不是已发生的历史事实。Move from samples to narrow validation, then adoption and improvement. These are implementation risks and suggested mechanisms, not claimed historical facts.

01 · INPUT → FILTER

先把证据变成候选Turn evidence into candidates

  • 授权采集并脱敏 CSR ticket。
  • 提取类型、频率、动作、角色、异常、结果。
  • 合并相似问题，按频率、耗时、损失、数据可得性共同筛选。
  • Top 10/Top 6 只是展示规模，不虚构问题名单或排名。
  • Collect and redact authorised CSR tickets.
  • Extract type, frequency, actions, roles, exceptions and outcomes.
  • Screen by frequency, effort, loss and data availability together.
  • Top 10/Top 6 are display scales, not invented lists or rankings.
02 · BUILD

能力卡与小范围验证Capability card and narrow test

  • 写触发、输入、输出、Action、升级、Owner、权限。
  • 区分真实、脱敏、模拟数据和只读/可写边界。
  • 先验证读取、判断、异常和回放，再讨论写回或高风险动作。
  • Define trigger, inputs, outputs, Actions, escalation, Owner and access.
  • Separate real, redacted and simulated data, plus read-only/write limits.
  • Validate reading, judgement, exceptions and replay before write-back or risky Actions.
03 · RISKS · 建议机制

可能困难如何处理How to handle likely risks

  • 字段缺失或口径不一 → 字段字典与专家抽样复核。
  • 接口权限不足 → 权限清单、模拟环境和最小授权。
  • 异常样本稀少 → 故障注入、边界样本和人工回放。
  • 接管无 Owner → 明确接管人、响应时限和关闭条件。
  • Missing or inconsistent fields → data dictionary and expert sampling.
  • Insufficient access → access inventory, sandbox and least privilege.
  • Few exceptions → fault injection, boundary samples and human replay.
  • No takeover Owner → name the owner, response time and closure rule.
04 · ADOPT → IMPROVE

业务采用与持续改进Adoption and continuous improvement

  • ISS Biz（team）审核分类、规则、优先级和验收。
  • 通过演示、受控试运行和反馈入口指导何时确认、拒绝或接管。
  • 新工单与运行错误更新分类、规则和知识。
  • 发布前保留回归、业务审核和回滚，不承诺全面采用。
  • ISS Biz (team) reviews taxonomy, rules, priority and acceptance.
  • Use demos, controlled trials and feedback to teach confirm, reject and takeover points.
  • Update taxonomy, rules and knowledge from new tickets and runtime errors.
  • Keep regression, business review and rollback; do not promise broad adoption.
