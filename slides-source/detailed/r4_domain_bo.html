BO fulfillment：目标、约束、例外与验收 BO fulfillment: targets, constraints and acceptance

把 Back Order、Customer Promise、分配、补货和异常放在同一条证据链上；不预锁 BO 为首场景，也不把“90%”当已确认口径。 Connect Back Order, Customer Promise, allocation, replenishment and exceptions in one evidence chain; do not preselect BO or treat “90%” as confirmed.

PROBLEM TO VERIFY

目标与约束 Targets & constraints

  • 客户承诺、订单优先级、库存与 ETA 如何共同约束？
  • 异常是缺货、延迟、分配冲突还是数据不同步？
  • How do customer promise, order priority, inventory and ETA constrain each other?
  • Is the exception shortage, delay, allocation conflict or data mismatch?
MATERIALS

需带到现场 Bring to the room

  • 订单、库存、补货、ETA、优先级、异常与处理记录。
  • 真实基线、规则版本、人工分配与客户承诺定义。
  • Orders, inventory, replenishment, ETA, priority, exceptions and handling records.
  • Real baseline, rule versions, human allocation and promise definitions.
VALIDATION BOUNDARY

验证与人工边界 Validation & human boundary

  • 分开评测 ETA、优先级、分配建议与异常解释。
  • 客户承诺、库存写回和跨组织分配须人工确认。
  • Evaluate ETA, priority, allocation advice and exception explanation separately.
  • Human confirmation for promises, inventory writeback and cross-organization allocation.
OUTPUT / OWNER

现场输出 Room output
