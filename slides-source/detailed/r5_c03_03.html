DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.3.3 · 实施过程与采用DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.3.3 · Build, adoption and iteration

从授权数据到可回放的小范围验证From authorised data to a replayable bounded validation

先锁定边界和证据，再建设页面与表单；未知阻力标成可能困难，不写成历史事实。Lock scope and evidence before building pages and forms; possible difficulties are hypotheses, not historical facts.

01 · INPUT

输入与候选Inputs and candidates

  • 授权真实数据、字段说明、现有报价材料。
  • 识别对象、字段、关联、状态与来源版本。
  • Authorised data, field notes and quote material.
  • Identify objects, fields, relations, states and source version.
02 · REVIEW

专家审核Expert review

  • 确认条件、有效期、例外、计算与决策边界。
  • 记录确认人、版本、生效范围。
  • Confirm conditions, validity, exceptions, calculation and decision boundaries.
  • Record reviewer, version and effective scope.
03 · BUILD

页面与表单Page and form

  • 同一本体生成分析页面、字段校验和受控 Action。
  • 正常、缺失、冲突、异常样本回放。
  • Generate analysis, validation and controlled Actions from one ontology.
  • Replay normal, missing, conflicting and abnormal samples.
04 · ADOPT

采用与迭代Adopt and iterate

  • 业务专家和一线使用者用受控真实样本验收。
  • 按反馈补条件、异常、样本；必要时缩小或停止。
  • Experts and frontline users accept with controlled real samples.
  • Add conditions, exceptions and samples; narrow or stop when needed.

可能困难（待验证）Possible difficulties (to verify)

字段缺少业务语义、来源口径不一致、例外未覆盖、页面与表单定义分叉、权限或接口不清晰。Missing business meaning, inconsistent sources, uncovered exceptions, diverging page/form definitions, unclear access or interfaces.

建议机制：候选/确认分层、异常集、版本溯源、规则回归、正式报价权限门。Suggested controls: candidate/confirmed layers, exception set, version trace, rule regression and formal-quote gate.
