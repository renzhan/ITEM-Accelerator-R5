训练覆盖多类邮件，但能正确识别并处理的主要只有“首次取货请求”这一流程明确、相对简单的问题。项目最终没有继续推进这条路线。Training covered many email types, but reliable handling was largely limited to the clearly defined, relatively simple “first pickup request.” The project did not continue on this path.

真实邮件比训练样本更复杂Real emails are more varied

同一诉求的表达、上下文和信息完整度不同，系统未必能稳定识别。The same request varies in wording, context and completeness, so recognition was not reliable.

“货到哪了” / “Any update on my delivery?”

看懂诉求，不等于答得对Understanding intent does not ensure a correct answer

回复还要结合具体业务信息。识别出客户问物流进度后，还需查到对应货件的最新状态。A reply also needs accurate business data. Recognising a delivery-status question is not enough; the latest shipment status is required.

业务专家投入容易被低估Expert effort is easy to underestimate

邮件整理不只是分类，还要反复澄清边界、处理例外并统一判断标准。Email curation takes more than classification: experts must clarify boundaries, handle exceptions and align decisions.
