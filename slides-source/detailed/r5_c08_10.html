案例对照：不同任务的验收标准Case comparison: acceptance criteria by task

任务完成与否，要看约定的交付物、证据和人工责任。Completion depends on agreed deliverables, evidence and human responsibility.

查事与草稿：核对事实和内容Lookup and drafting: facts and content

  • 邮件查询：结论与原邮件一致，来源、时间范围和不确定性清楚。Email lookup: match the original mail, with sources, time range and uncertainty made clear.
  • 回复草稿：符合邮件上下文，收件人、时间和承诺可审，未发送时不算动作完成。Reply draft: match the email context, support review of recipients, time and commitments, and distinguish unsent drafts from completed actions.
  • 人工责任：资料所有人核对来源，任务负责人审核内容与后续动作。Human role: source owners check records. Task owners review content and follow-up actions.

规则选择与经营判断：核对依据Selection and business judgment: supporting evidence

  • 供应商选择：选 A，满足预算和交期，理由可复核且未越过下单边界。Supplier selection: choose A within budget and delivery limits, with checkable reasons and no order placed.
  • 设仓判断：不以缺失资料强作批准，列出证据缺口与验证步骤。Warehouse assessment: defer unsupported approval and identify evidence gaps and validation steps.
  • 人工责任：采购负责人验收选择，业务与财务负责人补证，审批人决策。Human role: procurement accepts the choice. Business and finance owners supply evidence, and the approver decides.
