受控闭环：答复、拒答、接管、回写A controlled loop: answer, refuse, hand off, write back

建议把可验证的问答闭环先做实；复杂决策遇到缺证据或高风险时必须停下并交给人。Proposed: make the verifiable Q&A loop real first; stop and hand off complex decisions when evidence is missing or risk is high.

正常任务Normal task

  • 校验身份、权限、来源和敏感范围。
  • 检索有权限知识，返回证据与时间标记。
  • 仅输出 Q&A、整理或可回退建议。
  • Check identity, access, sources and sensitive scope.
  • Retrieve authorized knowledge with evidence and timestamps.
  • Return Q&A, organization or reversible advice only.

缺失信息／异常Missing or abnormal

  • 缺目标、事实、授权或可靠来源：澄清或拒答。
  • 知识冲突、过期、越权或正式决策请求：进入异常分支。
  • 不把本体当作完整经营背景。
  • Missing goals, facts, authorization or sources: clarify or refuse.
  • Conflicts, stale knowledge, overreach or formal decisions: exception path.
  • Do not treat ontology as full operating context.

人确认、状态与结果Human, status and outcome

  • ISS Biz (team) 作为业务 Owner 与验收方；专家复核高风险输出。
  • 建议回写任务分类、知识版本、证据、人工结论和时间戳。
  • 当前不声称存在成功决策执行或经营收益。
  • ISS Biz (team) owns business acceptance; experts review high-risk output.
  • Proposed write-back: task type, knowledge version, evidence, human conclusion and timestamp.
  • No claim of successful decision execution or operating benefit.
