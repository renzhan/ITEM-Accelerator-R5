查事：查邮件与项目进展Lookup: email replies and project progress

在常用入口提问，在授权范围内查找邮件与项目记录。Ask in familiar tools and find email and project records within authorized access.

ItemGPT：邮件有没有回复？ItemGPT: did they reply?

  • “查一下上周跟 Tonic 发的邮件，对方有没有回复？”“Check last week’s email with Tonic. Did they reply?”
  • 核对邮件线程，整理回复情况、主题、时间与关键信息。Check conversation threads and summarize reply status, subjects, dates and key points.
  • 提供原邮件入口，相似主题或不确定的关联单独说明。Link to original messages and flag similar subjects or uncertain connections.

Teams：同事不在也能查进展Teams: check progress while a colleague is away

  • 同事休假时，在 Teams 中询问其 ibot：“PA 项目最新进展如何？”When a teammate is away, ask their ibot in Teams: “What is the latest on the PA project?”
  • 在对方开放的知识范围内，查询项目节点、风险与下一步。Look up milestones, risks and next steps within the knowledge they have shared.
  • 缺少权限或资料时说明限制，请求可访问的工单、邮件或项目群。If access or records are missing, explain the limit and request accessible project sources.
