查事：从一句问题找到相关记录Lookup: find relevant records from one question

“查一下上周发给 Tonic 的邮件，对方有没有回复？”“Check last week’s emails to Tonic. Have they replied?”

查询与整理Search and organize

  • 查询已连接的邮箱，按时间和邮件线程核对往来。Search connected mailboxes and check dates and conversation threads.
  • 整理发送时间、主题、收件人及回复情况。Summarize sent dates, subjects, recipients and reply status.
  • 提炼回复重点，提供原邮件入口供用户复核。Summarize reply highlights and link to the original messages.

保留上下文差异Keep context distinct

  • 区分“上周邮件的回复”和“本周出现的相关来信”。Distinguish replies to last week’s email from related messages received this week.
  • 相似主题不直接合并，无法确认的关联明确说明。Do not merge similar subjects without evidence. State uncertain relationships.
  • 把跨邮箱查找与上下文核对，变成一次有依据的查询。Bring cross-mailbox search and context checks into one evidence-based query.
