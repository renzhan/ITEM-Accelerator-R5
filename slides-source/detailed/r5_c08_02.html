跨平台使用：在熟悉的入口调用 ItemBotAcross platforms: use ItemBot in familiar tools

不改变用户原有使用习惯，通过 ItemGPT、Teams 或 ItemBot 平台访问同一数字分身的能力。Keep existing work habits and access the same digital twin through ItemGPT, Teams or the ItemBot platform.

入口可不同，对外能力统一Different entry points, one set of capabilities

  • 在 ItemGPT 中提问，在 Teams 中联系同事的 ibot，也可进入 ItemBot 平台。Ask in ItemGPT, contact a colleague’s ibot in Teams, or open the ItemBot platform.
  • 对使用者提供统一的查事、办事入口，减少逐个寻找资料和联系本人的成本。Offer one way to look things up and request help, reducing source hunting and repeated follow-ups.
  • 入口与数据源分开：在熟悉的工具里发起请求，由 ItemBot 连接相关资料。Separate the entry point from the source: ask in familiar tools while ItemBot connects the relevant records.

邮件示例：一句问题查到回复Email example: check for a reply

  • “查一下上周跟 Tonic 发的邮件，对方有没有回复？”“Check last week’s email with Tonic. Did they reply?”
  • 核对邮件线程，返回回复情况、主题、时间和原邮件入口。Check the thread and return reply status, subject, dates and a link to the original mail.
  • 同事不在时也可通过其数字分身查询项目，开放范围与使用权限需先确认。When a colleague is away, their digital twin can be an entry point for project questions. Confirm sharing scope and access first.
