从交付到信任：共同验收，证据说话From delivery to trust: shared acceptance, evidence first

FDE 与业务共同定义标准、复核结果和确认边界；成果是否可用，由可追踪证据决定。FDE and the business define standards, review results and confirm boundaries together; traceable evidence determines whether the capability is ready.

共同验收Shared acceptance

  • FDE：构建能力、组织测试、定位并修复问题。FDE: builds the capability, organizes tests, isolates and fixes issues.
  • 业务专家：确认规则、例外和预期结果。Business experts: confirm rules, exceptions and expected results.
  • 业务 Owner：验收成果，审批高风险动作，决定使用范围。Business Owner: accepts results, approves high-risk actions and decides the operating scope.
  • 系统团队：按需支持数据、权限、接口和运行环境。System team: supports data, access, interfaces and runtime as needed.

可信证据Evidence we trust

  • 验收基线：场景、样本、规则版本和验收标准。Acceptance baseline: scenario, samples, rule version and criteria.
  • 结果对比：预期结果、Agent 输出与专家结论。Result comparison: expected result, Agent output and expert conclusion.
  • 过程记录：判断依据、人工审批、状态变化和回写日志。Process record: rationale, human approval, state changes and write-back logs.
  • 状态标识：已验证、待验证和模拟内容明确区分。Status labels: validated, pending and simulated content are clearly separated.

验收原则Acceptance principle没有完整验证，就不宣称整体准确率；只报告已验证范围内的结果。Without complete validation, do not claim overall accuracy; report only results within the validated scope.
