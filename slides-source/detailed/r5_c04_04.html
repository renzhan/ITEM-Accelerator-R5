组织与价值证据：把 90% 放回正确分母Organization and value evidence: put 90% on the right denominator

职责可以先建议，结果必须后验收；回答、规则判断、ETA、建议与 Action 分开报告。Responsibilities can be proposed; results must be evidenced. Report answers, rule judgments, ETA, recommendations, and Actions separately.

责任与运营Responsibilities and operations

  • Tom 主持；ITEM 对接 COO AI Team / Hollis；ISS Biz (team) 负责业务规则与验收；DT 按需支撑数据、代码、软件、接口和跨部门。Tom facilitates; ITEM connects COO AI Team / Hollis; ISS Biz (team) owns rules and acceptance; DT supports data, code, software, interfaces, and cross-team work as needed.
  • FDE 连接知识、例外、样本、系统能力与可运行应用，不替代业务 Owner。FDE connects knowledge, exceptions, samples, systems, and runnable applications; it does not replace the business Owner.
  • 建议明确本体、样本、评测、发布、回滚与异常队列的维护人。Assign maintainers for ontology, samples, evaluation, release, rollback, and exception queues.

验收与 90%口径Acceptance and the 90% metric

  • 绑定任务、场景、版本、样本来源、专家口径与失败分布。Bind it to task, scenario, version, sample source, expert rubric, and failure distribution.
  • 尚无完整评测时，只标为验证目标，不写成整体上线结果。If evaluation is incomplete, label it a target, not an overall production result.
  • 高风险错误单列；权限、风险和人工确认决定能否执行。Report high-risk errors separately; authority, risk, and human approval gate execution.
  • 训练/调试样本与独立评测集分开；合成样本标注来源。Separate training/debug samples from independent evaluation; label synthetic sources.

待补：正确案例数/有效评测案例数、周期、基线、成本、人工介入与错误等级。To fill: correct/valid cases, period, baseline, cost, human intervention, and error levels.
