EVIDENCE STATUS

四种状态，不做成熟度排名Four states, no maturity ranking

  • 已常态运行：有来源、版本、Owner和核验日期。
  • 局部试点：范围、样本、结果和限制写清。
  • 尚未形成：材料支持缺口判断，但不推断能力不存在。
  • 待补证：需材料、专家、授权、接口或正式审批。
  • Established: source, version, Owner and verification date exist.
  • Local pilot: scope, samples, results and limits are explicit.
  • Not yet formed: evidence supports a gap, not a claim of absence.
  • Evidence gap: needs material, expert, access, interface or approval.
