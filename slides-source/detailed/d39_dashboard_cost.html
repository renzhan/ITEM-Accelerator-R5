Dashboard 连接采用、质量与成本Dashboard connects adoption, quality and cost

Dashboard 面向人，回答业务得到什么；路由和内部计量用于比较能力与单位成本，不承诺价格。The Dashboard serves people and business outcomes; routing and internal metering compare capability and unit cost without price promises.

业务视角Business view

  • 采用用户/团队、有效任务、质量、异常和结果。Adopting users/teams, effective tasks, quality, exceptions and outcomes.
  • 按周期、版本和口径解释变化。Explain changes by period, version and metric definition.

成本视角Cost view

  • Token、调用次数、GPU 时间、服务实例或能力包。Tokens, calls, GPU time, service instances or capability packages.
  • 把人工介入和维护纳入单位业务成本。Include human intervention and maintenance in unit business cost.

路由原则Routing principle

  • 简单任务用规则、模板、缓存或小模型。Use rules, templates, cache or small models for simple tasks.
  • 复杂任务再调用适合的大模型与上下文。Call a suitable large model and context only for complex tasks.
讨论焦点Discussion首个场景要让人看到哪些采用、质量和成本信号？当前能得到哪种计量？Which adoption, quality and cost signals must people see, and what metering is currently available?
