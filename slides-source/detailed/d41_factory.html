Factory：让专业人员构建、评测和治理Factory: let specialists build, evaluate and govern

Factory 连接建设与治理：草案、能力接入、权限、沙盒、版本、发布和内部日志。The Factory links build and governance: drafts, capability access, permissions, sandbox, versions, release and internal logs.

共建角色Co-building roles

  • FDE、Senior User、Developer、COO AI 共建者。FDE, Senior User, Developer and COO AI co-builders.
  • COO AI Team 仅在数据、代码、软件、接口或跨部门需求时按需参与。COO AI Team joins as needed for data, code, software, interfaces or cross-team needs.

建到管Build to govern

  • Group/Master Agent、Data/Model/Skill/MCP/Tools 接入。Group/Master Agent and Data/Model/Skill/MCP/Tools integration.
  • 沙盒测试、权限和 Action 配置、版本发布与日志。Sandbox tests, access and Action configuration, versioned release and logs.
讨论焦点Discussion谁能构建，谁能发布，谁对业务结果负责？Factory 的最小测试集是什么？Who may build, who may release and who owns the business result? What is the minimum Factory test set?
