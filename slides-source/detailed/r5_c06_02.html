从客户问题变化，到服务流程更新的闭环From changing demand to updated service flows

Item CCS 不是一次性改一个 IVA，而是把发现问题、形成内容、人工审核、Genesys 发布和结果验证连成一条闭环。Item CCS is not a one-off IVA change; it closes the loop from detecting demand to creating, approving, publishing and validating service content.

01

来电趋势Call trend

发现某类问题持续增加。Detect a sustained increase in a type of issue.

02

核实是真问题Verify the signal

过滤偶然波动、错误标签和人为改名。Filter one-off noise, bad labels and naming changes.

03

查找企业知识Find enterprise knowledge

从授权的知识库和 SOP 中找到依据。Find the supporting answer in authorised knowledge and SOPs.

04

起草服务内容Draft service content

形成 IVA、路由或坐席辅助的候选改动。Draft a candidate IVA, routing or agent-assist change.

05

人工审核Human approval

查看依据、影响范围和风险后决定。Review evidence, impact and risk before deciding.

06

Genesys 发布Publish in Genesys

受控发布，并保留版本和回滚点。Release under control with a version and rollback point.

07

自动拨测Test automatically

像真实客户一样拨打，检查流程是否走通。Call like a real customer and check that the flow works.

08

结果回写Write back

把结果用于下一轮改进，而不是停在演示。Use the result for the next improvement rather than stopping at a demo.

核心原则Core principle

  • 自动化的是流程，不是取消人工责任；人工始终掌握批准、发布和停止权。Automate the process, not human accountability; people retain approval, release and stop authority.
