A：Skill-based JEV HarnessA: Skill-based JEV Harness

  • 过渡方式：标准接口接入现有能力。Transition: standard interfaces connect existing capability.
  • 快速编排、测评，记录质量、调用链和成本。Compose and evaluate quickly; record quality, call chain and cost.
  • 不要求现有 Agent 立即迁移。Existing Agents do not need immediate migration.

B：ISS Skill-based ModelB: ISS Skill-based Model

  • 目标方式：成熟 Skill 登记、发现、组合和评估。Target: register, discover, compose and evaluate mature Skills.
  • 在 Hollis 框架支持下逐步服务化。Gradually service them with Hollis framework support.
  • 业务责任、托管、模型与平台职责共同确认。Confirm business, hosting, model and platform responsibilities together.
