DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.3.6 · 变更链与知识沉淀DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.3.6 · Change chain and knowledge

规则变化必须走审核、回归、发布与回滚Rule changes need review, regression, release and rollback

把业务知识沉淀为可授权、可审核、可回放的资产；第二场景复用机制，不复用未经验证的语义。Turn business knowledge into authorised, reviewable and replayable assets; reuse mechanisms, not unverified semantics.

业务变更链Business change chain

01 · BUSINESS业务提出费率、报价条件或例外变更。Business proposes a rate, quote-condition or exception change.
02 · EXPERT专家审核含义、边界、生效范围。Expert reviews meaning, boundaries and effective scope.
03 · FDE / ITEM修改本体、规则、页面或表单。FDE / ITEM changes ontology, rule, page or form.
04 · GATE回归 → 业务验收 → 按权限发布或回滚 → 运营回写。Regression → business acceptance → authorised release or rollback → operations write-back.

可沉淀与需重验证Reuse versus re-validate

  • 可沉淀：审核定义、异常样本、版本溯源、字段校验、权限门、评测集、回滚和运营机制。
  • 需重验证：客户/服务/运输/费率语义、接口、权限、计算条件、例外和正式报价边界。
  • 第二场景：不因都使用本体就视为同一应用。
  • Reuse: reviewed definitions, exception samples, traceability, validation, access gates, evals, rollback and operations.
  • Re-validate: customer/service/transport/rate semantics, interfaces, access, calculations, exceptions and formal-quote boundary.
  • Second scene: ontology use alone does not make one application.
