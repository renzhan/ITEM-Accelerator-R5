验收基线与待补结果Acceptance baseline and missing results

  • 分母：预先锁定的样本集与业务边界。
  • 周期：约定回放/运营周期。
  • 观察：概念一致、必填完整、来源可追溯、计算差异、异常升级、接管记录、页面/表单一致。
  • 错误级别：语义、规则/计算、权限/流程、数据质量、结果记录。
  • Denominator: locked sample set and business boundary.
  • Period: agreed replay/operations period.
  • Observe: semantic consistency, completeness, traceability, calculation gaps, escalation, takeover and page/form alignment.
  • Error levels: semantic, rule/calculation, access/process, data quality and result-record errors.

待补证据Evidence needed

样本量、基线、各类错误数、人工时长/成本、接管率、回滚次数、正式报价通过/退回情况。Sample size, baseline, error counts, effort/cost, takeover rate, rollbacks and formal-quote approvals/returns.
