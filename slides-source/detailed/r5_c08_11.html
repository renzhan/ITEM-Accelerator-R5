如何用好 iCEO：让输出与证据相匹配Using iCEO: match output to evidence

结论有依据，动作有授权，信息缺口可见。Ground conclusions in evidence, authorize actions and make information gaps visible.

事实查询与受控执行Fact lookup and controlled execution

  • 有可核对的记录：返回事实、来源与查询范围，例如邮件回复查询。With checkable records: return facts, sources and search scope, as in email lookup.
  • 动作明确且获得确认：执行并报告状态，例如确认后回复邮件。With a clear, confirmed action: execute and report status, as in the email reply.

规则选择与复杂经营判断Rule-based choices and business judgment

  • 信息充分、规则明确：给出选择与比较理由，例如供应商 A。With complete information and explicit rules: give a choice and reasons, as with supplier A.
  • 关键证据缺失：说明缺口并提出验证步骤，例如暂缓批准自营仓。With missing decision evidence: state gaps and validation steps, as with deferring the warehouse.
  • 复杂经营问题先用于整理证据、比较方案和支持专家判断。Use complex business analysis to organize evidence, compare options and support experts.
