ISS 试点：先验证，再决定推广范围ISS pilot: validate before expanding scope

从一个低风险任务开始，用样本和人工对照决定是否继续。Start with one low-risk task and use samples and human comparison to decide whether to proceed.

小范围验证Validate on a small scale

  • 准备正常、资料缺失与信息冲突样本，预先写明通过条件。Prepare normal, missing-data and conflicting-data samples, with acceptance criteria agreed in advance.
  • 业务人员核对事实、来源、建议和边界，记录错误与人工处理成本。Business reviewers check facts, sources, proposals and boundaries, recording errors and manual effort.
  • 根据结果选择通过、改进、缩小范围或停止，不以单次演示替代验收。Use results to accept, improve, narrow or stop. A single demo does not replace acceptance.

通过后沉淀能力卡Document accepted scope in a capability card

  • 写清任务输入输出、来源与更新要求、业务验收人及异常接手人。Record inputs and outputs, source refresh requirements, acceptance owner and exception handler.
  • 保留样本、通过条件、版本和回滚点，供后续复验与复用。Keep samples, acceptance criteria, versions and rollback points for repeat validation and reuse.
  • ISS 待补：知识、样本、接口与责任人，流程作为建议机制逐项确认。ISS must supply knowledge, samples, interfaces and owners. Confirm the proposed process step by step.

暂无实测 KPI，不预设收益。验证流程与能力卡是建议机制，需落实后再确认可用范围。No measured KPIs are available and no benefits are assumed. The validation process and capability card are proposals requiring implementation.
