业务、组织、平台、产品四条路线泳道Four roadmap lanes: business, organization, platform, product

0–3 / 3–12 / 12–24 / 24–36月是规划窗口；四条路线并行，产品/IP近期启动验证。0–3 / 3–12 / 12–24 / 24–36 months are planning windows; all four lanes run in parallel, with product/IP validation starting now.

BUSINESS

业务流程与价值Process & value

  • 0–3：首范围、基线、样本、人工边界。
  • 3–12：进入可维护流程并看结果。
  • 12–36：复制已验证流程，按证据扩展。
  • 0–3: scope, baseline, samples, human boundary.
  • 3–12: move into maintainable flow and observe outcomes.
  • 12–36: replicate proven flows and scale by evidence.
ORGANIZATION

组织与人才机制Organization & talent

  • 0–3：明确三方、FDE、专家、审批、运营。
  • 3–12：建立变更、评测、培训、故障机制。
  • 12–36：形成跨域Owner与维护能力。
  • 0–3: clarify partners, FDE, experts, approval and operations.
  • 3–12: establish change, evaluation, training and incident routines.
  • 12–36: build cross-domain Owners and maintenance capability.
PLATFORM

平台与能力资产Platform & assets

  • 0–3：盘点输入并建能力卡、权限、评测、日志最小集。
  • 3–12：标准接入、观测、回归、退出。
  • 12–36：积累语义、反馈和评测资产。
  • 0–3: inventory inputs; minimum cards, access, evaluation and logs.
  • 3–12: standard access, observation, regression and retirement.
  • 12–36: build semantic, feedback and evaluation assets.
PRODUCT

产品与商业化Product & commercialization

  • 0–3：权利盘点、客户问题、产品假设和验证方案。
  • 3–12：有限客户/场景验证与可重复交付包。
  • 12–36：依据市场证据扩展；不把内部有效直接外推。
  • 0–3: rights, customer problem, product hypothesis and validation.
  • 3–12: limited customer/scenario validation and repeatable package.
  • 12–36: scale by market evidence; do not extrapolate internal success.
