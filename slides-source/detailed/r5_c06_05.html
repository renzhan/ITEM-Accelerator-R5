从真实操作到本体：把专家经验变成系统知识From real work to ontology: turning expertise into system knowledge

脱敏后的操作证据先形成可审核 SOP，再把 SOP 中的对象、条件、动作和结果组织成本体；本体不是把文字换成技术名词。Redacted evidence first becomes an auditable SOP; its objects, conditions, actions and outcomes are then organised into an ontology. Ontology is more than renaming text with technical terms.

第一步｜从视频提取 SOPStep 1 | Extract an SOP from evidence

  • 触发条件：什么问题开始这个流程；输入信息：需要哪些字段。Trigger: what starts the flow; inputs: which fields are required.
  • 观察与判断：看到了什么状态，什么结果进入哪条路径。Observation and decision: what state was seen and which path follows.
  • 动作与例外：做什么处理，何时停止、补充信息或转人工。Action and exception: what to do, and when to stop, request data or hand over.
  • 完成条件：什么状态和证据证明任务已经完成。Completion: which state and evidence prove that the task is complete.

第二步｜由专家审核Step 2 | Expert review

  • 确认哪些步骤是业务必要，哪些只是个人习惯或一次性处理。Confirm which steps are business-critical and which are personal habit or one-off handling.
  • 确认适用范围、例外、不能自动执行的动作和人工接管点。Confirm scope, exceptions, forbidden automation and human takeover points.
  • 未经审核的推断、不确定内容和缺失信息不能直接成为规则。Unreviewed inference, uncertainty and missing data cannot become rules directly.

第三步｜SOP 变成本体Step 3 | Turn SOP into ontology

  • 对象关系：客户/设备/服务 → 问题 → 检查字段 → 判断条件 → 建议动作 → 结果状态。Relations: customer/device/service → issue → check field → condition → suggested action → outcome state.
  • 每条知识附带来源、版本、适用范围、审核人、生效和失效条件。Each knowledge item carries source, version, scope, reviewer, effective and expiry conditions.
  • 本体让系统能查询关系、解释建议，并知道何时必须转人工。Ontology lets the system query relationships, explain suggestions and know when to hand over.
