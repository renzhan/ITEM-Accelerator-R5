十二月后启动：客户验证、资源与决策门After December: Validation, Resources and Decision Gates

这里的“启动”是商业验证/试点/资源方向，不是未经审批的正式上市承诺；产品化从近期并行准备，不拖到两年后才讨论。“Start” means commercial validation, pilot or resourcing direction—not an unapproved launch promise; product preparation starts in parallel, not after two years.

先满足这些条件Conditions first

  • 客户/用户、问题、范围、排除项和愿意验证的结果清楚。
  • 真实/脱敏材料、正常/异常/边界样本、专家口径、基线与验收可用。
  • 通用核心、客户配置、部署、评测、日志、回滚、Training 与维护责任可拆分。
  • IP、数据、第三方许可、合同、法务、安全、采购和正式审批路径可审。
  • Customer/user, problem, scope, exclusions and desired evidence are clear.
  • Real/redacted material, normal/exception/boundary samples, experts, baseline and acceptance are available.
  • Core, customer configuration, deployment, evaluation, logs, rollback, training and maintenance are separable.
  • IP, data, third-party rights, contracts, legal, security, procurement and approval paths are reviewable.

启动后要做什么Tasks after the gate

  • 验证：客户/内部试点，比较业务结果、质量、成本、人工介入与复制工作量。
  • 交付：修正产品包、配置说明、SLA/支持边界、部署和退出条件。
  • 决策：按证据继续、缩小、暂停或转交；未批准不对外上市、不承诺客户或价格。
  • 责任：ISS Biz 业务 Owner；COO AI/ITEM 共建与交付；DT/安全/采购/法务按需。
  • Validate: customer/internal pilot; compare outcomes, quality, cost, intervention and replication effort.
  • Deliver: refine package, configuration, SLA/support, deployment and exit conditions.
  • Decide: continue, narrow, pause or transfer on evidence; no external launch, customer or price promise before approval.
  • Own: ISS Biz business Owner; COO AI/ITEM co-build and delivery; DT/security/procurement/legal as needed.
时间纪律：近期做问题、基线、授权和最小产品包；十二月后按批准进入验证/试点；更长期才依据价值和市场证据扩展。Timing: prepare problem, baseline, rights and minimum package now; enter validation/pilot after December only with approval; scale later on value and market evidence.
启动记录Launch-direction record请记录启动条件、缺口、Owner、日期、资源、领导意见与下一门。Record conditions, gaps, Owner, date, resources, leadership view and next gate.
