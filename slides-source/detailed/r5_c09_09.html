DAY 1 / MD §D1.2.8.3 / 从实践得到的认识Lessons from practice

从模型识别到业务知识架构From model recognition to business knowledge architecture

让业务知识更清楚，让回复有准确的信息依据。Make business knowledge explicit and ground replies in accurate information.

当时的做法与局限What we did and where it fell short

业务边界：逐封标注传递判断，边界变化就要重审标准和样本。Per-email labels encoded decisions; changing boundaries meant reviewing rules and samples.
表达差异：继续补训练样本，但少见场景样本有限且受上下文影响。More samples helped, but rare cases remained sparse and context-dependent.
错误定位：数据、业务边界和模型问题交织，答错后难以判断该改哪里。Data, business rules and model errors intertwined, making failures hard to diagnose.
回复依据：意图之外还需要最新货件状态、客户约定和付款方归属。Beyond intent, replies need current shipment data, customer commitments and BillTo ownership.

实践带来的改进方向What the experience suggests

明确业务边界：整理对象、关系和规则作为共同依据，减少规则更新对重训的依赖。Make objects, relationships and rules explicit so every rule change does not require retraining.
利用大语言模型：理解不同表达与邮件上下文；效果仍需按场景验证。Use an LLM to interpret varied wording and context; validate results for each task.
分开检查环节：分别验证知识、模型判断和接口，便于定位错误与评估改动影响。Evaluate knowledge, model decisions and interfaces separately to locate errors and assess changes.
提供可靠依据：结合适用规则与业务记录；信息不足时澄清或转人工。Ground replies in applicable rules and records; clarify or hand off when information is missing.
专家投入沉淀业务判断与例外处理规则Capture expert decisions and exceptions
少见场景用规则补充稀缺样本，并以案例验证Use rules to cover rare cases; validate with examples
知识沉淀维护文档与 SOP，让知识可查可复用Maintain documents and SOPs for reuse
可靠信息客户约定标明范围，实时状态查业务系统Scope customer rules; fetch live status from systems
谨慎回答依据不足先澄清或人工确认Clarify or ask a person when evidence is lacking
关键认识：业务规则是否适用、信息是否准确、回答是否切中问题，共同决定回复是否有用。Key lesson: Applicable rules, accurate information and a relevant answer together determine usefulness.
