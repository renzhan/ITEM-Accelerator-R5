DAY 1 / MD §D1.2.8.3 / 自建模型实验复盘Self-built model experiment review

自建私有模型的 14 个技术限制14 technical limits of the private model

本次实验的断点分为四层，需结合具体业务任务逐项判断适用边界。The experiment's breakpoints fall into four layers; assess the boundary for each business task.

A · 训练数据层A · Training data
5 项5 items
A1
样本覆盖不足Insufficient sample coverage
每类约 100 条样本，难覆盖训练分布外表达。About 100 samples per class cannot cover out-of-distribution phrasing.
A2
新类别干扰旧类别New classes interfere with old ones
小样本类别加入全局训练，可能降低已有类别准确率。Adding a small class to global training may reduce existing-class accuracy.
A3
标注边界不统一Inconsistent label boundaries
各方分类口径不一致，产生矛盾标签。Different classification rules create conflicting labels.
A4
邮件上下文丢失Email context is lost
孤立标注和回复链切割，丢失语义或混入历史。Isolated labels and split reply chains lose meaning or mix in history.
A5
隐性业务知识缺失Implicit business knowledge is missing
BillTo、客户身份等判断依据不在邮件正文中。BillTo and customer identity are not stated in the email body.
B · 模型架构层B · Model architecture
4 项4 items
B1
识别与执行断层Gap between recognition and execution
槽位、API 编排、状态和回复需另建执行系统。Slots, API orchestration, state and replies require a separate execution system.
B2
分布外泛化不足Weak out-of-distribution generalisation
新表达容易识别失败，运行错误不会自动修正。New phrasing fails easily, and runtime errors do not self-correct.
B3
意图与系统错位Intent and system boundaries diverge
客户语言分类与内部系统功能边界不同。Customer-language categories differ from internal system functions.
B4
语言与字段脱节Language and fields are disconnected
“standard pallet”到尺寸字段需要领域映射。Mapping “standard pallet” to size fields needs domain knowledge.
C · 训练工程层C · Training engineering
3 项3 items
C1
标注能力难规模化Annotation expertise is hard to scale
人员需同时理解业务逻辑与统一的数据定义。People must understand both business logic and shared data definitions.
C2
数据反馈链中断The data feedback loop breaks
生产宕机影响邮件处理，也切断后续训练数据。Production outages affect email handling and cut off future training data.
C3
维护成本持续上升Maintenance cost keeps rising
新场景需重新采集、标注、训练，并回归旧场景。New scenarios require collection, annotation, training and regression.
D · 业务集成层D · Business integration
2 项2 items
D1
接口调用方式不匹配Interface invocation does not fit
面向人工表单的接口，需要参数与容错适配。Human-form interfaces need parameter and fault-tolerance adaptation.
D2
执行层缺位Execution layer is absent
识别后无接口可执行，只能返回预设数据或固定回复。Without executable interfaces, the system can only return preset data or fixed replies.
