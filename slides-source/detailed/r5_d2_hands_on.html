DAY 2 · MD §D3.2 · R5

授权材料动手共建：45–60分钟Hands-on co-build with authorized material: 45–60 minutes

嵌入D2.3的120分钟；完成小范围草稿、能力卡、验收与下一步，不承诺生产上线或认证。Embedded in D2.3’s 120 minutes; produce a small draft, capability card, acceptance and next step—no production launch or certification promise.

01 · SCOPE

范围与角色Scope & roles

  • 5–10分钟：授权SOP/脱敏样本、目标流程、排除项。
  • 确认ISS Biz、专家、ITEM/FDE、COO AI及按需COO AI Team。
  • 无授权材料则切换离线样例/回放。
  • 5–10 min: authorized SOP/redacted samples, target flow, exclusions.
  • Confirm ISS Biz, expert, ITEM/FDE, COO AI and COO AI Team as needed.
  • Use an offline sample/replay if material is unavailable.
02 · FLOW DRAFT

流程草稿Flow draft

  • 10–15分钟：画步骤、输入、判断、例外和结果。
  • 标自动、辅助、人工确认、拒答/升级与停止。
  • 10–15 min: map steps, inputs, judgment, exceptions and outcome.
  • Mark automation, assistance, human confirmation, refusal/escalation and stop.
03 · CARD & TEST

能力卡与验收Card & acceptance

  • 10–15分钟：填来源、输入输出、权限、版本、依赖、Owner。
  • 用正常/异常/边界样本区分建议、Action调用与业务结果。
  • 10–15 min: capture source, I/O, access, version, dependencies and Owner.
  • Use normal, abnormal and boundary samples to separate advice, Action call and business outcome.
04 · NEXT GATE

下一步而非上线Next step, not launch

  • 5分钟：补证据、责任人、日期、投入、审批路径。
  • 选择继续/有条件继续/补证缩小/暂缓转交。
  • 不足时缩小或停止，不强行产品化。
  • 5 min: evidence, Owner, date, effort and approval path.
  • Choose continue / conditional / narrow for evidence / pause-transfer.
  • Narrow or stop when weak; do not force productization.
