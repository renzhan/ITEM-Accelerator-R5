容易失败的地方：把需求线索推成投资结论Decision risk: jumping from demand to investment

需要更快交付、应在美东备货、应选北新泽西、应租仓自营，需要分别证明。Faster delivery, East Coast stock, northern New Jersey and direct operation each require separate evidence.

从服务问题到需求证明From service issue to demand evidence

  • 配送慢：先确认根因，以及新仓能改善多少。Slow delivery: identify the cause and how much a new warehouse would improve it.
  • 有需求：验证可兑现订单、贡献利润和持续时间。Demand: validate realizable orders, contribution margin and duration.
  • 现有仓无法承接：比较其他可行的承接方式。Existing warehouse limits: compare other feasible ways to serve the demand.

从方案设想到投资验证From proposal to investment validation

  • 比较自营与第三方仓、共享仓、小规模试点。Compare direct operation with third-party warehousing, shared sites and a small pilot.
  • 核算完整成本、现金流和退出风险，再判断是否值得投资。Assess full costs, cash flow and exit risk before judging the investment.
  • 任一步缺少依据，最终建议都可能依赖未经验证的假设。A missing link can leave the final recommendation resting on untested assumptions.
