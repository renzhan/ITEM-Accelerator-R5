复杂案例分析：历史教训与当前改进Complex case analysis: historical lessons and current progress

历史尝试未达预期，当前设仓回答选择暂缓批准，将信息不足转为验证任务。Historical attempts fell short. The current warehouse response defers approval and turns evidence gaps into validation tasks.

历史教训：建议需要业务证据Historical lesson: proposals need business evidence

  • 早期采集知识、构建 PA 并生成建议，原记录显示复杂决策未达业务预期。Early efforts captured knowledge, built a PA and generated proposals. Records report unmet expectations for complex decisions.
  • 有限知识难以补全经营背景，目标、成本与替代方案应逐项核对。Limited knowledge can leave business context incomplete. Check goals, costs and alternatives individually.
  • 缺少早期完整输入、答复与验收记录，具体错误原因仍需复盘验证。Without the full original inputs, responses and acceptance records, specific causes still require review.

当前改进：先补证，再审批Current progress: gather evidence before approval

  • 实际回答未直接批准租仓自营，说明缺少原始资料，建议推进可研与限额验证。The actual response deferred direct-operation approval, stated source gaps and proposed feasibility work and bounded validation.
  • 补齐订单与交付证据、完整成本和现金流，并比较第三方仓、共享仓与试点。Gather order and delivery evidence, full costs and cash flow, and compare third-party warehousing, shared sites and pilots.
  • 明确补证人与审批人，核对人员、系统、租约和退出条件后再判断。Name evidence owners and approvers, and check staffing, systems, lease and exit terms before deciding.

历史失败记录与当前演示分开评价：暂缓批准是当前案例的合理表现。Evaluate historical failure separately from the current demo: deferring approval is a reasonable response here.
