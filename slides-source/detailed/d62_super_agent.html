MD §D1.4.2 · 编排定义

Super Agent：业务编排，不是无限权限Super Agent: orchestration, not unlimited access

先问首个场景是否真的需要跨能力编排；简单流程足够时，不额外增加复杂度。First ask whether the scenario needs cross-capability orchestration; do not add complexity when a simple flow is enough.

工作定义Working definition

围绕业务目标，在授权范围内理解上下文、拆解任务、调用已验证 Skill/Agent/Tools、处理异常并请求人工确认。Around a business goal, understand context within authorization, decompose tasks, call verified Skills/Agents/Tools, handle exceptions and request human confirmation.

必须写清Make explicit

  • 目标与范围、子能力输入/输出、最大权限Goal/scope, sub-capability inputs/outputs and maximum access
  • 失败/终止条件、上下文隔离、调用成本、最终 OwnerFailure/stop conditions, context isolation, call cost and final Owner

不能越过Must not cross

  • 不能凭空增加事实或越权调用另一 AgentIt cannot invent facts or use another Agent’s access
  • 工具返回不等于业务任务完成A tool response is not business completion

决策输出Decision output

首个场景是否需要 Master/Group/Super Agent；若不需要，保留简单流程。Decide whether the first scenario needs a Master/Group/Super Agent; otherwise keep the simpler flow.

讨论焦点Discussion哪个业务目标需要编排？最大权限和最终 Owner 是谁？Which business goal needs orchestration? What is the maximum access and final Owner?
