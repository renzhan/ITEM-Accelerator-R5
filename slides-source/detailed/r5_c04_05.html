会中具体追问Questions to answer live

  1. 当前端到端流程、平均周期、重复查询和交接点是什么？What are the end-to-end flow, average cycle, repeat queries, and handoffs?

  3. 哪些字段可读/可写？哪些 Action 必须人工审批？Which fields are readable/writable, and which Actions need human approval?
  4. 谁维护本体、样本、评测、发布、回滚和异常队列？Who maintains ontology, samples, evaluation, release, rollback, and exception queues?
