进入新场景前：先确认能否复用Before entering a new scenario: confirm what can be reused

FDE 复用实施方法，但不直接复制规则、数据和结果；现场先确认差距与边界。FDE reuses the delivery method, not the rules, data or results; gaps and boundaries must be confirmed on site.

可以复用的方法What can be reused

  • 需求、范围和验收标准的定义方式。How requirements, scope and acceptance criteria are defined.
  • Ontology 和业务对象的建模方法。How the ontology and business objects are modeled.
  • 正常、异常、缺失和边界样本的组织方式。How normal, exception, missing and boundary samples are organized.
  • Agent 流程、人工确认、批量测试和问题分类方法。Agent flows, human approval, batch testing and issue classification.
  • 版本、发布、回滚和责任管理机制。Versioning, release, rollback and ownership management.

必须重新确认的条件What must be revalidated

  • 业务术语、规则和服务承诺。Business terms, rules and service promises.
  • 数据字段、来源、版本和样本分布。Data fields, sources, versions and sample distribution.
  • 系统接口、读写权限和审批链。System interfaces, read/write access and approval chain.
  • 高风险动作、人工确认边界和业务 Owner。High-risk actions, human approval boundaries and the business Owner.
  • 新场景的验收标准和使用范围。Acceptance criteria and operating scope for the new scenario.

复用原则Reuse principle复用的是实施方法和治理机制，不是直接复制某个案例的规则、结果或准确率。Reuse the delivery method and governance, not the rules, results or accuracy of one case.
