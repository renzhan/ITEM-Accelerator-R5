其他踩过的坑：先定义可验收的业务结果Pitfalls: Define an Acceptable Business Result First

六类问题的共同根因不是“模型不够大”，而是任务、证据、权限、责任与交接没有先闭环。Across six pitfalls, the root issue is not model size; task, evidence, access, ownership, and handoff were not closed first.

改造前：资料多，交接不清Before: many materials, unclear handoffs

  • 知识汇总缺角色、流程、更新责任和实际任务。
  • CSR 在知识、检索、权限、评测未稳时就可能先训练模型。
  • HR 自动化可能把技术可行误当作可合法上线。
  • LLM 可能补出不存在的事实、规则或系统状态。
  • Knowledge lacks roles, workflows, update owners, and real tasks.
  • CSR training may start before knowledge, retrieval, access, and evaluation are stable.
  • HR automation may treat technical feasibility as legal readiness.
  • An LLM may invent facts, rules, or system states.
