成功案例：三家供应商，如何选出一家？Success case: choosing among three suppliers

采购 100 套办公用品，满足全部要求后选择总价最低者。Buy 100 office-supply sets. Choose the lowest total price among compliant offers.

采购条件与报价Requirements and quotations

  • 预算不超过 10,000 元，订单确认后 5 个工作日内到货。Budget: CNY 10,000. Delivery: within 5 working days of confirmation.
  • 三家资质、规格、质量均合格，足量供应，报价含税和运费。All meet qualification, specification, quality and quantity requirements. Prices include tax and delivery.
  • A：9,200 元／3 天；B：8,600 元／7 天；C：9,800 元／2 天。A: CNY 9,200 / 3 days. B: CNY 8,600 / 7 days. C: CNY 9,800 / 2 days.
  • 用户要求：直接给出选择与理由，只做选择，不下单。Request: provide a choice and reasons, without placing an order.
