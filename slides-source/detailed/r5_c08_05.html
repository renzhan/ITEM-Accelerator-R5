任务定义：做什么、交付什么、谁验收Task definition: scope, deliverables and acceptance owner

把“接入了多少资料”改为“完成什么任务、怎样验收”。Define the task and acceptance criteria alongside the connected sources.

任务卡：输入与交付物Task card: inputs and deliverables

  • 任务：选一个低风险、边界清楚的业务问题，写明范围与约束。Task: choose a low-risk business question with clear scope and constraints.
  • 输入：列出必需资料、来源、时间范围与更新要求。Inputs: list required records, sources, time range and refresh requirements.
  • 输出：约定答案、来源、草稿或选择理由，信息不足时列缺口。Outputs: agree on answers, sources, drafts or selection reasons, including gaps when evidence is missing.

任务卡：责任与验收证据Task card: ownership and acceptance evidence

  • 负责人：指定业务验收人，明确异常时由谁补充资料或接手。Owner: name the business acceptance owner and who supplies missing evidence or handles exceptions.
  • 验收：准备样本与人工对照记录，逐项检查事实、来源和边界。Acceptance: use samples and human comparison records to check facts, sources and boundaries.
  • 状态：区分回答完成、草稿待审与动作完成，保留相应证据。Status: distinguish answered, draft under review and action completed, with supporting evidence.
