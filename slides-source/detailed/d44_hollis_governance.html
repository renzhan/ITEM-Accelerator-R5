Open → Transform → Engineer 的阶段治理Open → Transform → Engineer: staged governance

保留探索速度，同时逐步补齐场景、接口、权限、部署、监控、评估、日志、成本和接管。Keep exploration fast while progressively adding context, interfaces, access, deployment, monitoring, evaluation, logs, cost and takeover.

Open

  • 快速探索，允许局部创新和假设。Explore quickly with local innovation and hypotheses.
  • 先登记，不把探索当生产承诺。Register first; exploration is not a production promise.

Transform

  • 放进真实场景、知识、角色与指标。Put it in real context, knowledge, roles and metrics.
  • 按样本、规则和风险评测分类。Evaluate and classify by samples, rules and risk.

Engineer

  • 标准接入，按成熟度认证。Standardize integration and certify by maturity.
  • 必要时托管与服务化，保留创新边界。Host and service when needed while preserving innovation boundaries.
