两类回写：业务事实与知识能力Two writebacks: business facts and capability knowledge

建议、调用成功和业务执行成功必须分开记录；未经审核的推断不能覆盖正式事实。Separate recommendation, call success and business execution success; unreviewed inference must not overwrite facts.

回写业务平台Write back to business systems

  • 订单/任务状态、流程结果、确认记录和必要动作。Order/task status, workflow result, approvals and necessary actions.
  • 明确源系统、写入权限、幂等、重试、冲突和追踪标识。Define source, write permission, idempotency, retry, conflict and trace ID.

回写能力与知识Write back capability and knowledge

  • 执行日志、失败、人工修正、经审核 SOP 与知识缺口。Execution logs, failures, human fixes, reviewed SOPs and knowledge gaps.
  • 反馈到 UKM、本体、Skill、Agent 和治理平台。Feed UKM, ontology, Skill, Agent and governance layers.
