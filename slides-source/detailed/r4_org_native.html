MD §D2.4.1 · Day 2

AI 原生：重排信息、决策、执行与责任AI-Native: Rewire Work and Accountability

AI 原生不是增加聊天入口，而是围绕业务结果重新组织信息流、判断点、执行动作、反馈和责任。AI-native work is not another chat entry; it reorganizes information, decisions, actions, feedback and accountability around outcomes.

01 · INFORMATION

信息可用Useful information

  • 业务事实、规则、知识与来源可追踪
  • 原始记录、AI 提取、SOP 草稿、审核分层
  • Traceable business facts, rules, knowledge and sources
  • Separate raw records, AI extraction, SOP drafts and review

02 · DECISION

判断有边界Bounded decisions

  • 标记规则、例外、拒答、升级和人工确认
  • Agent 不自行扩权限，不把建议写成批准
  • Mark rules, exceptions, refusal, escalation and human confirmation
  • Agents do not expand access or turn advice into approval

03 · EXECUTION

执行可控Controlled execution

  • Action、系统回写、权限和停止条件可查
  • 高风险动作保留人确认与正式审批
  • Trace actions, write-back, access and stop conditions
  • Keep human confirmation and formal approval for high risk

04 · FEEDBACK

反馈回到能力Feedback into capability

  • 观测采用、质量、成本、风险和业务结果
  • 反馈触发训练、版本、回归或退出
  • Observe adoption, quality, cost, risk and business outcomes
  • Feedback triggers training, versioning, regression or retirement
组织原则：ISS Biz 保留业务规则与价值判断；技术团队负责实现与治理协同，不替代业务 Owner。Operating principle: ISS Biz keeps business rules and value judgment; technical teams implement and coordinate governance without replacing the business Owner.
