ISS 对标：先填差距，再决定迁移ISS benchmark: map gaps before deciding migration

对标不是宣布已有能力，而是把软件、知识、数据、接口和组织的缺口变成会中可回答的问题。Benchmarking does not declare capability; it turns software, knowledge, data, interface and organisation gaps into answerable questions.

差距地图Gap map

维度Dimension先确认什么First confirm
软件/源码Software/source版本、源码、组件和权限范围Version, source, components and access scope
知识/字段Knowledge/fields对象、状态、字段字典、专家解释Objects, states, field dictionary and expert meaning
数据/接口Data/interfaces脱敏样例、状态更新、审计、回滚Redacted samples, state update, audit and rollback
组织/工程Org/engineeringOwner、审核、发布、维护、DT 进入条件Owner, review, release, maintenance and DT entry

会中直接提问并确认Ask and confirm in the room

  • Q1 哪一个 ISS 页面痛点明确且可拿到源码与本地环境？Which ISS page has a clear pain and accessible source and local environment?
  • Q2 最低授权范围、数据批准人和字段 Owner 是谁？What is the minimum access, data approver and field Owner?
  • Q3 哪些 Action 只能模拟或必须人确认？Which Actions must be simulated or human-confirmed?
  • Q4 业务验收、代码审核、发布、运营和回滚分别由谁负责？Who owns acceptance, code review, release, operations and rollback?

现场确认：Owner 由会中指定；版本/环境由会中记录；证据位置由会中登记。Confirm live: assign the Owner in the room; record the version/environment; register the evidence location.

讨论Discussion如果源码或接口拿不到，是否缩小为只读/模拟验证，还是转交 DT？If source or interfaces are unavailable, do we narrow to read-only/simulation or hand off to DT?
