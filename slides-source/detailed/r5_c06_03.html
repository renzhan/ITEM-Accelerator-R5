SOP 如何从真实操作中沉淀出来How SOPs emerge from real-world operations

专家可以直接写 SOP；经授权的录屏则补足真实操作中的隐性判断，也让团队能够比较不同专家处理同类问题的路径，找出更稳定、可复用的经验，最后仍由专家确认。Experts can write SOPs directly. Authorised recordings add tacit decisions from real operations and let teams compare how different experts handle the same problem, identify more stable and reusable practices, and leave final approval to experts.

从真实过程提炼可复用经验Extract reusable experience from real processes

  • 专家可以直接写 SOP；录屏的价值，是保留实际字段、操作顺序、系统切换、判断分支和最终结果。Experts can write SOPs directly; recordings preserve actual fields, action order, system switches, decision branches and final outcomes.
  • 把不同专家处理同类问题的路径放在一起比较，看处理结果、返工情况、稳定性和适用范围，识别更值得复用的经验。Compare different experts’ paths for the same problem by outcome, rework, stability and applicability to identify experience worth reusing.
  • 录屏只提供候选证据，看不到全部思考；专家补充判断依据、适用条件和例外，确认后才形成 SOP。Recordings provide candidate evidence, not the full reasoning; experts add rationale, applicability and exceptions before approving an SOP.

SOP 的产生路径How an SOP is produced

  • 真实操作记录 → 提取不同处理路径 → 比较结果与适用条件。Real operation records → extract different paths → compare outcomes and applicability.
  • 专家确认更优或更稳妥的做法，补齐“为什么这样做”和不能照搬的例外。Experts confirm the better or safer approach and add why it works and which exceptions cannot be copied.
  • 通过测试、版本化和回归验证后，才发布为可复用 SOP；不是系统自动决定谁的经验最好。Only after testing, versioning and regression does it become a reusable SOP; the system does not automatically rank whose experience is best.

录屏不是唯一来源：已有准确 SOP 直接复用；不能录制时采用访谈或演示。只有文档不足、流程复杂且需要保留真实过程时，才补充授权录屏。Recording is not the only source: reuse accurate SOPs; use interviews or demonstrations when recording is not permitted. Add authorised recordings only when documentation is insufficient, the work is complex and the real process needs to be preserved.

讨论Discussion我们需要的不是“监控员工”，而是获得经过授权的业务证据；下一页说明这些证据如何安全脱敏。We need authorised business evidence, not employee surveillance; the next page explains how that evidence is safely redacted.
