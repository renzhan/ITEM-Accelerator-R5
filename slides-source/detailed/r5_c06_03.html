从被动观察到受控采用：建议的实施路径From passive observation to controlled adoption

先把真实流程转成可审核SOP，再逐步开放知识、建议和允许的后台动作；未证实的阻力只作为可能困难。Turn real work into an auditable SOP first, then open knowledge, suggestions and permitted back-office Actions step by step; unverified resistance remains a possibility.

输入与建设Inputs and build

  • ISS Biz（team）与坐席选一个可授权、可脱敏、可复盘的高频任务。ISS Biz (team) and agents select an authorised, redacted and reviewable frequent task.
  • Tom主持；ITEM对接COO AI Team/Hollis共同开发；DT按需支撑。Tom hosts; ITEM works with COO AI Team/Hollis; DT supports as needed.
  • 逐段标注字段、判断、切换、例外、权限、输出与接管点。Annotate fields, decisions, switches, exceptions, access, outputs and takeover points.

可能困难｜建议机制Possible difficulties | Proposed controls

  • 授权/隐私：Consent/privacy: 先脱敏或模拟，保留差异清单。start redacted or simulated; keep a difference log.
  • 隐性判断：Tacit judgement: 用自动/辅助/人工审核表，勿强行产品化。use an automate/assist/human review sheet; do not force productisation.
  • 接口边界：Interface scope: 先画最小闭环，逐项确认权限、日志、回写。draw the minimum loop and confirm access, logs and write-back item by item.

采用与持续改进Adoption and improvement

  • 先展示建议、来源、缺失字段和接管点，再决定是否受控执行。Show suggestion, source, missing fields and takeover before controlled execution.
  • 每轮只扩大已验证范围；用抽检、版本、权限复核、回归更新知识和规则。Expand only validated scope; use sampling, versioning, access review and regression.
  • 实际采用率、质量、成本和运营结果待测。Adoption, quality, cost and operating results remain to be measured.

建设顺序建议：观察标注 → 专家审核 → UKM/Ontology/Skill → Connector/Tools → 小范围回归 → 受控发布。Suggested order: observe and annotate → expert review → UKM/Ontology/Skill → Connector/Tools → small regression → controlled release.
