为什么不只让专家写 SOP？Why not rely only on experts writing SOPs?

不是专家不能写，而是从零写文档会占用解决问题的时间，也容易漏掉熟练操作中的判断。授权录屏辅助整理，专家仍负责解释和确认。Experts can write SOPs, but documenting from scratch takes time away from solving problems and may omit tacit decisions. Authorised recordings help draft them; experts still explain and approve.

让专家审核经验，而不是从零整理Let experts review rather than draft from scratch

  • 写文档需要重现步骤、截图和说明例外；专家忙于处理新问题，文档容易滞后。Recreating steps, screenshots and exceptions takes time; documentation can lag while experts resolve new issues.
  • 熟练操作容易被概括成“检查一下”；录屏保留实际字段、顺序、分支和结果，减少事后回忆遗漏。Experts may summarise tacit steps as “check it”; recordings preserve fields, order, branches and outcomes that memory may omit.
  • 录屏看得到动作，看不到全部思考；系统整理候选 SOP，专家补充为什么、适用条件和不能照搬的例外。Recording shows actions, not all reasoning. The system drafts an SOP; experts add rationale, applicability and exceptions.

授权观察的范围Scope of authorised observation

  • 在员工知情、业务授权和明确用途的前提下，采集指定任务的屏幕操作和流程证据。With informed staff participation, business authorisation and a defined purpose, capture screen actions and process evidence for a specified task.
  • 只观察必要的任务时段和窗口，不做无边界、无期限的全屏监控。Observe only the necessary task and windows, not unrestricted full-screen monitoring without an end date.
  • 保留字段、判断、例外和最终状态；等待、重复点击和无效操作不直接变成 SOP。Keep fields, decisions, exceptions and final state; waiting, repeated clicks and noise do not become SOP by default.

录屏不是唯一来源：已有准确 SOP 直接复用；不能录制时采用访谈或演示。只有文档不足、流程复杂时，才补充授权录屏。Recording is not the only source: reuse accurate SOPs; use interviews or demonstrations when recording is not permitted. Add authorised recordings where documentation is insufficient or work is complex.

讨论Discussion我们需要的不是“监控员工”，而是获得经过授权的业务证据；下一页说明这些证据如何安全脱敏。We need authorised business evidence, not employee surveillance; the next page explains how that evidence is safely redacted.
