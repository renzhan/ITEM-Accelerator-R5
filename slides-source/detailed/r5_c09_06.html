思考：业务规则变更要穿过完整链路Reflection: Business Rule Changes Need a Full Chain

可沉淀的是证据字段、流程与治理机制；不可直接外推的是具体规则、样本、接口和错误代价。Evidence fields, workflows, and governance can be reused; specific rules, samples, interfaces, and error costs must be revalidated.

This is proposed governance, not proof of current implementation.

可复用 / 需重验证Reusable / Revalidate

  • 可复用：证据字段、来源与有效期、权限先行、拒答/转人工、变更链、回归/回滚、生命周期成本表。
  • 需重验证：业务规则、知识来源、审批人、个人信息/员工权益、接口可用性、模型/检索效果、样本分布、错误代价和人工负荷。
  • 可沉淀：授权/审核知识、流程、Skill、结构化输出模板、工具验证契约、评测集、错误分级与运营机制。
  • Reusable: evidence fields, sources/dates, access-first, rejection/takeover, change chain, regression/rollback, lifecycle cost.
  • Revalidate: business rules, sources, approvers, data/rights, interfaces, model/retrieval, samples, error cost, human load.
  • Codify: authorized knowledge, workflows, Skills, output templates, tool contracts, evaluation, error levels, operations.
