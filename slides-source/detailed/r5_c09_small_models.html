原先的设想Original plan

按承运商做模板 OCR 或训练字段抽取模型，抽出 BOL 号、PRO 号、件数、重量、封签号。Use carrier-specific OCR templates or field extraction models for BOL and PRO numbers, piece count, weight and seal number.
退货质检Returns inspection
训练良品 / 次品分类模型，替代质检员判断退货能否二次销售。Train a pass/fail classifier to replace the inspector's decision on whether a returned item can be resold.
踩过的坑What went wrong
Carrier BOL 识别Carrier BOL recognition
头部承运商很快覆盖，长尾格式各异、每月只有几张，样本始终不够；关键的少件、破损信息又常是手写批注。Major carriers were covered quickly, but each long-tail format had only a few samples per month. Critical shortage and damage details were often handwritten.
退货质检Returns inspection
模型只给“次品”标签，说不清吊牌已剪、使用痕迹还是配件缺失；各货主标准不同，质检员还得重写说明。A fail label did not explain cut tags, signs of use or missing accessories. Owners had different standards; inspectors still had to write the explanation.
复盘后的认识Lesson learned
Carrier BOL 识别Carrier BOL recognition
承运商格式是持续增长的长尾，“先攒样本再训练”追不上变化；手写批注是自由文本，不是固定字段。Carrier formats form a growing long tail; collecting samples before each round of training cannot keep up. Handwritten notes are free text, not fixed fields.
退货质检Returns inspection
质检要看懂实物、套用该货主的规则，再解释结论；单一分类标签承载不了多套规则和判断理由。Inspection requires understanding the item, applying that owner's rules and explaining the result. A single class label cannot carry all three.
大模型承接什么Role of the large model
Carrier BOL 识别Carrier BOL recognition
读取新承运商首单，映射标准字段并与 ASN、PO 核对；单独提取手写异常批注，进入差异流程。Read a new carrier's first BOL, map standard fields and cross-check against ASN and PO records. Extract handwritten exceptions into the discrepancy workflow.
退货质检Returns inspection
读入货主退货政策，给出能否二次销售的结论、依据条款与问题描述，回传货主。Read the owner's returns policy and provide a resale decision, supporting clauses and a problem description for the owner.
