iCEO：先把业务边界说清iCEO: make the business boundary explicit first

已知：受控问答可行；当前复杂决策未达业务门槛。建议本轮先验证可追溯问答，不交付自动经营决策。Known: controlled Q&A is viable; complex decisions are below the business bar. Proposed: validate traceable Q&A first, not automated operating decisions.

改造前路径与痛点Current path and pain point

  • 知识采集 → 构建 PA（个人助理）→ 答复与决策。
  • 复杂决策同时依赖多维约束、动态背景、少量样本和隐性判断。
  • 演示流畅不能替代业务合格；本体不能补足缺失事实。
  • Knowledge capture → PA construction → answers and decisions.
  • Complex decisions depend on many constraints, dynamic context, sparse samples and tacit judgment.
  • A smooth demo is not business qualification; ontology cannot add missing facts.
