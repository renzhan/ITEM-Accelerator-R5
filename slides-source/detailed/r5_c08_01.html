iCEO：功能、成功案例与失败教训iCEO: capabilities, success cases and failure lessons

ItemBot 是员工的数字分身，将个人知识与工作能力汇集为统一的对外服务入口。ItemBot is an employee’s digital twin, bringing knowledge and work capabilities into one interface for others.

ItemBot：员工的数字分身ItemBot: an employee’s digital twin

  • 痛点：信息散在不同系统，查询要反复切换，后续推进依赖本人，交付与责任不清。Pain point: scattered information means switching tools, relying on the employee for follow-up, and unclear handoffs.
  • 查事：汇集邮件、文档与协作记录，帮助本人及协作者查找所需信息。Lookup: bring together mail, documents and collaboration records for employees and collaborators.
  • 办事：理解任务并准备回复与行动建议，邮件作为本次视频演示示例。Action: understand tasks and prepare replies and action proposals. This video uses email as an example.
  • 可控：明确对外开放范围、动作确认与人工责任，按已验证能力使用。Control: define what is shared, when actions need confirmation and who is responsible, within validated capabilities.
