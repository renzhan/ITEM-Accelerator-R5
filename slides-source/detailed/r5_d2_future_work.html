一线、专家、主管与跨域工作方式How frontline, experts, supervisors and domains work

目标蓝图必须落到日常任务、审核、运营和跨域复用；人员、权限和指标未确认时保留为待补证。The blueprint must reach daily tasks, review, operations and cross-domain reuse; unconfirmed people, access and metrics stay as evidence gaps.

FRONTLINE

一线Frontline

  • 接收任务并补上下文。
  • 使用建议/流程，确认允许动作。
  • 缺失或冲突时拒答、转人工、升级。
  • Receive tasks and add context.
  • Use advice/workflows and confirm allowed actions.
  • Refuse, hand off or escalate missing/conflicting cases.
EXPERT

专家Experts

  • 维护术语、规则、例外和SOP。
  • 审核业务变更、样本与验收口径。
  • 让错误回到知识/规则/流程。
  • Maintain terms, rules, exceptions and SOPs.
  • Review changes, samples and acceptance definitions.
  • Feed errors back to knowledge, rules and process.
SUPERVISOR

主管Supervisors

  • 看质量、周期、成本、风险、采用和结果。
  • 按证据决定继续、调整、缩小或退出。
  • 不以调用量替代业务价值。
  • Watch quality, cycle, cost, risk, adoption and outcomes.
  • Use evidence to continue, adjust, narrow or retire.
  • Do not equate call volume with value.
CROSS-DOMAIN

跨域协作Cross-domain

  • ISS Biz负责目标、规则、采用和验收。
  • COO AI/ITEM共同开发，COO AI Team按需支撑。
  • 复用前重验语义、权限和验收。
  • ISS Biz owns goals, rules, adoption and acceptance.
  • COO AI and ITEM co-develop; COO AI Team supports as needed.
  • Revalidate semantics, access and acceptance before reuse.
