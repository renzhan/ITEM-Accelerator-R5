验收与价值证据字段Acceptance and value evidence

  • 样本时间窗、任务分母、触发数、完成数、人工接管数。
  • 缺失信息、状态冲突、错误/重试、超时、异常回收。
  • 质量：分类、状态一致性、Action 合规性、人工复核。
  • 效率/成本：处理时长、人工主动时间、调用/接口/维护成本。
  • 每项带背景、周期、来源和对照口径；无实测则待补。
  • Sample window, task denominator, triggers, completions and takeovers.
  • Missing data, state conflicts, errors/retries, timeouts and recovery.
  • Quality: classification, state consistency, Action compliance and human review.
  • Efficiency/cost: processing time, active human time, call/interface/maintenance cost.
  • Attach context, period, source and comparator; mark unmeasured data to collect.
