怎么判断项目真的有效？How do we know the project works?

  • 做对了吗：信息是否准确，操作是否符合业务规则？
  • 做完了吗：多少任务自动完成，多少需要人工，有没有遗漏？
  • 人更省力了吗：同样的工作量，人工操作和跟进时间是否减少？
  • 整体更划算了吗：算上系统运行与维护费用，整体成本和业务结果是否改善？
  • 遇到问题接得住吗：异常能否及时交给人，并最终处理完成？

选定观察周期，与原有做法按相同标准比较；没有实测的数据，先标记待验证。

  • Is it correct? Is the information accurate, and do actions follow business rules?
  • Is it complete? How many tasks finish automatically, need human help or get missed?
  • Does it save effort? For the same workload, does manual work and follow-up take less time?
  • Is it worth the cost? Including system operation and maintenance, do overall costs and business outcomes improve?
  • Can people handle exceptions? Are problems handed off promptly and ultimately resolved?

Choose an observation period and compare with the previous process using the same standards. Mark unmeasured results as awaiting validation.
