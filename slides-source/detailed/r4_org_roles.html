MD §D2.4.2 · Day 2

三方与按需支持：角色—责任—审批矩阵Roles, Responsibilities and Approval

三方共建不等于责任混同：ISS Biz 定义结果，COO AI Team/Hollis 提供方法资产与按需支撑，ITEM/FDE 交付工程。Three-way build does not merge accountability: ISS Biz defines outcomes, COO AI Team/Hollis provides methods and as-needed support, and ITEM/FDE delivers engineering.

角色主要责任边界与审批
ISS Biz / 跨业务 Owner目标、流程、规则、例外、价值、优先级与验收不能把业务规则与价值判断交给 IT 或 AI
COO AI Team / Hollis方法、已有 AI/数字化成果、模型/底座与治理协同；数据、代码、软件、接口、基础设施、安全与正式认证按需支撑未确认能力、接口、资源不作已交付；会议共识不替代正式审批、预算和架构判断
ITEM / FDE建模、Agent/Workflow、页面、编排、测试、交付与产品化不绕过权限、审核、正式审批和生产要求
专家 / Training知识、样本、例外判定、高风险接管、用户训练与反馈培训不等于上线认证；人不能默认取消
运维 / 平台观测、告警、版本、容量、成本、故障、回滚与退出不替代业务 Owner 对结果负责
RoleAccountabilityBoundary / approval
ISS Biz / cross-business OwnerOutcome, process, rules, exceptions, value, priority and acceptanceBusiness judgment stays with the business
COO AI Team / HollisMethods, existing AI/digital assets, models/base and governance; data, code, software, interfaces, infrastructure, security and formal certification as neededUnconfirmed capability is not delivered; consensus does not replace formal approval, budget or architecture review
ITEM / FDEBuild, orchestration, testing, delivery and productizationNo bypass of access, review or production controls
Experts / TrainingKnowledge, samples, exceptions, takeover, training and feedbackTraining is not launch certification
Operations / platformObservation, alerts, versions, cost, incidents, rollback and retirementDoes not own business outcomes
