DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.9.2

改造后闭环：正常任务与异常接管并行Target Loop: Normal Work and Exception Takeover

建议把“回答”变成带证据、带权限、可拒答、可回写的闭环；原案例未证明这些能力已上线。The proposed loop makes answers evidenced, access-aware, rejectable, and traceable; the case does not prove these capabilities are live.

正常任务｜建议路径Normal task | Proposed path

  1. 登记业务问题、申请角色、知识版本与期望输出。
  2. 只召回有来源、有效期、审核人和用途标注的内容；权限先于生成。
  3. 优先用规则、结构化校验和工具验证系统状态。
  4. 输出带来源与状态；CSR 做小范围评测，HR 保留人的决策责任。
  5. 把采纳、拒答、澄清、转人工和错误类型回写评测与知识队列。
  1. Register the task, requester role, knowledge version, and expected output.
  2. Retrieve only sourced, dated, reviewed, purpose-tagged content; access comes first.
  3. Prefer rules, structured checks, and tools to verify system state.
  4. Return sources and status; evaluate CSR narrowly and retain human HR decisions.
  5. Write adoption, rejection, clarification, takeover, and error types to queues.

缺失信息 / 异常｜必须接管Missing data / exceptions | Take over

  • 来源缺失、过期或权限不明：停止生成，补知识或转人工。
  • 检索为空、规则冲突、工具无法验证：说明信息不足，不补造事实。
  • 涉及个人信息、员工权益或人事判断：升级 HR、法务、安全及业务 Owner。
  • 成本、质量或可替换性不占优：先缩小、暂不做或转交，不强行产品化。
  • Missing, stale, or unclear sources/access: stop, repair knowledge, or hand off.
  • Empty retrieval, rule conflict, or unverifiable state: state the gap; do not invent.
  • Personal data, employee rights, or HR judgment: escalate to HR, legal, security, and owner.
  • If cost, quality, or replaceability is weak: narrow, stop, or transfer.

已知边界：iCEO 不补造成功决策执行。

Known boundary: iCEO must not be credited with an invented successful decision execution.
