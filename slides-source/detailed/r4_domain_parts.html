parts supply chain：预测优化怎样进入实际流程 parts supply chain: bringing prediction into operations

把备件需求、库存、补货、分配、运输和异常连回服务指标；预测/优化是否可用，必须与规则、数据和执行权限一起验证。 Connect parts demand, inventory, replenishment, allocation, transport and exceptions back to service metrics; prediction/optimization must be validated with rules, data and execution access.

PROBLEM TO VERIFY

跨系统断点Cross-system breakpoint

  • 需求、库存、补货、运输和异常如何跨系统运行？
  • 服务水平、缺件、周期与成本哪个是真实约束？
  • How do demand, inventory, replenishment, transport and exceptions run across systems?
  • Which is the real constraint: service level, shortage, cycle time or cost?
MATERIALS

需带到现场Bring to the room

  • 备件需求、库存、补货、运输、分配和异常样本。
  • 规则、历史基线、预测口径、系统字段与接口清单。
  • Parts demand, inventory, replenishment, transport, allocation and exception samples.
  • Rules, historical baseline, forecast definitions, system fields and interface list.
VALIDATION BOUNDARY

预测、优化、执行分开Separate predict, optimize, execute

  • 分别测预测误差、优化建议、规则冲突与异常解释。
  • 补货、分配、运输指令和库存写回必须人工确认。
  • Test forecast error, optimization advice, rule conflicts and exception explanation separately.
  • Human confirmation for replenishment, allocation, transport orders and inventory writeback.
OUTPUT / OWNER

现场输出Room output

输出一个可取样的预测或优化问题、指标与规则边界。ISS Biz supply-chain Owner 待确认；ITEM 记录，COO AI Team 按需确认数据、软件与接口。 Output one sampleable prediction or optimization problem, metrics and rule boundary. ISS Biz supply-chain Owner to confirm; ITEM records, COO AI Team checks data, software and interfaces as needed.

讨论焦点Discussion 材料会前采集；会中比较跨域断点。先验证预测、规则冲突解释，还是人工辅助分配？数据互联与实际执行权限如何分开记录？ Collect material before the workshop and compare cross-domain breaks. Do we validate prediction, rule-conflict explanation or assisted allocation first? How do we record connectivity separately from execution authority?
