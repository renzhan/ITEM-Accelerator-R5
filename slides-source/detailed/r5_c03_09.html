Rate Sheet 转型 AI Native 商业价值Business value of taking Rate Sheet AI Native

UNIS 建立这套系统，不只是为了更快地生成 Quote，而是为了让每一个报价都有数据依据、价格标准和可评估的商业价值。UNIS is building this system not just to generate quotes faster, but so that every quote has a data basis, a price standard and business value that can be assessed.

01

提高报价效率Faster quoting

减少人工整理 Ratesheet、匹配 Billing Item 和计算报价的工作量。Less manual work to compile rate sheets, match billing items and calculate quotes.

02

统一价格标准One price standard

建立 UNIS Standard Ratesheet，规范不同 Customer、Facility 的收费项目、UOM 和定价逻辑。Establish the UNIS Standard Ratesheet to standardise charge items, UOM and pricing logic across customers and facilities.

03

提升 Revenue 和 ProfitabilityHigher revenue and profitability

识别低价、漏收费及价格优化机会，提高客户和服务项目的盈利能力。Identify underpricing, missed charges and pricing opportunities to raise customer and service profitability.

04

支持智能定价决策Smarter pricing decisions

结合历史 Invoice、Customer Level、Item Usage 和行业价格，提供有数据依据的价格建议。Combine historical invoices, customer level, item usage and industry prices into data-backed price suggestions.

05

降低报价风险Lower quoting risk

通过 Simulator 评估价格调整对历史 Revenue 和利润的影响，避免盲目调价。Use the Simulator to assess how a price change would affect historical revenue and profit, avoiding blind repricing.

06

实现 Quote-to-Billing 自动化Quote-to-billing automation

将最终批准的 Quotation 与 BNP Billing 配置关联，减少人工录入错误及报价与实际计费不一致的问题。Link the final approved quotation to the BNP billing configuration, reducing manual entry errors and mismatches between quotes and actual billing.

07

提高业务可扩展性Business scalability

支持未来更多服务类型、客户及 Facility 的标准化报价管理。Support standardised quote management for more service types, customers and facilities in future.

最终价值Overall value提高报价效率、优化收入与利润、统一定价标准，并实现从价格分析、智能报价到 BNP Billing 的完整业务闭环。Faster quoting, better revenue and profit, one pricing standard, and a complete business loop from price analysis and smart quoting to BNP billing.
