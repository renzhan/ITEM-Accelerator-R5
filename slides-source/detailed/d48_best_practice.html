ISS 最佳实践：业务轴与功能轴ISS Best Practice: Two Axes

先从 ISS 已有业务、知识与 Agent 找到真实场景，再用功能分类识别跨场景复用；本轮不预锁 BO。Start with ISS business, knowledge and Agents, then classify reusable capabilities. BO is not pre-selected.

业务分类轴Business axis

  • 业务链、部门/角色、服务对象与用户旅程
  • 流程节点、区域、价值指标与明确 Owner
  • Business chain, roles, users and journey
  • Process nodes, region, value measure and Owner

功能分类轴Capability axis

  • 问答/检索、知识采集、数据分析、页面生成
  • 规则判断、预测/优化、Workflow、后台执行与异常处理
  • Q&A/retrieval, knowledge capture, analytics and page generation
  • Rules, prediction/optimization, Workflow, execution and exception handling

候选筛选条件Candidate screen

价值、数据与知识可得性、规则稳定性、风险、复杂度、评测条件、维护责任、复用潜力。

Value, data and knowledge access, rule stability, risk, complexity, evaluation, maintenance and reuse.

先有材料，再有清单Evidence before shortlist

UKM、PEI、BO、CEC、eSVC、物流或 Field Service 仅作候选入口；名称和范围需确认。

UKM, PEI, BO, CEC, , eSVC, logistics or Field Service are entry points only; names and scope need confirmation.

讨论焦点Discussion哪条业务链既有 Owner，又能在功能层形成可验证复用？Which business chain has an Owner and a capability reuse hypothesis we can verify?
