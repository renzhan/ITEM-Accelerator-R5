DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.6.7

可重复交付＋现场动手：小范围、可验收、可停止Repeatable delivery and hands-on: small, testable and stoppable

把观察、审核、动作控制和运营交付做成方法包；证据不足时缩小、暂停或转交，不强行产品化。Package observation, review, Action control and operations; when evidence is weak, narrow, pause or hand over instead of forcing productisation.

六步可重复方法Six repeatable steps

  1. 场景资格：授权、脱敏、边界、Owner、停止条件。Qualify: consent, redaction, scope, Owner and stop condition.
  2. 被动观察：标注步骤、判断、系统、字段、例外和输出。Observe: label steps, decisions, systems, fields, exceptions and outputs.
  3. 能力分级：自动、辅助、人工，并记录依据和风险。Classify: automate, assist or human, with rationale and risk.
  4. 知识与动作：UKM/Ontology/Skill＋最小权限Connector/Tools。Knowledge and Actions: UKM/Ontology/Skill plus least-privilege Connectors/Tools.
  5. 闭环验收：正常、缺失、异常、接管、回写、最终状态。Accept: normal, missing, exception, takeover, write-back and final state.
  6. 运营交付：Owner、版本、日志、抽检、回归、回滚和停止。Operate: Owner, version, logs, sampling, regression, rollback and stop.

通用核心：Core: 授权、标注、审核、分级、日志、回归、责任矩阵。consent, annotation, review, classification, logs, regression and RACI.

场景配置：Config: CEC术语、Genesys动作/字段/权限、回复和升级规则。CEC terms, Genesys Actions/fields/access, replies and escalation rules.

现场动手：一次小范围修改Hands-on: one small change

  • 选一条已授权规则或流程，修改一个步骤或条件。Select one authorised rule or flow and change one step or condition.
  • 产出流程草稿/能力卡：输入、输出、允许/禁止动作、人工接管点。Produce a flow draft/capability card: inputs, outputs, allowed/forbidden Actions and takeover.
  • 补2–3条回归样本、验收口径、责任人和回滚条件。Add 2–3 regression samples, acceptance measure, Owner and rollback condition.
  • 把接口响应、失败、回写和最终状态列为证据字段。Treat response, failure, write-back and final state as evidence fields.

失败机制：Failure mechanism: 不满足授权、质量、安全或接口门槛时，缩小范围、停止或转人工；不能强行产品化。If consent, quality, safety or interface gates fail, narrow, stop or hand over; do not force productisation.
