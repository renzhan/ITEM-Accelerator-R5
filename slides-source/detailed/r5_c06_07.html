ISS CEC项目：我们建议怎样开始ISS CEC project: how we recommend starting

ISS CEC只是应用方向示例，不代表已经完成交付；项目重点是用客户自己的知识、数据和接口，验证本体如何驱动 Genesys。ISS CEC is an application direction, not a completed delivery; the focus is to use the customer’s own knowledge, data and interfaces to validate ontology-driven Genesys flows.

起点建议｜先选一个能证明价值的场景Starting point | Choose one scenario that can prove value

  • 工单或维修状态联动：客户来电时，若状态可信，直接进入进度说明或相关人工路径。Case or repair-status linkage: when status is trusted, route directly to progress information or the relevant person.
  • 坐席实时辅助：根据客户描述、历史记录和已审核 SOP，提示下一步检查与回复。Real-time agent assist: use the description, history and approved SOP to suggest the next check and reply.
  • 动态分流：根据客户已授权的服务状态，减少重复询问并进入合适队列。Dynamic routing: use authorised service status to reduce repeated questions and route to the right queue.

推进建议｜先验证，再逐步扩大Delivery approach | Validate first, then expand

  • 先选一个低风险、高频、结果可核验的流程；不从全量 IT 客服开始。Start with one low-risk, frequent and verifiable flow; do not start with all IT support.
  • 采集真实操作，完成授权、脱敏、SOP 提取和客户专家审核。Capture real work, then complete authorisation, redaction, SOP extraction and customer-expert review.
  • 把客户确认的 SOP 转成本体，连接 Genesys、知识库、工单或其他业务系统。Turn customer-approved SOP into ontology and connect Genesys with knowledge, case and other systems.
  • 先做坐席辅助或可信状态分流，再根据结果决定是否扩大范围。Start with agent assist or trusted-state routing, then expand only from measured results.

启动前确认｜双方需要准备什么Before starting | What both sides need to prepare

  • 客户提供并确认业务知识、数据用途、接口权限和验收结果；我们提供方法、集成和能力建设。The customer provides and confirms business knowledge, data use, access and acceptance; we provide method, integration and capability building.
  • 未经授权、脱敏、审核和回归验证，不把视频、SOP 或本体直接用于线上服务。Without authorisation, redaction, review and regression, video, SOP or ontology does not enter live service.
