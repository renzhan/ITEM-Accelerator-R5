MD §D1.4.4 · 入口融合

Copilot：保留入口，验证能力融合Copilot: Keep the Entry, Verify the Integration

Copilot 的产品、版本与实际实施范围待确认；先沿同一业务链验证能力和授权，不承诺无缝融合。Copilot product, version and implementation are unconfirmed; verify capability and authorization along one flow, without promising seamless fusion.

先确认Confirm first

  • 产品/版本、实施范围、用户入口与身份
  • 知识、接口、日志、平台回写和实际权限
  • Product/version, implementation, entry and identity
  • Knowledge, interfaces, logs, writeback and actual access

同链比较Compare on one chain

  • 知识访问、页面/代码生成、业务语义
  • Workflow、受控 Action、日志与状态回写
  • Knowledge access, page/code generation and business semantics
  • Workflow, controlled Actions, logs and state writeback

融合边界Integration boundary

优先复用现有入口、身份、知识与能力；缺口再由 ITEM/COO AI 补充，统一权限与业务状态。

Prefer the existing entry, identity, knowledge and capability; fill gaps with ITEM/COO AI while keeping access and state aligned.

决策输出Decision output入口是否保留、哪些能力以 Connector/Tools/Skill 接入、哪些要扩展；技术与授权待验证。Decide what entry remains, what connects through Connector/Tools/Skill and what needs extension; technology and authorization remain to be verified.
