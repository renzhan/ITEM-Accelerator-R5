变更与复用Change and Reuse

规则变了，再演示一遍或在对话里说一句；技能可以发布给同事，各自用自己的权限执行。When a rule changes, demonstrate again or say it in chat. Skills can be shared with colleagues, who run them with their own permissions.

规则变了怎么办When a rule changes

再演示一遍，或在对话里直接说明新规则；ItemGPT 更新原来的技能，保留没有被推翻的内容。Demonstrate again, or state the new rule in chat; ItemGPT updates the existing skill and keeps what still holds.

例：“封条照片看不清就先问我，不要发。”Example: “If the seal photo is unreadable, ask me before sending.”

需要重新确认的What to recheck

换到新系统或新场景时，账号权限、页面结构和业务规则要重新演示或确认；录得下来不代表能直接照搬。For a new system or scenario, recheck access, page structure and business rules; being recordable does not mean it transfers as-is.

复用与共享Reuse and sharing

  • 技能可发布给同一租户的同事；同事在自己的浏览器里、用自己的登录和权限执行，不共用账号。Publish a skill to colleagues in the same tenant; each runs it in their own browser with their own sign-in and permissions, with no shared accounts.
  • 技能更新后，使用它的同事都会用到新版本；发布前建议由业务 Owner 审阅 SOP。Updates reach everyone using the skill, so a business owner should review the SOP before publishing.
  • SOP 可直接用于培训和交接；同一套方法可用于其他跨系统流程。SOPs serve training and handover; the same method applies to other cross-system flows.
