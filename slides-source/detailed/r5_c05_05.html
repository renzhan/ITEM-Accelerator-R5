对标不靠猜：把 ISS 的差距现场填出来Benchmark Without Guessing: Fill ISS Gaps Live

输入没有说明 ISS 现有最好实践；本页把未知项变成会中可验证的问题和证据位置。The input does not identify an existing ISS best practice; this page turns unknowns into verifiable questions and evidence slots.

五类差距核对Five gap checks

  • 知识：视频能否关联步骤、来源和不确定性？Knowledge: can video link to steps, sources, and uncertainty?
  • 数据：脱敏、字段、访问和留存是否可证明？Data: can de-identification, fields, access, and retention be evidenced?
  • 接口：跨系统读写、状态回写和回滚是否存在？Interfaces: do cross-system read/write, write-back, and rollback exist?
  • 组织：Owner、专家审核、运营、接管责任是否明确？Organization: are Owner, expert review, operations, and takeover clear?
  • 评测：基线、样本、错误级别、回归和发布门槛是什么？Evaluation: what are baseline, samples, severity, regression, and release gates?
