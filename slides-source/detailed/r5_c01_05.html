ISS 的哪些工作，可以交给无头 Agent？Which ISS tasks can a headless Agent handle?

01 · TRIGGER

什么触发它What triggers it

  • 收到邮件、文件或新任务，自动开始处理。
  • 到达约定时间，主动查询、检查和跟进。
  • 任务完成或状态变化，触发下一步处理或建议。
  • Start automatically when an email, document or new task arrives.
  • Query, check and follow up at scheduled times.
  • Task completion or a status change triggers the next action or recommendation.
02 · BACKGROUND

它在后台负责什么What it handles in the background

  • 按明确规则读取信息、核对状态、调用系统。
  • 正常情况自动完成，不需要人逐次发指令。
  • 需要持续跟进的任务，按触发条件再次检查，直到完成或转人工。
  • Read information, check status and call systems according to clear rules.
  • Complete routine work automatically, without a new human prompt each time.
  • Recheck ongoing tasks when triggered, until completion or human handoff.
03 · HUMAN

什么时候找人When it asks for human help

  • 数据缺失、查询受阻或执行异常，进入 Need Help。
  • 需要业务判断时给出建议，由人决定。
  • 人工可拒绝或调整建议，并记录原因。
  • Route missing data, blocked queries or execution errors to Need Help.
  • Offer recommendations when business judgement is needed; people decide.
  • People may reject or adjust recommendations, with the reasons recorded.
