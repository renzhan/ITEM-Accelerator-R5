GAP MAP · 待填

五类差距与责任Five gaps and Owners

  • 知识：术语、状态、SOP、例外、授权。
  • 数据：ticket、订单、监控、派车、结算。
  • 接口：读写、权限、审计、测试环境。
  • 组织：业务 Owner、专家、FDE、接管与运维。
  • 评测：样本、分母、质量、错误、回滚门。
  • Knowledge: terms, states, SOPs, exceptions and access.
  • Data: tickets, orders, monitoring, dispatch and settlement.
  • Interfaces: read/write, access, audit and test environment.
  • Organisation: business, experts, FDE, takeover and operations.
  • Evaluation: samples, denominators, quality, errors and rollback gate.
MEETING FORM · 会中填写

用问题锁定下一步Questions for the meeting

  • 哪批 ticket 可授权、可脱敏？
  • 谁审核问题合并与优先级？
  • 哪个状态字段是业务真值？
  • 哪些 Action 只建议、必须确认或可模拟？
  • 谁接管、多久响应、如何关闭？
  • 什么记录能证明“完成”？
  • Which ticket batch can be authorised and redacted?
  • Who reviews issue merging and priority?
  • Which status field is the business truth?
  • Which Actions are suggest-only, confirmed or simulated?
  • Who takes over, within what time, and how is it closed?
  • What record proves completion?
