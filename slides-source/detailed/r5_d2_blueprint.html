DAY 2 · MD §D2.1.1 · R5

未来两到三年目标蓝图Target operating blueprint: the next 2–3 years

把实践、案例与ISS材料汇成目标工作方式；这是待共创的规划，不是现状评分或上线承诺。Combine practice, cases and ISS evidence into a target way of working; this is a co-created plan, not a maturity score or launch promise.

01 · OPERATING CHAIN

从任务到反馈From task to feedback

  • 一线接任务、补上下文、用建议或受控Action。
  • 缺失、冲突和高风险动作进入人工确认/升级。
  • 结果、状态与运行反馈回到业务和能力资产。
  • Frontline teams receive tasks, add context and use advice or governed Actions.
  • Missing, conflicting or high-risk cases go to human confirmation or escalation.
  • Outcomes, states and runtime feedback return to business and capability assets.
02 · COMMON ASSETS

可复用但可追踪Reusable and traceable

  • 知识、规则、流程、Skill、接口、评测和反馈有版本、权限与Owner。
  • 跨域复用前重新确认语义、权限与验收。
  • 没有证据的目标只登记为待验证问题。
  • Knowledge, rules, processes, Skills, interfaces, evaluation and feedback have versions, access and Owners.
  • Revalidate semantics, access and acceptance before cross-domain reuse.
  • Goals without evidence remain validation questions.
03 · PEOPLE & PRODUCT

组织运行与近期并行People, product and near-term parallel work

  • 专家维护规则，主管看质量/成本/服务结果，跨域Owner推动复用。
  • 近期同步盘点可授权IP、候选客户问题与可重复交付结构。
  • Experts maintain rules; supervisors watch quality, cost and service outcomes; cross-domain Owners drive reuse.
  • In parallel now, identify licensable IP, candidate customer problems and repeatable delivery structures.
04 · BLUEPRINT STATUS

建议目标，证据落地Target proposal, evidence-led

现场用一条真实服务/供应链业务链画图，区分已知事实、建议目标、待验证事实、正式审批依赖。Use one real service or supply-chain chain in the room; separate known facts, target proposals, validation items and formal approvals.
