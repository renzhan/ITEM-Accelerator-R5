Qualification 与 Internal LoggingQualification and Internal Logging

一次演示成功只是起点；资格认定要覆盖规则、异常、权限、Action、成本、接管和版本日志。One successful demo is only a start; qualification covers rules, exceptions, access, Actions, cost, takeover and versioned logs.

Qualification 检查Qualification checks

  • 任务覆盖、正常/异常/缺失信息、权限与安全、Action 风险。Task coverage, normal/exception/missing inputs, access and security, Action risk.
  • 响应时间、成本、人工接管、版本和日志完整性。Response time, cost, human takeover, version and log completeness.
  • 演示成功不等于已上线；要用独立样本和明确版本。Demo success is not launch; use independent samples and an explicit version.

Internal Logging 至少关联Internal Logging should link

任务、调用链、版本、输入来源、结果、异常、人工介入与成本，并遵守敏感信息和留存边界。Task, call chain, version, input source, result, exception, human intervention and cost within sensitive-data and retention boundaries.
