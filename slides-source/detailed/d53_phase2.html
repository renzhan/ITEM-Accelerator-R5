MD §D1.5.1 · 七阶段 2/7

阶段二：能力基座准备Phase 2: Prepare the Capability Base

把真实场景需要的资料、能力、权限与环境列清，区分已有、缺失和需开发项。Make the required materials, capabilities, access and environment explicit; separate existing, missing and to-build items.

输入Inputs

  • 阶段一确认的场景、流程、规则与材料
  • 数据、知识、代码、模型、算力、Connector/Tools
  • Phase 1 scenario, process, rules and materials
  • Data, knowledge, code, models, compute and Connectors/Tools

工作输出Work outputs

  • Capability Inventory 与能力目录
  • 授权/安全清单、POC 环境；标明已有/缺失/需开发
  • Capability Inventory and capability catalog
  • Access/security checklist and POC environment; mark existing, missing and to-build

验收门Acceptance gate

关键输入可访问、权限责任可追溯、测试环境与日志/评测路径明确。

Key inputs are accessible, access ownership is traceable, and test, logging and evaluation paths are clear.

本轮边界This round

不把能力目录当作已上线能力；缺口必须进入决策字段并指定责任人。

A catalog is not production readiness; gaps go into the decision record with an owner.
