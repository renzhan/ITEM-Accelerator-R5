可重复交付：小范围能力卡与停止机制Repeatable delivery: a small capability card and stop rule

失败例也可以复用方法，但不能强行产品化。现场只做一个可审计的小范围草稿，无法验收就停止或转交。A failure case can reuse method, but must not be forced into a product. Draft one auditable small-scope card; stop or hand off if it cannot be accepted.

六步交付法（建议）Six-step delivery (proposed)

  1. 选任务并分类。
  2. 写清权限、目标、禁止输出和停止条件。
  3. 准备真实脱敏样本并标注模拟样本。
  4. 配置知识、Skill、接口和状态回写。
  5. 评测引用、权限、拒答、转交和错误级别。
  6. ISS Biz (team) 决定发布、研究、停止或转交。
  1. Select and classify the task.
  2. Define access, goal, prohibited output and stop rule.
  3. Prepare real anonymized samples and label simulations.
  4. Configure knowledge, Skill, interfaces and write-back.
  5. Evaluate evidence, access, refusal, hand-off and error level.
  6. ISS Biz (team) decides release, research, stop or hand-off.

现场动手：能力卡Hands-on: capability card

  • 现场修改一条 Q&A 规则或转交流程。
  • 产出：输入、允许输出、拒答条件、人工接管、证据字段。
  • 补齐：验收分母／周期、错误级别、责任人、回滚条件。
  • Modify one Q&A rule or hand-off flow in the room.
  • Produce: inputs, allowed output, refusal, human hand-off and evidence fields.
  • Add denominator/period, error level, owner and rollback condition.

停止／转交门槛Stop / hand-off gate

若没有责任授权、真实样本或可验证目标，产出停止／转交草稿，不强行产品化；需要完整经营背景或稀缺数据的任务交给相应团队。

Without accountability, real samples or a testable goal, produce a stop/hand-off draft rather than force productization. Hand off tasks needing full operating context or scarce data.

待补：规则版本、样本、验收签字与回滚确认。

Missing: rule version, samples, acceptance sign-off and rollback confirmation.
