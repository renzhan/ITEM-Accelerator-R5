成功条件：规则明确，结果可核对Success conditions: clear rules and checkable results

助手选择供应商 A，总价 9,200 元，预算结余 800 元。The assistant chose supplier A at CNY 9,200, leaving CNY 800 within budget.

逐项比较，结论可复核A checkable comparison

  • B：8,600 元最低，但 7 个工作日到货，超过交付上限。B is cheapest at CNY 8,600, but its 7-day delivery misses the deadline.
  • A 和 C 都符合要求，A 比 C 低 600 元。A and C both qualify. A costs CNY 600 less.
  • 按既定规则，选择合格方案中总价最低的 A。Under the stated rule, A is the lowest-price compliant choice.

怎样验收这次选择Acceptance criteria for this choice

  • 通过：选择 A，预算与交期均合格，排除 B 的理由正确。Pass: choose A, satisfy budget and delivery limits, and correctly explain why B fails.
  • 范围：只完成选择，未下单、未付款、未通知供应商。Scope: selection only, with no order, payment or supplier notification.
  • 800 元是预算结余，尚非已实现的采购节省。CNY 800 is budget headroom, not realized procurement savings.
  • 资料和规则明确使本任务可核验，复杂投资需补充更多证据。Complete facts and explicit rules make this task checkable. Investment decisions need more evidence.
