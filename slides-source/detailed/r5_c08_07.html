失败教训：能回答，为什么仍做不好复杂决策？Failure lesson: answers do not establish decision quality

早期尝试采集知识、构建 PA，再生成答复与决策，复杂决策未达业务预期。The early effort captured knowledge, built a PA and generated answers and decisions. Complex decisions fell below expectations.

问答与经营决策的差距The gap between lookup and business decisions

  • 查询事实主要依赖找到正确资料。Fact lookup primarily depends on finding the right records.
  • 经营决策还依赖目标、完整成本、动态背景和替代方案。Business decisions also require goals, full costs, dynamic context and alternatives.
  • 稀缺样本、风险偏好与专家隐性判断，难以由有限知识重现。Sparse examples, risk appetite and tacit expertise are difficult to reproduce from limited knowledge.

失败带来的教训What the failure teaches

  • 知识库和本体能组织信息，无法补出缺失的经营事实。Knowledge bases and ontologies organize information but cannot supply missing facts.
  • 回答完整、表达流畅，仍需验证依据能否支持结论。A complete, fluent answer still needs evidence that supports its conclusion.
  • 从“能生成建议”到“建议可以采用”，需要真实证据与验证。Turning a generated recommendation into an adoptable one requires evidence and validation.
