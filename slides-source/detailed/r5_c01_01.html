先从业务结果与改造前流程入手Start with the outcome and current flow

不要先假设“需要一个 Agent”：先把重复问题、交接和可验收结果说清楚，再选择可控切入点。Do not assume “we need an Agent” first: clarify repeated work, handoffs and acceptance before choosing a controlled entry point.

01 · OUTCOME

痛点与期望结果Pain and intended outcome

  • 重复工作多，但优先级、责任和触发条件不清。
  • 建议结果：用经授权的 CSR ticket 找到可审核的问题分类。
  • 再形成一个有输入、输出、Action、升级和 Owner 的候选 Agent。
  • 不预先承诺节省、排名、模型能力或运营收益。
  • Repetition is high, but priority, ownership and triggers are unclear.
  • Intended outcome: build a reviewable issue taxonomy from authorised CSR tickets.
  • Then define an Agent candidate with inputs, outputs, Actions, escalation and Owner.
  • No pre-commitment to savings, rankings, model capability or operating gains.
02 · CURRENT FLOW

改造前流程（待用真实流程核对）Current flow (to verify with reality)

  • CSR 提交或状态变化
  • 人员查找订单、监控、派车、状态、结算信息
  • 多角色交接、补问并人工更新状态
  • 异常继续追踪或升级，结果留在工单与业务记录
  • CSR submission or status change
  • People look up order, monitoring, dispatch, status and settlement data
  • Handoffs, follow-up questions and manual status updates
  • Exceptions are tracked or escalated; results stay in tickets and records

建议用一张真实流程图逐项确认系统边界、交接和状态真值。Use one real process map to verify system boundaries, handoffs and the source of truth.
