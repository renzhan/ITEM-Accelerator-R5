从适合的任务切入：减少人工重复，让调度有据可依Start with suitable tasks: less manual work, better-informed dispatch

不只看发生次数，优先选择耗人力、规则清楚、容易验证的任务；对依赖经验的决策，先用数据与计算辅助判断。Prioritise suitability, not just frequency: labour-intensive tasks with clear rules and verifiable results. Support experience-led decisions with data and computation.

01 · OUTCOME

痛点与期望结果Pain and intended outcome

  • 邮件、PDF 录入和码头查询依赖人工，重复耗时。
  • 查询频次因人而异；派车主要靠直觉与经验，难以稳定比较方案。
  • 用 Agent 承接重复操作、辅助调度，目标是以更少人力更准确、稳定地完成工作。
  • 经验让计划可执行，计算让方案更优，实时变化驱动动态调整。
  • Reading emails and PDFs, entering data and checking terminals are repetitive manual work.
  • Checking frequency varies by person; intuition-led dispatch makes consistent comparison difficult.
  • Agents handle repetitive work and assist dispatch, aiming for less manual effort, greater accuracy and consistency.
  • Experience makes plans workable; computation improves choices; live changes drive adjustments.
02 · CURRENT FLOW

改造前流程Current workflow

  • 客户邮件 / PDF → CSR 提取箱号、ETA、发送日期等，录入系统。
  • 临近 ETA，人工逐个码头查询到港状态及免费期，每人每天约1–8轮。
  • 每区域每天约30–50名司机，安排150个集装箱的送／挂／还。
  • 任务顺序、到港时间、免费期、司机工时与空驶成本相互牵动，实时路况又不断打乱原计划，调度需要持续比较和调整。
  • Customer email / PDF → CSR enters container numbers, ETA, sent date and other details into the system.
  • Near ETA, staff check terminal websites for arrivals and free time, about 1–8 rounds per person daily.
  • Each region assigns roughly 30–50 drivers to delivery, drop and return work for 150 containers daily.
  • Task order, arrivals, free time, driver hours and empty-mile costs interact. Changing traffic disrupts plans, requiring continual comparison and adjustment.

重复操作消耗人力，复杂调度依赖个人经验。Repetitive work consumes labour; complex dispatch depends on individual experience.
