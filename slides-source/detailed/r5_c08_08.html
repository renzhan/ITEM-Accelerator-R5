视频设定：已有线索与拟议方案Video scenario: signals and proposal

  • 美东客户反馈配送偏慢，销售认为当地备货有利于开发客户。East Coast customers report slow delivery. Sales expects local stock to help win customers.

需要分别证明的判断Claims that need separate evidence

  • 配送慢：确认根因，以及当地备货能改善多少。Slow delivery: establish the cause and quantify the benefit of local stock.
  • 选址与需求：验证可兑现订单、服务范围及仓网影响。Location and demand: validate realizable orders, service coverage and network effects.
  • 租仓自营：与第三方仓、共享仓及小规模试点比较完整经济性。Direct operation: compare full economics with third-party warehousing, shared sites and a small pilot.
