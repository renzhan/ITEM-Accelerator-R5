MD §D2.3.1 + D2.5.2 · Day 2

四条实施泳道：近期共建、资产化与产品验证并行Four Implementation Lanes: Build, Assetize and Validate Products in Parallel

保留七阶段作为具体项目路径，同时用四条泳道管理业务、组织、平台与产品；产品化从近期准备，不拖到两年后。Keep the seven stages for project delivery while four lanes manage business, organization, platform and product; product preparation starts now, not after two years.

BUSINESS · 业务价值

0–3 月先证实Prove in 0–3 months

  • 锁定首场景、用户、基线、样本与验收
  • 正常/异常/边界流程和人工边界
  • 进入小范围验证，不假设上线
  • Lock scene, users, baseline, samples and acceptance
  • Normal, exception and boundary flow with human bounds
  • Enter narrow validation; do not assume launch

ORGANIZATION · 组织机制

Owner 与采用同步建立Build ownership and adoption

  • ISS Biz 业务 Owner；COO AI/ITEM 共建
  • 专家、FDE、Training、运维和决策人到位
  • 3–12 月形成反馈、运营与跨域复用机制
  • ISS Biz business Owner; COO AI/ITEM co-build
  • Experts, FDE, Training, operations and decision-maker
  • Build feedback, operations and reuse over 3–12 months

PLATFORM · 能力资产

可观测、可回归、可维护Observable and maintainable

  • 登记知识、规则、Skill、Workflow、接口与权限
  • 补齐评测、日志、版本、成本、回滚与退出
  • 成熟后再复制到相邻场景
  • Register knowledge, rules, Skills, Workflows, interfaces and access
  • Add evaluation, logs, versions, cost, rollback and exit
  • Replicate to adjacent scenes after maturity

PRODUCT · 产品与商业化

近期并行验证Validate in parallel

  • 定义客户问题、通用核心、配置和差异化
  • 盘点 IP/合同，准备第二场景受控复验
  • 十二月后按批准进入客户验证/试点，不等于上市
  • Define customer problem, common core, configuration and differentiation
  • Review IP/contracts and prepare a second controlled scene
  • Enter validation/pilot after December with approval; not a launch
并行关系：七阶段管理项目交付；四泳道管理两到三年转型。每条泳道都有 Owner、日期、资源、验收和下一门。Parallel tracks: seven stages manage project delivery; four lanes manage the two-to-three-year transformation. Each lane has Owner, date, resources, acceptance and next gate.
