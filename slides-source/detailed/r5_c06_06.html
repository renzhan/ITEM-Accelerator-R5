建议ISS CEC应用：动态 IVA 与坐席流程智能Proposed for ISS CEC: dynamic IVA and agent process intelligence

以下是面向ISS CEC 的拓展建议，不是我方已交付能力：以本体关联设备、服务工单与已审核 SOP，支持个性化服务和坐席决策。These are proposed extensions for ISS CEC, not capabilities already delivered: relate devices, service cases and approved SOPs through ontology to support personalised service and agent decisions.

1｜建议ISS CEC：千人千面的动态 IVA1 | Proposed for ISS CEC: personalised IVA

  • 核验客户身份后，若维修工单状态可信，可优先询问是否查询维修进度，减少重复报机型和工单号。After verifying identity, a trusted repair-case status could prompt a repair-progress enquiry first, reducing repeated model and case details.
  • 若设备遥测获得授权且接口可用，可结合报错记录建议排查路径；报错不等于已确认故障原因，仍需客户确认来意。If authorised telemetry and interfaces are available, error records could suggest a diagnostic path; an error does not prove its cause, and the caller must confirm their intent.
  • 无法确认时回到通用流程或人工，不凭空猜测客户需求。When the state cannot be confirmed, return to the general flow or a person; do not guess.

2｜建议ISS CEC：流程智能辅助坐席2 | Proposed for ISS CEC: agent process intelligence

  • 建议关联机型、系统版本、客户症状和历史处理记录，匹配ISS CEC专家审核的 SOP。Relate model, OS version, symptoms and treatment history to SOPs approved by ISS CEC experts.
  • 提示最适用的路径、下一步检查、缺失信息、回复建议和升级条件。Show the best-fit path, next check, missing information, reply suggestion and escalation condition.
  • 坐席可以采纳、拒绝或修改；系统记录依据和最终结果。Agents can accept, reject or edit; the system records rationale and final outcome.

3｜建议ISS CEC：用处理结果更新知识3 | Proposed for ISS CEC: improve knowledge from outcomes

  • 记录客户选择、坐席采纳或修改、人工接管、接口调用和最终状态。Record customer choice, agent adoption or correction, human takeover, calls and final state.
  • 发现某条 SOP 经常不能解决问题时，进入业务复核，而不是自动改规则。When an SOP often fails, send it for business review instead of changing rules automatically.
  • Genesys、知识、本体和业务系统形成可追踪的改进闭环。Genesys, knowledge, ontology and business systems form a traceable improvement loop.
