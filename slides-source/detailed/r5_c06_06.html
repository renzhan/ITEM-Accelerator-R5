DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.6.6

把一次改动变成可审计、可复用的业务能力Turn one change into auditable, reusable business capability

规则和接口变更不直接改线上行为；通过专家审核、FDE修改、回归和发布/回滚形成责任链。Do not change live behaviour directly; use expert review, FDE change, regression and release/rollback to create an accountable chain.

业务变更链Business change chain

业务提规则Business rule来源、范围Source, scope
专家审核Expert review风险、例外Risk, exceptions
FDE改FDE change版本、权限Version, access
回归Regression样本、错误级别Samples, severity
发布/回滚Release/rollback窗口、责任Window, owner

每次变更建议绑定规则版本、适用范围、审批人、回归样本、错误级别、发布窗口和回滚条件。Bind every change to rule version, scope, approver, regression samples, severity, release window and rollback condition.

可沉淀资产与复用边界Reusable assets and revalidation

  • 核心知识：授权/脱敏、CEC SOP、例外规则、人工责任、UKM/Ontology/Skill契约。Core knowledge: consent/redaction, CEC SOP, exception rules, human accountability and UKM/Ontology/Skill contracts.
  • 运营资产：回归集、错误分级、接管/修正标签、抽检、失效、权限复核、日志和回滚。Operating assets: regression set, severity, takeover/correction labels, sampling, expiry, access review, logs and rollback.
  • 第二场景可复用：Reusable: 观察表、审核链、日志字段、评测与停止机制。observation sheet, review chain, log fields, evaluation and stop mechanism.
  • 必须重验证：Revalidate: 术语、知识、接口字段/权限、敏感沟通、风险和业务结果。terms, knowledge, interface fields/access, sensitive communication, risk and business outcome.
