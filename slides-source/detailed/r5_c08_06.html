DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.8.6

思考：把业务变更做成可审计链Reflection: make business change an auditable chain

复用的是边界、审核、评测和回滚机制；每个新场景仍需重新验证目标、授权、样本与风险。Reuse boundaries, review, evaluation and rollback; every new scenario still revalidates goals, authorization, samples and risk.

业务变更链（建议）Business change chain (proposed)

业务提规则 → 专家审核 → FDE 改 → 回归 → ISS Biz (team) 验收 → 发布 → 监测 → 回滚／停止

Business proposes rule → expert review → FDE changes → regression → ISS Biz (team) accepts → release → monitor → rollback / stop

每个节点保留版本、责任人、证据和批准记录。

Keep version, owner, evidence and approval at every node.

可沉淀到核心知识库Assets for the core knowledge base

  • 审核后的概念、约束、权限和敏感信息规则。
  • 问答／拒答流程、人工接管、升级和运营机制。
  • 评测集、错误标签、回归结果、Skill／提示版本。
  • Reviewed concepts, constraints, access and sensitivity rules.
  • Q&A/refusal flow, human hand-off, escalation and operations.
  • Evaluation sets, error labels, regressions and Skill/prompt versions.

复用与重验证Reuse and revalidation

  • 复用：任务分类、证据门、人工门、日志、错误级别。
  • 重验：目标、数据权限、时效、样本分布、专家判断、接口副作用。
  • Q&A 可行不代表新场景决策可行。
  • Reuse: task types, evidence and human gates, logs and error levels.
  • Revalidate goals, data access, freshness, sample mix, judgment and side effects.
  • Viable Q&A does not make a new decision scenario viable.
讨论Discussion当前谁批准规则、谁改实现、谁验收、谁能回滚？哪些知识有授权可沉淀？Who approves rules, changes implementation, accepts release and can roll back? Which knowledge is authorized to persist?
