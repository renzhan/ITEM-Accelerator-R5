为什么选对：先筛约束，再比较价格Why it worked: constraints before price

助手选择供应商 A，总价 9,200 元，预算结余 800 元。The assistant chose supplier A at CNY 9,200, leaving CNY 800 within budget.

逐项比较，结论可复核A checkable comparison

  • B：8,600 元最低，但 7 个工作日到货，超过交付上限。B is cheapest at CNY 8,600, but its 7-day delivery misses the deadline.
  • A 和 C 都符合要求，A 比 C 低 600 元。A and C both qualify. A costs CNY 600 less.
  • 按既定规则，选择合格方案中总价最低的 A。Under the stated rule, A is the lowest-price compliant choice.

成功条件与实际状态Conditions and actual status

  • 事实资料齐全，硬性约束和选择规则明确。Facts are complete. Hard constraints and the selection rule are explicit.
  • 结论、排除理由和原始依据能够逐项复核。The choice, rejection reasons and source evidence can be checked.
  • 本次完成采购选择，未下单、未付款、未通知供应商。This completed the selection only. No order, payment or supplier notification occurred.
  • 800 元是方案的预算结余，尚非已实现的采购节省。CNY 800 is budget headroom, not realized procurement savings.
