DAY 2 · MD §D2.2.9 · R5

从差距到突破主线From gaps to breakthrough lines

优先级来自影响、证据、风险、复用、依赖和可验收性，不来自未经证据的ISS评分。Priorities come from impact, evidence, risk, reuse, dependencies and testability—not unsupported ISS scoring.

01 · CLOSE THE LOOP

知识到行动Knowledge to action

  • 统一来源、版本、专家审核、例外。
  • 连到决策、人工接管、Action和状态回写。
  • Unify source, version, expert review and exceptions.
  • Connect to decisions, takeover, Actions and state writeback.
02 · REUSE

POC到能力POCs to capability

  • 补能力卡、接口、权限、样本和独立评测。
  • 加入日志、版本、回滚、运营和退出。
  • Add capability cards, interfaces, access, samples and independent evaluation.
  • Add logs, versions, rollback, operations and retirement.
03 · OPERATE

协作成机制Collaboration as routine

  • 明确提出、改规则、评测、批准、培训。
  • 运行、故障、成本和退出责任不缺位。
  • Clarify who proposes, changes rules, evaluates, approves and trains.
  • Operations, failures, cost and retirement must have owners.
04 · IP / MARKET

近期并行验证Validate in parallel now

  • 盘点领域语义、流程、Skill、评测与交付方法。
  • 验证客户问题和重复交付结构，不等两年后开始。
  • Identify domain semantics, processes, Skills, evaluation and delivery methods.
  • Validate customer problems and repeatable delivery now, not after two years.
