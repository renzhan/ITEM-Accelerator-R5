闭环不是“自动完成”：它必须可暂停、可回写A Closed Loop Must Pause and Write Back

从视频到 SOP，再到受控执行；任何证据不足、权限不明或不可逆动作都触发人工确认。From video to SOP to controlled execution; insufficient evidence, unclear permission, or irreversible actions trigger human confirmation.

01
已知路径 + 建议输入KNOWN PATH + PROPOSED INPUT

采集与提取Capture and extract

  • 授权录制或导入可共享视频。Record with authorization or import a shareable video.
  • 提取顺序、输入、判断、切换、人工动作、例外、结果。Extract order, inputs, decisions, changes, manual actions, exceptions, and results.
  • 无法证明的条件保留为“不确定”，不猜补。Keep unproven conditions as “uncertain”; do not guess.
02
建议闭环PROPOSED LOOP

问清楚与接管Clarify and take over

  • 缺字段、画面不可读、权限不明或异常时，生成问题清单并暂停。Pause and create a question list when fields are missing, screens unreadable, permissions unclear, or exceptions appear.
  • 实际操作人员与 ISS Biz (team) Owner 审核步骤。The operator and ISS Biz (team) Owner review each step.
  • 跨系统提交、敏感数据和不可逆动作要求人确认。Human confirmation is required for cross-system submission, sensitive data, and irreversible actions.
03
验证边界 · 待补结果VALIDATION BOUNDARY · RESULTS PENDING

受控执行与回写Controlled run and write-back

  • 先在沙盒跑正常与异常路径，再按最小权限执行。Run normal and exception paths in a sandbox, then execute with least privilege.
  • 回写状态、规则版本、人工修正、失败原因和最终结果。Write back status, rule version, corrections, failure reason, and final result.
  • 当前没有成功率、自动化比例或收益数据；不得补造。No success rate, automation ratio, or benefit data is provided; do not fabricate it.
