八案例：从结果倒推可重复交付Eight cases: work backward from outcomes to repeatable delivery

各案例后续有预录视频位置与分段展开；结果数字、效率或组织变化只在证据核验后记录，不在此页造数。Each case has video slots and a structured deep dive. Numbers, efficiency and organizational change are recorded only after evidence checks.

01UNIS 港口｜无头 AgentUNIS port | headless Agent

从 CSR ticket 识别高频、耗时、依赖经验的任务，由 Agent 辅助决策与执行，以更少人力、更高准确性与稳定性完成工作。Identify frequent, time-consuming, experience-dependent tasks from CSR tickets. Agents support decisions and execution to get work done with less manual effort, greater accuracy and consistency.

02WMS｜动态表单WMS | Dynamic Forms

现场直接对懂 WMS 的 Agent 说需求，当场生成能试用的任务页和工作台；是否发布由人决定。Floor staff describe a need to an Agent that knows WMS and get a working task screen or workbench on the spot; people decide what is released.

03Rate & Quote｜数据本体Rate & Quote | data ontology

让业务与开发共享语义，再连接分析页和业务表单。Create shared language for business and development, then connect analytics and forms.

04BO｜FDE 与专家验证BO | FDE and expert validation

规则、本体、样本与专家审核分开评测，准确性不外推。Evaluate rules, ontology, samples and expert review separately; do not extrapolate accuracy.

05Record & Reply｜知识采集Record & Reply | knowledge capture

用视频降低线下流程表达门槛，形成经审核的 SOP 草案。Use video to lower the barrier for offline-process capture and draft a reviewed SOP.

06CEC / Genesys｜观察到受控执行CEC / Genesys | observe to controlled action

经授权观察坐席流程，区分可自动、辅助和必须人工的动作。Observe agent flows with authorization and separate automatic, assisted and human-only actions.

07iCEO｜功能、成功案例与失败教训iCEO | capabilities, success cases and failure lessons

查事、草稿与能力边界；对比选择与设仓判断，明确任务验收。Lookup, drafts and capability boundaries. Compare selection and warehouse assessment with task acceptance.

08其他坑｜法规、知识与模型投资Other pitfalls | law, knowledge and model investment

先补知识、权限和评测，再决定模型投资；必要时缩小、停止或转交。Stabilize knowledge, access and evaluation before model investment; narrow, stop or hand off when needed.

观看顺序Viewing order先确认业务结果和证据，再看前/后流程、实施与组织变化，最后填写ISS对标和重复交付条件。Confirm outcome and evidence first, then review before/after flow, implementation and organization, before recording ISS comparison and reuse conditions.
