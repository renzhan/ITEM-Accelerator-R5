可重复交付，但不强行产品化Repeatable delivery without forced productization

现场动手的目标是产出一份可审核的小范围草稿，而不是证明已有运营结果或把失败例隐藏起来。The hands-on goal is a reviewable narrow draft, not proof of operating results or a reason to hide failures.

01 · REPEATABLE METHOD

通用交付流程Repeatable method

  • 取样并脱敏 → 分类合并。
  • 按频率、耗时、损失、数据可得性筛选。
  • 写能力卡 → 画正常/异常闭环。
  • 连接或模拟 Tools → 建回归集。
  • 专家验收 → 受控发布/回滚 → 持续优化。
  • Sample and redact → merge taxonomy.
  • Screen by frequency, effort, loss and data availability.
  • Write capability card → map normal/exception loop.
  • Connect or simulate Tools → build regression set.
  • Expert acceptance → controlled release/rollback → improve.
02 · HANDS-ON · 建议

现场产出四件套Four hands-on outputs

  • 能力卡：触发、输入、输出、Action、升级、Owner。
  • 小范围草稿：一个问题的 Agent/Workflow。
  • 验收样本：正常、缺失、冲突和失败例。
  • 责任表：业务、专家、FDE、接口、接管、运维。
  • Capability card: trigger, inputs, outputs, Actions, escalation and Owner.
  • Narrow draft: one issue’s Agent/Workflow.
  • Acceptance samples: normal, missing, conflict and failure cases.
  • Responsibility sheet: business, expert, FDE, interface, takeover and operations.
03 · STOP / NARROW / HAND OFF

失败时的下一步When the test fails

  • 样本不足、规则不可审核、状态真值不明：缩小范围。
  • 权限或接口不可得：只读、模拟或转交治理。
  • 高风险动作无人接管：停止执行，不强行产品化。
  • 记录失败理由、责任人和下一决策门。
  • Few samples, unreviewable rules or unclear truth: narrow scope.
  • Missing access or interfaces: read-only, simulate or hand off.
  • No takeover for risky Actions: stop; do not force productization.
  • Record failure reason, Owner and next decision gate.

成功与否都要留下证据，失败不是可删除的演示噪音。Success and failure both stay in evidence; failure is not demo noise to delete.
