业务规则变更，要经过审核、回归与回滚Business rule changes need review, regression and rollback

可复用的是受控变更机制，不是把港口字段和规则未经验证地复制到第二个场景。Reuse the controlled change mechanism, not unverified port fields and rules copied into a second scene.

01 · CHANGE

业务提出规则Business proposes

  • 业务 Owner 提出规则、例外或状态变化。
  • 记录来源、授权范围、影响对象和版本。
  • 未经审核的个案不直接变成通用知识。
  • Business Owner proposes a rule, exception or state change.
  • Record source, access scope, affected objects and version.
  • Unreviewed cases do not become generic knowledge.
02 · REVIEW → BUILD

专家审核，FDE 改Expert reviews, FDE edits

  • 专家确认业务含义、例外和风险等级。
  • FDE 修改 Agent、Workflow、页面或规则。
  • 责任、权限和接口前置条件同步更新。
  • Experts confirm meaning, exceptions and risk.
  • FDE edits the Agent, Workflow, page or rule.
  • Ownership, access and interface prerequisites update together.
03 · TEST → RELEASE

回归后发布或回滚Regress, then release or roll back

  • 用正常、缺失、冲突、超时、高风险样本回归。
  • 业务 Owner 验收，受控发布并观测。
  • 不通过就回滚上一版本，记录理由。
  • Regress normal, missing, conflicting, timeout and risky cases.
  • Business Owner accepts; release under control and observe.
  • Roll back to the prior version when it fails; record why.
04 · REUSE

沉淀什么，重验什么Reuse versus revalidate

  • 可复用：能力卡、责任字段、日志、回放、评测、接管、发布机制。
  • 重验证：港口对象、状态、接口、权限、规则、完成定义和风险等级。
  • 第二场景不能由第一场景自动推断。
  • Reuse: capability card, roles, logs, replay, evaluation, takeover and release.
  • Revalidate: port objects, states, interfaces, access, rules, completion and risk.
  • Do not infer a second scene from the first.
