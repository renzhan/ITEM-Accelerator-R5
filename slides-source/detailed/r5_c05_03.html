先做小样本，再把知识变成可维护资产Start Small, Then Make Knowledge Maintainable

实施重点是可复现、可审核和可停止；输入没有证明的阻力只作为待验证假设。Implementation should be reproducible, reviewable, and stoppable. Unproven resistance remains a hypothesis to validate.

建设起点 · 建议BUILD START · PROPOSED

一个边界清楚的沙盒样本One bounded sandbox sample

  • 选择低风险、可复现流程；收集授权、视频/共享样本、角色与系统清单。Choose a low-risk reproducible flow; collect authorization, video/shareable sample, roles, and system list.
  • 先产出带时间点证据和不确定性标签的 SOP 草稿。First produce an SOP draft with timestamped evidence and uncertainty labels.
  • 让实际操作人员直接改步骤和规则，再拆出最小 Agent、Tool 或 Workflow 能力卡。Let the operator edit steps and rules, then define the smallest Agent, Tool, or Workflow capability card.
建议交付：草稿、正常样本、异常样本、审核记录。Proposed outputs: draft, normal sample, exception sample, review record.
困难与采用 · 未证实假设RISKS & ADOPTION · UNVERIFIED

把“可能困难”变成可记录的检查项Turn possible friction into checkable items

  • 视频质量、敏感信息、跨系统上下文、未演示例外、权限和 Owner 不清。Video quality, sensitive data, cross-system context, unseen exceptions, unclear permissions, and unclear ownership.
  • 使用脱敏、最小样本、双人审核、异常清单和沙盒回归逐项验证。Validate each item with de-identification, minimal samples, two-person review, exception lists, and sandbox regression.
  • 原始视频是否缓存、抽检、回放或长期留存，须另行由业务、安全与审批方确认。Caching, sampling, replay, or long-term retention of raw video requires separate business, security, and approval decisions.
待补：已有采用者、培训、使用频率、持续运营人。Evidence pending: adopters, training, usage frequency, and ongoing operator.
讨论Discussion哪一个样本能在一周内被复现、审核并安全停止？Which sample can be reproduced, reviewed, and safely stopped within one week?
