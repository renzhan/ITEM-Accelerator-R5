MD §D1.3.4 · Day 1 · 知识状态

原始记录、草稿、审核与发布不可混用Raw records, drafts, review and release must stay separate

知识状态是执行边界：来源、Owner、有效期和不确定性必须随状态流转。Knowledge state is an execution boundary: source, owner, validity and uncertainty travel with every state.

原始与提取Raw and extracted

  • 原始记录保留来源与上下文。Raw records keep provenance and context.
  • AI 提取结果标注生成方式和不确定性。AI extraction labels method and uncertainty.

草稿与审核Draft and reviewed

  • SOP 草稿不得冒充已批准规则。An SOP draft is not an approved rule.
  • 专家审核记录结论、例外和责任人。Expert review records conclusions, exceptions and owner.

发布与更新Released and updated

  • 无来源、Owner 或有效期的知识不进高风险执行。Knowledge without source, owner or validity stays out of high-risk execution.
  • 错误版本要可发现、回退和更新。Wrong versions must be discoverable, reversible and updateable.
