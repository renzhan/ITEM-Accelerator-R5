FDE 介入后的提升Improvement after FDE intervention

FDE 整理业务需求，辅助业务收集散落的资料与信息，构建 Ontology 与 Agents，完成测试验证并交付可运行能力。FDE structures business requirements, helps the business collect scattered materials and information, builds the ontology and Agents, validates them through testing, and delivers runnable capabilities.

对比维度Dimension介入前Before AI介入后After AI
数据获取Data access人工查多表、切页面、问状态。People check multiple tables, switch screens and ask for status.Agent 按 Ontology 识别对象并聚合相关数据。The Agent identifies objects through the ontology and aggregates related data.
业务规则Business rules依赖个人记忆，经验不同SOP可能不同。It depends on personal memory and inconsistent SOPs.规则从 WIKI 结构化，按统一条件执行并留下版本。Rules are structured from WIKI, applied consistently and versioned.
判断输出Judgment output手工拼 ETA、风险和下一步，容易漏字段。ETA, risks and next steps are assembled manually, so fields can be missed.Agent 输出建议、依据、风险和下一步，业务人员负责确认。The Agent returns recommendations, evidence, risks and next steps; business users confirm.
协作交接Handoffs多轮邮件和聊天，责任人和状态反复确认。Responsibility and status are reconfirmed across email and chat.结构化通知、审批和回写，Case ID、Owner、时间戳可追踪。Structured notifications, approvals and write-back make Case ID, Owner and timestamps traceable.
准确率管理Accuracy management只有个别结果，没有统一测试基线。There are isolated outcomes without a common test baseline.用批量测试和专家审核量化准确率提升，问题可以回溯到规则、数据或 Agent 步骤。Batch tests and expert review quantify accuracy gains; issues trace back to rules, data or Agent steps.
