DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.4.2

改造后闭环：回答、判断、接管、回写Future loop: answer, decide, hand over, write back

建议把“可回答”与“可执行”拆开：先给出可追溯判断，再按风险和权限决定是否放行。Separate “answerable” from “executable”: give a traceable judgment first, then gate action by risk and authority.

01 · 建议路径01 · Suggested path

正常任务Normal task

  • 读取订单、料件、库存、替代件、物流与 ETA 信息。Read order, part, inventory, substitute, logistics, and ETA data.
  • 输出延迟原因、预计完成时间、候选方案与下一步 Action。Return delay reason, ETA, options, and next Action.
  • 保留知识/规则版本与证据来源。Keep knowledge/rule version and evidence sources.
02 · 建议路径02 · Suggested path

缺失信息与异常Missing data and exceptions

  • 字段缺失、规则冲突、超范围或高风险时，返回缺口与待确认项，不猜测。For missing fields, conflicts, out-of-scope, or high risk, return gaps and questions; do not guess.
  • 进入人工队列，标记失败类型与影响范围。Route to a human queue with failure type and impact.
  • 复杂预测/优化仅按实际可接入范围调用。Use advanced prediction/optimization only where actually connected.
03 · 人确认＋状态回写03 · Human approval + write-back

放行边界Release boundary

  • ISS Biz 专家确认规则、结果与可接受动作。ISS Biz experts confirm rules, results, and acceptable actions.
  • 确认后回写结果、证据、版本、审批人与执行状态。After approval, write back result, evidence, version, approver, and execution status.
  • 失败/超时回人工；不把完整跨组织接通写成已完成。On failure/timeout, return to humans; do not claim full cross-organization integration.
