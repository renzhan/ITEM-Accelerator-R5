从任务自动化走向自主智能运营From task automation to autonomous intelligent operations

长期愿景：让 Agent 像员工一样持续成长，从数字实习生逐步成长为能够承担复杂业务工作的专家。Long-term vision: let Agents grow like employees, from digital interns into experts who can handle complex business work.

长期愿景：从数字实习生到业务专家Long-term vision: from digital intern to business expert

让 Agent 持续积累知识与技能，逐步提升业务理解、分析判断和自主决策能力。Let Agents accumulate knowledge and skills, steadily improving business understanding, judgment and autonomy.

  1. 从基础任务起步Start with foundational tasks从信息检索、数据查询、订单跟进等标准化工作开始。Begin with standardized work such as information retrieval, data queries and order follow-up.
  2. 在实践中持续成长Grow through practice在真实执行与反馈中积累业务知识（本体）和执行技能。Accumulate business knowledge (ontology) and execution skills through real work and feedback.
  3. 逐步成长为业务专家Become a business expert最终承担更多原本由人完成的复杂业务工作。Ultimately take on more complex business work previously performed by people.

基于业务本体，打开更多智能运营可能Business ontology opens more possibilities for intelligent operations

01. 统一智能运营工作台01. Unified intelligent operations workbench

从分散入口与人工流程，走向统一的智能运营工作台。Move from fragmented entry points and manual flows to one intelligent operations workbench.

  • 统一查看订单、工单及端到端业务状态View orders, tickets and end-to-end status in one place
  • 自动识别待处理、待催促及异常事项Identify pending, overdue and exceptional items
  • 打通邮件、Excel、数据库与工单流程Connect email, Excel, databases and ticket flows

02. 预测性与主动式业务运营02. Predictive and proactive operations

从被动响应需求，走向主动预测与业务优化。Move from reactive response to proactive prediction and optimization.

  • 提前识别订单延迟、备件短缺及供应风险Detect delays, shortages and supply risks early
  • 预测区域备件需求，提前准备库存与调拨Forecast regional parts demand and prepare inventory
  • 分析高频问题，发现规律并辅助运营决策Find patterns in recurring issues and support decisions
