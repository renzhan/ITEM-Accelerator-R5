共同根因Common root cause

先把业务结果定义清楚Define the business result first

每个案例都回到同一条验收链：输入是否明确、证据是否足够、谁能批准、谁来接管、结果如何回写。Every case returns to one acceptance chain: clear inputs, sufficient evidence, approval, takeover and write-back.

1任务：先选一个可验证的小范围问题Task: choose one bounded problem to validate
2证据：补齐来源、权限、样本和分母Evidence: source, access, samples and denominator
3责任：明确 Owner、人工接管与停止条件Ownership: owner, human takeover and stop conditions
4结果：回写业务状态，再决定是否扩展Result: write back the business state before scaling
讨论Discussion先选一个可验收任务：什么输入、谁负责、什么证据才算完成？Choose one acceptance-ready task: what is the input, who owns it and what evidence proves completion?
