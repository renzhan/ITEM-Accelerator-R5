MD §D1.5.1 · Day 1 · 长期优势

长期优势来自语义、验证与可持续交付Durable advantage comes from semantics, proof and sustainable delivery

优势不等于 Agent 数量、模型归属或某一个聊天入口，而是能否持续产生可靠结果、降低重复建设并加快跨场景交付。Advantage is not Agent count, model ownership or one chat entry; it is the ability to deliver reliable results, reduce duplicate build and move faster across scenarios.

共同语义Shared semantics

  • 业务对象、关系、状态、规则与 Action 可被业务和工程共同理解。
  • 同一语义支撑知识、页面、Workflow 与业务结果。
  • Business objects, relations, states, rules and Actions are understood by business and engineering.
  • One semantic base supports knowledge, pages, Workflows and outcomes.

专家知识与标准行动Expert knowledge and standard Actions

  • 知识、规则、例外和责任经专家审核，带来源、版本与有效期。
  • 连接器、Tools、Skill 和 Action 可授权、测试、观测、回写与复用。
  • Knowledge, rules, exceptions and ownership are expert-reviewed with source, version and validity.
  • Connectors, Tools, Skills and Actions are authorized, tested, observed, written back and reused.

稳定评测与可持续工程Stable evaluation and sustainable engineering

  • 独立样本、失败分类、回归门与业务基线持续验证，而不是模型自评。
  • 版本、部署、成本、维护、回滚和质量反馈形成闭环。
  • Independent samples, failure classes, regression gates and business baselines persist beyond self-rating.
  • Versioning, deployment, cost, maintenance, rollback and quality feedback form a loop.

组织的长期优势Organizational advantage

ISS Biz 定义业务与价值，COO AI Team 提供能力与治理，ITEM 负责应用工程；FDE、专家、审批、运营及 DT 按需协同，持续把证据沉淀为可复制交付。

ISS Biz owns business and value, COO AI Team provides capability and governance, and ITEM delivers application engineering; FDEs, experts, approvers, operations and DT collaborate as needed.

讨论焦点Discussion哪个已验证语义、评测闭环或组织机制，能在多个业务场景重复产生价值？Which proven semantic base, evaluation loop or organizational mechanism can create value across multiple scenarios?
