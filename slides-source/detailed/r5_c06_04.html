验收与实测证据Acceptance and measured evidence

  • 基线：时长、切换次数、缺失信息、升级、采纳/修正、错误级别、最终状态。Baseline: time, switches, missing data, escalation, adoption/correction, error severity and final state.
  • 分母/周期：按任务、渠道、队列或班次固定口径；当前为空。Denominator/period: fix by task, channel, queue or shift; currently blank.
  • 质量/成本：正确性、接管、高风险误执行、接口失败、重复工作和运维成本。Quality/cost: correctness, takeover, risky mis-execution, interface failure, rework and operations cost.
  • 验收物：授权材料、版本SOP、动作清单、回归集、日志/回写和责任矩阵。Evidence: authorised material, versioned SOP, Action list, regression set, logs/write-back and RACI.

待补实测：任务量、周期、基线数值、结果、接管率、接口成功/失败、修正量、上线/停止日期。Missing measurements: volume, period, baseline values, outcomes, takeover, interface success/failure, corrections and go/stop date.
