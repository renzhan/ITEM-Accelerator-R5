从真实操作到可使用知识：视频脱敏与安全处理From real work to usable knowledge: video redaction and safety

原始视频不能直接进入知识库。先限制采集范围，再识别和处理画面、声音与文字中的敏感信息，最后由授权人员复核。Raw video does not go directly into the knowledge base. Limit capture first, identify and handle sensitive visual, audio and text data, then perform authorised review.

1｜采集前控制1 | Controls before capture

  • 明确谁可以被录制、录制哪个任务、保存在哪里、保存多久、谁可以查看。Define who may be recorded, which task, where it is stored, how long and who may view it.
  • 使用指定窗口、指定时段和敏感页面黑名单；进入高风险页面时自动暂停或遮挡。Use selected windows, time limits and sensitive-page exclusions; pause or mask high-risk pages automatically.
  • 员工能看到录制状态，并知道用途、范围、留存和删除方式。Employees can see recording status and understand purpose, scope, retention and deletion.

2｜识别需要处理的内容2 | Identify data to handle

  • 画面：姓名、电话、地址、邮箱、账号、工单号、序列号、订单号和内部人员信息。Visuals: names, phones, addresses, emails, accounts, case IDs, serial numbers, order IDs and staff information.
  • 秘密：密码、验证码、Token、API Key、支付信息和证件信息必须重点拦截。Secrets: passwords, codes, tokens, API keys, payment and identity data require priority blocking.
  • 声音和文字同样处理：语音转录、OCR、窗口标题、复制粘贴内容都可能含有敏感信息。Audio and text also require handling: transcripts, OCR, window titles and clipboard content may contain sensitive data.

3｜按风险选择脱敏方式3 | Choose redaction by risk

  • 需要保留流程结构的字段，可替换为格式一致但非真实的值。Fields needed for process structure can be replaced with format-preserving but synthetic values.
  • 密码、验证码、Token、支付和证件信息直接遮挡或删除，不用“假值”继续保存。Passwords, codes, tokens, payment and identity data are masked or deleted, not retained as “fake values”.
  • 不需要原声时静音；需要语义时只保留已脱敏的文字或转录。Mute audio when it is not needed; when meaning is needed, retain only redacted text or transcript.

4｜自动处理后必须人工复核4 | Human review is mandatory

  • 复核画面、音频、OCR 和转录文本，确认敏感信息没有残留。Review video, audio, OCR and transcripts to confirm that sensitive data is not left behind.
  • 确认流程证据没有被误删；无法确认时整段不可用，不勉强进入知识库。Confirm that process evidence was not removed; if uncertain, reject the segment rather than forcing it into the knowledge base.
  • 原始视频不默认长期保存；脱敏结果也要带授权、版本、来源和访问记录。Raw video is not retained by default; redacted output also carries authorisation, version, source and access records.
