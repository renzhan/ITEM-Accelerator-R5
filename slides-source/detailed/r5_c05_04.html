人工确认、接管与合规Human Confirmation, Takeover and Compliance

ItemGPT 在用户自己的电脑上、以用户本人的权限运行；遇到登录或未授权的动作就交给人。ItemGPT runs on the user's own computer with the user's own permissions, and hands back to the user for sign-ins or unapproved actions.

什么时候交给人When it hands back

  • 需要登录或验证码：不代填密码，请用户在自己的浏览器里完成。Sign-in or CAPTCHA: it never enters passwords; the user completes it in their own browser.
  • 只让查找或起草：只存草稿，不擅自发送；无法唯一定位时先问人。Asked only to look up or draft: it saves a draft and never sends; it asks when a record is ambiguous.

合规Compliance

  • 录制由用户发起和结束；对外分享前脱敏。Users start and stop each recording; de-identify before sharing.
  • 原始视频和元数据默认 1 天后删除，可按业务需求设定；保留技能和 SOP。Raw video and metadata are deleted after 1 day by default, configurable per business need; skills and SOPs are kept.
  • 写入和发送按授权执行，每一步留在对话记录里。Writes and sends follow the user's authorization; every step stays in the chat record.
