正常任务Normal task

  • 输入客户、服务、运输条件、费率、报价条件、有效期。
  • 分析页面展示口径、条件影响、来源与缺失信息。
  • 表单做字段校验并生成报价建议。
  • Enter customer, service, transport conditions, rate, quote conditions and validity.
  • Analysis shows definition, condition impact, source and missing data.
  • The form validates fields and produces a quote suggestion.

缺失与异常Missing data and exceptions

  • 必需条件缺失：标缺口，停止自动推进。
  • 规则冲突、超期、计算差异：保留输入与规则版本。
  • 无法解释的异常报价：进入人工判断。
  • Required data missing: flag the gap and stop automatic progress.
  • Rule conflict, expiry or calculation difference: retain input and rule versions.
  • Unexplainable abnormal quote: route to human judgement.

确认、回写与结果Confirm, write back and result

HUMAN GATE专家确认语义与例外；授权人员确认建议；正式报价按既定审批。Experts confirm meaning and exceptions; authorised staff confirm suggestions; formal quotes follow existing approval.
STATE建议状态：草稿／待补信息／待审核／待授权／已确认／退回或停止。Suggested states: draft / missing data / review / authorisation / confirmed / returned or stopped.
WRITE-BACK使用与业务结果反向更新本体与规则；目标系统与结果定义待补。Usage and business results feed back to ontology and rules; target system and result definition are pending.
