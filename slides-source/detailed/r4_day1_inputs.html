MD §D1.5.1 · Day 1 · 输出与衔接

Day 1 输出：候选、材料、能力缺口与第二天输入Day 1 output: candidates, evidence, gaps and Day 2 inputs

第一天形成可核验的初步判断；第二天是独立完整议程，用真实业务与企业能力继续深化，不是可选延伸。Day 1 forms a verifiable first view; Day 2 is a separate full agenda to deepen with real business and enterprise capability, not an optional extension.

01 · 选定候选01 · Select a candidate

  • 按业务轴与功能轴整理一个候选场景，写清用户、流程断点、范围与排除项。
  • 不预先锁定 BO 或某个 Agent 名称。
  • Classify one candidate by business and capability axes; state users, breakpoints, scope and exclusions.
  • Do not pre-lock BO or an Agent name.

02 · 取得真实材料02 · Secure real evidence

  • 取一组可授权材料：ticket、WIKI/UKM、代码、数据、视频、SOP 或已有 Agent。
  • 标明来源、版本、专家与可共享范围。
  • Bring an authorized set of tickets, WIKI/UKM, code, data, video, SOPs or existing Agents.
  • Record provenance, version, expert and sharing scope.

03 · 补齐能力与流程03 · Map capability and flow

  • 确认规则、样本、数据/代码/知识/模型/接口/环境及权限可得性。
  • 起草小本体或 SOP/Workflow，区分自动、辅助、人工确认与模拟。
  • Confirm rules, samples, data/code/knowledge/models/interfaces/environments and access.
  • Draft a small ontology or SOP/Workflow; separate automatic, assisted, human-confirmed and simulated work.

04 · 带入第二天04 · Carry into Day 2

形成能力缺口、风险、责任、首轮交付边界、评测要求与下一门；D2.1–D2.4 分别深化实践、业务域、资产和组织衔接，D2.5 统一建议，D2.6 决策确认。

Carry capability gaps, risks, owners, first-delivery boundaries, evaluation needs and the next gate into D2.1–D2.4; D2.5 unifies the recommendation and D2.6 confirms the decision.
