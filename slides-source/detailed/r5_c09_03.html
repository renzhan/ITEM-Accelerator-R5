DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.9.3

实施过程：先缩小验证，再持续改进Implementation: Narrow Validation, Then Improve

把“模型训练”从默认起点改成有证据才进入的选项，并把失败样例保留在回归循环里。Make model training an evidence-based option, not the default starting point; keep failures in the regression loop.

01

输入先变可验收Make inputs testable

  • 从实际业务问题收集知识，不继续堆资料。
  • 补来源、有效期、审核人、用途、角色、流程和更新责任。
  • HR 任务先写合规边界、人工审批和决策责任。
  • Collect knowledge from real tasks, not more documents.
  • Add source, validity, reviewer, purpose, role, workflow, and update owner.
  • For HR, define compliance, human approval, and decision ownership first.
02

最小验证与克服困难Minimum validation

  • 先验证知识质量、检索、权限、规则和现有模型。
  • 用来源、结构化输出、工具验证和拒答/澄清/转人工降低幻觉。
  • 用全生命周期成本、质量、维护、评测和迁移成本比较方案。
  • Validate knowledge, retrieval, access, rules, and existing models first.
  • Use sources, structured output, tools, and reject/clarify/hand-off controls.
  • Compare lifecycle cost, quality, maintenance, evaluation, and migration.
03

采用与持续改进Adoption and improvement

  • 建议：从低风险、边界清楚的一个任务开始。
  • 展示输入—输出—接管记录，再按错误级别扩展。
  • 把反馈分成知识、权限、规则、检索、模型和运营问题。
  • Proposed: start with one low-risk, bounded task.
  • Show input-output-takeover evidence before expanding by error level.
  • Classify feedback as knowledge, access, rules, retrieval, model, or operations.
