DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.7.4

可控：企业身份、知识范围与动作授权Control: identity, knowledge scope and action permissions

公司账号登录，数据按授权接入，能看与能改可以分别设置。Sign in with a company account, authorize data sources and manage read and write access separately.

公司身份与授权知识Company identity and authorized knowledge

  • 通过企业 IAM 使用公司账号登录，按当前身份访问个人助手。Use a company account through enterprise IAM to access the personal assistant.
  • 邮箱、Teams、SharePoint、OneDrive 与 Jira 等数据源按授权连接。Connect authorized sources such as mail, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and Jira.
  • 同事的 ibot 只在其开放给你的知识范围内回答。A colleague’s ibot answers within the knowledge they have shared with you.

读写分开，动作先确认Separate read and write access, confirm actions

  • 查询权限与写入权限分别管理，能读取工单不等于能修改。Manage read and write access separately. Reading a ticket does not grant permission to edit it.
  • 发邮件、改工单前先确认具体内容，执行还需对应授权。Confirm the content before sending mail or changing tickets. Execution also requires authorization.
  • 区分草稿、待确认与已执行，明确返回当前状态和信息缺口。Distinguish draft, pending confirmation and executed states. Report status and missing information.
