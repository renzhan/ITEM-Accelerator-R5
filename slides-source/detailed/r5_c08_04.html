DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.7.4

可控：身份、知识与动作都有边界Control: identity, knowledge and action boundaries

助手能回答什么、能执行什么，取决于当前身份与授权。What the assistant can answer or execute depends on the current identity and access.

入口与数据Entry points and data

  • 可通过 ItemGPT、Teams 和 ItemBot 平台使用。Available through ItemGPT, Teams and the ItemBot platform.
  • 接入经授权的邮箱、网盘和协作工具。Connect authorized mailboxes, drives and collaboration tools.
  • 查询、起草和发送分别处理，明确当前执行状态。Handle lookup, drafting and sending separately, with explicit status.

Teams 项目查询示例Teams project lookup example

  • 询问同事的 PA 项目进展时，助手未披露私人沟通。When asked about a colleague’s PA project, the assistant did not disclose private conversations.
  • 资料不足时，请求提供用户参与的工单、邮件主题或项目群。With insufficient evidence, it asked for a ticket, email subject or project group involving the user.
  • 有权限、有证据时回答，信息不足时把限制说清楚。Answer within access and evidence. Explain limitations when information is missing.
