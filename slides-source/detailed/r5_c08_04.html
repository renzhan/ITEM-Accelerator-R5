可控：数字分身的开放范围与动作边界Control: sharing scope and action boundaries

统一对外访问入口，同时明确谁能查询什么、哪些动作需要本人确认。Provide one interface for others, with clear rules for what they can query and what needs the employee’s confirmation.

对外服务的范围Scope of the service offered to others

  • 通过企业身份登录和数据源设置管理接入，开放内容应由资料责任人确认。Use enterprise sign-in and source settings for connectivity. Content owners should confirm what can be shared.
  • 数字分身可作为协作者查询与任务请求的入口，私人沟通不应默认对外开放。The digital twin is an entry point for collaborators’ questions and requests. Private conversations should not be shared by default.
  • 缺少资料或无法确认访问范围时，说明限制并交由本人或资料负责人处理。When records are missing or scope is unclear, state the limit and refer to the employee or source owner.

当前能力与验证要求Current capabilities and validation requirements

  • 企业登录、数据接入不等同于完整角色权限隔离，需要用不同账号验证。Enterprise sign-in and connected data do not establish complete role-based isolation. Test with different accounts.
  • 对外知识范围、读写分离及跨用户隔离，按验证结果确定可用范围。Define usable sharing scope, read/write separation and cross-user isolation from validation results.
  • 发送、回写与审批分别核实，明确待确认、已执行和人工接手状态。Verify sending, write-back and approval separately, and distinguish pending confirmation, executed and human handoff states.
讨论Discussion数字分身应向谁开放哪些知识？哪些任务只能起草，哪些允许确认后执行？Who should access which knowledge, and which tasks allow drafting or confirmed execution?
