DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.4.6

思考：业务变更链，才是可持续泛化的边界Reflection: the change chain defines sustainable generalization

复用的是治理与交付方法，不是把一个小本体的结果直接外推到全部 BO。Reuse the delivery and governance method, not the result of one small ontology across all BO.

建议变更链Suggested change chain

  1. 业务提出规则/服务承诺变化Business proposes a rule or service-promise change
  2. ISS Biz 专家审核授权ISS Biz expert reviews and authorizes
  3. FDE 修改知识、本体、规则与样本FDE changes knowledge, ontology, rules, and samples
  4. 独立回归评测，复核风险与权限Independent regression; recheck risk and authority
  5. 发布；失败则回滚或转人工Release; roll back or hand over if it fails

可沉淀与必须重验证What to retain and revalidate

  • 可沉淀：授权术语、规则与例外、流程、Skill、评测集、错误分类、运营 SLA、审计字段、发布/回滚机制。Retain: authorized terms, rules, exceptions, processes, Skills, evaluation sets, error taxonomy, SLAs, audit fields, release/rollback.
  • 第二场景复用：本体骨架、样本分层、专家模板、评测拆分、风险分级、责任矩阵。Reuse: ontology skeleton, sample stratification, expert template, evaluation split, risk tiers, responsibility matrix.
  • 必须重验证：术语、服务承诺、地区规则、料件/库存/物流字段、接口权限、样本分布、高风险动作。Revalidate: terms, promises, regional rules, part/inventory/logistics fields, interface authority, sample mix, high-risk Actions.
记录：每次变更保留版本、样本来源、评测结果、审批人与生效范围。Record: version, sample source, evaluation result, approver, and effective scope for every change.
