DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.4.6

从自由探索到稳定可靠，再到智能平衡From free exploration to reliable stability, then intelligent balance

好的 Agent 既不能完全自由发挥，也不能被规则过度束缚；关键是在可控边界内保留探索和思考的空间。A good Agent is neither completely unconstrained nor over-bound by rules; the goal is to preserve room for exploration within controlled boundaries.

01 · 自由探索01 · Free exploration

释放分析能力与创造力Unleash analysis and creativity给予 Agent 较大的自主发挥空间，获得灵活性和有价值的 Insight；但约束宽松，输出容易发散。Give the Agent room to act, gaining flexibility and valuable insights; loose constraints can make outputs drift.

02 · 稳定可靠02 · Reliable stability

用工程约束守住结果Use engineering constraints to secure results通过设计优化和工程手段强化约束，将准确性和稳定性提升到 97%–99%；但过度约束也会牺牲灵活性。Strengthen constraints through design and engineering, raising accuracy and stability to 97%–99%; over-constraint can reduce flexibility.

03 · 智能平衡03 · Intelligent balance

可靠基础上的适度自主Measured autonomy on a reliable base以准确性和稳定性为基础，在可控边界内引入自主分析和灵活决策，保留探索与思考空间。Build on accuracy and stability, then add autonomous analysis and flexible decisions within controlled boundaries.

最终目标：可靠性与智能灵活性的平衡Goal: balance reliability with intelligent flexibility

核心认识Key insightAgent 的成熟不是从“自由”走向“束缚”，而是从自由探索走向可控的智能平衡。Agent maturity is not a move from freedom to restriction, but from free exploration to controlled intelligent balance.
