MD §D2.3.3 · Day 2

能力卡、技术 Marketplace 与业务 DashboardAsset Card, Marketplace and Dashboard

同一项能力需要两种工作面：技术 Marketplace 管“能否被正确调用”，业务 Dashboard 管“是否产生正确结果”。One capability needs two work surfaces: the technical Marketplace governs correct invocation; the business Dashboard governs useful outcomes.

TECHNICAL LAYER · MARKETPLACE

能力卡最小技术字段Minimum technical fields

  • 知识状态、版本、Owner、测评与回归结果
  • 权限、API/Tool 元数据、部署、依赖与成本
  • 日志、留存、回滚、退出条件与授权范围
  • Knowledge state, version, Owner, evaluation and regression
  • Access, API/Tool metadata, deployment, dependencies and cost
  • Logs, retention, rollback, retirement and authorization scope

BUSINESS LAYER · DASHBOARD

人员与管理者要看什么What people and managers see

  • 采用、质量、异常、人工介入与业务价值
  • 成本、风险、反馈、责任人与当前版本
  • 把事实、推测/合成样本与待确认状态分开
  • Adoption, quality, exceptions, intervention and business value
  • Cost, risk, feedback, accountable Owner and current version
  • Separate facts, inferred/synthetic samples and pending confirmation
边界：Marketplace 是技术能力层，不等于业务授权；Dashboard 是人员业务层，不替代正式审批。Boundary: Marketplace is the technical capability layer, not business authorization; the business Dashboard is not formal approval.
