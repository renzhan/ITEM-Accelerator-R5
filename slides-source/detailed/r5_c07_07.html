DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.7.7

可重复交付＋动手：现场产出一条可审计小流程Repeatable delivery + hands-on: leave with one auditable small flow

目标是产出草稿、能力卡、验收与责任，不因演示通过就强行产品化。Leave with a draft, capability card, acceptance and owners—not forced productization after a demo.

三步可重复交付Three repeatable steps

1授权低风险小流程，收集 SOP、样本、异常、角色、权限、状态、证据和接口。Authorize a low-risk slice; collect SOP, samples, exceptions, roles, permissions, states, evidence and interfaces.
2输出 Workflow 草稿、状态机、数据/动作清单和接管剧本；用自然语言改一条规则，展示差异、影响、回归与回滚点。Output the workflow draft, state machine, data/action list and handoff script; change one rule in natural language and show diff, impact, regression and rollback.
3专家审核、FDE/实现人员修改、业务验收后小范围发布；记录事件、异常、修正、完成/未完成、回滚和能力卡。Expert reviews, FDE/implementer changes, and business accepts before limited release; record events, exceptions, fixes, completion, rollback and the capability card.

现场动手、产出与停止线Hands-on, outputs and stop lines

带入与产出｜先建议/模拟Bring and produce | suggest/simulate first

带一条已授权 SOP、一个脱敏样本和一个要修改的规则；产出 Workflow 草稿、差异、回归用例、回滚点、能力卡、验收标准和责任分配。通用核心是版本化、权限/人工接管、接口确认、回归/回滚、证据台账与维护。Bring one authorized SOP, one de-identified sample and one rule; produce the draft, diff, regression cases, rollback point, capability card, acceptance and owners. Reusable core: versioning, permissions/handoff, interface confirmation, regression/rollback, evidence ledger and maintenance.

失败即缩小或转交Narrow or escalate on failure

术语、权限、接口、状态或证据不明，保持只读/模拟；异常无法安全接管或视频边界未明，停止自动动作并转交 ISS Biz/专家，不能强行产品化。If terms, permissions, interfaces, states or evidence are unclear, stay read-only/simulated; if handoff or video boundaries are unsafe, stop automation and escalate to ISS Biz/expert. Do not force productization.
