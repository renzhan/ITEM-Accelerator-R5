MD §D2.4.3 · Day 2

Training、采用、反馈、运维与退出责任Training, Adoption, Operations and Exit

能力交付之后，用户采用、知识更新、告警、成本、版本和退出必须有明确责任，不把 Training 当作上线认证。After delivery, adoption, knowledge updates, alerts, cost, versions and retirement need named accountability; Training is not launch certification.

TRAINING · ADOPTION

让人会用，也会反馈Enable use and feedback

  • Training 对应角色、流程、边界和转人工方式
  • 记录采用、误用、反馈与待补知识
  • 专家确认例外；人不能被默认取消
  • Train roles on process, boundaries and human handoff
  • Capture adoption, misuse, feedback and knowledge gaps
  • Experts confirm exceptions; humans are not removed by default

RUN · OBSERVE

让运行可观测Make runtime observable

  • 运维看日志、告警、容量、成本与异常
  • 业务看质量、采用、人工介入与结果
  • 规则、知识或模型变化触发回归
  • Operations watches logs, alerts, capacity, cost and incidents
  • Business watches quality, adoption, intervention and outcomes
  • Rule, knowledge or model changes trigger regression

EXIT · REPLACE

退出也要可负责Retire responsibly

  • 明确低价值、过时、无 Owner 或风险过高条件
  • 记录影响、替代、撤销、通知和回滚安排
  • 正式审批与生产变更仍按企业路径完成
  • Define low value, obsolescence, no Owner or high-risk conditions
  • Record impact, replacement, revocation, notice and rollback
  • Complete formal approval and production change separately
输出：培训/专家/运维/预算/认证缺口清单，以及每项缺口的 Owner 与证据要求。Output: a gap list for training, experts, operations, budget and certification, each with an Owner and evidence requirement.
