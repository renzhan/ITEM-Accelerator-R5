DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.7.3

实施：把失败条件变成可回归机制Implementation: turn failure conditions into regression mechanisms

已知困难是决策维度多、背景动态、样本少；以下建设与采用路径均为建议，不是已完成运营结果。Known difficulties are many dimensions, dynamic context and sparse samples; the build and adoption path below is proposed, not a completed operating result.

输入与建设Inputs and build

  • 先分类 Q&A、辅助分析、建议、正式决策。
  • 标注来源、权限、敏感级别、有效期和责任人。
  • 用真实脱敏样本建立可答／澄清／拒答／转交标签。
  • Classify Q&A, analysis, advice and formal decisions first.
  • Label sources, access, sensitivity, validity and owners.
  • Use real anonymized samples for answer/clarify/refuse/hand-off labels.

困难与克服（建议）Difficulties and mitigations (proposed)

  • 设置权限门、证据门、任务分类门和人工确认门。
  • 保留原始输入、知识版本、上下文和失败样本。
  • 高风险请求设停止条件，变更后做回归。
  • Use access, evidence, task-type and human-approval gates.
  • Keep original input, knowledge version, context and failure samples.
  • Set stop conditions for high risk and regress after changes.

推动采用与持续改进（建议）Adoption and continuous improvement (proposed)

  • 从一个低风险、边界清晰的问答任务开始。
  • 展示来源、限制和接管路径，教育“可答不等于可决策”。
  • 反馈按错误级别进入迭代队列；不把未证实阻力写成历史事实。
  • Start with one low-risk, clearly bounded Q&A task.
  • Show sources, limits and hand-off; teach that answerable is not decision-ready.
  • Route feedback by error level; do not invent historical resistance.
