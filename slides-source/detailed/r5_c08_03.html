DAY 1 · MD §D1.2.7.3

办事：从查到信息，到确认后执行Action: from information to confirmed execution

“帮我草拟回复，确认参加约定时间的通话。”“Draft a reply confirming I can attend the call at the agreed time.”

先理解，再出草稿Understand, then draft

  • 读取相关邮件，理解对方诉求与约定时间。Read the relevant message and identify the request and proposed time.
  • 生成回复草稿，明确标注“尚未发送”。Generate a reply draft and mark it as not sent.
  • 用户先检查内容，再决定是否发送。The user reviews the content and decides whether to send it.

确认后发送，返回状态Send on confirmation and report status

  • 用户明确说“帮我直接回复”后，才执行发送。Send only after the user explicitly asks for the direct reply.
  • 视频中通过 Outlook 回复，并返回发送结果。The video shows an Outlook reply followed by a completion message.
  • 缩短从理解到行动的距离，同时保留用户确认。Reduce the work between understanding and action while keeping user confirmation.
