办事：以邮件回复展示任务承接Action: email replies illustrate task handling

数字分身承接明确任务，交付可审核的草稿，确认与执行状态分别说明。A digital twin handles a defined task and returns a reviewable draft, with confirmation and execution tracked separately.

理解上下文，准备回复Understand the context and prepare a reply

  • “根据这封邮件起草回复，确认参加约定时间的通话。”“Draft a reply to this email confirming attendance at the agreed time.”
  • 读取相关邮件，核对对方诉求、时间与上下文，生成回复正文。Read the relevant email, check the request, time and context, then draft the reply.
  • 交付物是可审核的回复草稿，明确标注“尚未发送”。Deliver a reviewable reply draft and explicitly mark it as not sent.

任务可承接，责任需明确Delegate the task and retain clear ownership

  • 用户审核收件人、内容与承诺，决定是否发送或继续修改。The user reviews recipients, content and commitments, then decides whether to send or revise.
  • 确认与执行分别记录，仅在具备对应授权和已验证动作能力时发送。Record confirmation and execution separately. Send only with authorization and a validated action capability.
  • 邮件是本次演示示例，其他系统动作须逐项验证后再扩展。Email is the example in this demo. Validate other system actions individually before extending the scope.
