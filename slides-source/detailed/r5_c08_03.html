办事：邮件回复与 Jira 变更建议Action: email drafts and Jira change proposals

在 ItemBot 中提出诉求，先检查回复草稿和工单建议，再确认执行。Make the request in ItemBot, review the reply draft and ticket proposal, then confirm execution.

理解邮件，准备回复和工单建议Read the email and prepare drafts

  • “根据这封邮件起草回复，并给出 Jira XXX-123 的状态和下一步建议。”“Draft a reply to this email and propose status and next steps for Jira XXX-123.”
  • 结合邮件上下文生成回复正文，标注“草稿，尚未发送”。Use the email context to draft the reply and label it as not sent.
  • 列出建议修改的工单状态、下一步行动及依据，供用户核对。List the proposed ticket status, next actions and supporting evidence for review.

确认前停在草稿和建议Wait for confirmation before making changes

  • 本场景停在待确认状态：邮件未发送，Jira 尚未修改。This scenario stops at review: no email sent and no Jira changes made.
  • 用户分别确认发送内容与工单变更，执行仍受写入权限约束。The user confirms the reply and ticket changes. Write permissions still apply.
  • 执行后分别返回发送结果与工单更新状态，失败或未完成也说明。After execution, report email and ticket results separately, including failures or incomplete work.
