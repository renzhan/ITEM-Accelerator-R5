UKM：从可访问知识到可审核、可执行能力UKM: from accessible knowledge to governed action

文档量不等于质量；知识只有经过定义、版本、专家审核、样本验证和执行边界，才可进入受控能力。Document volume is not quality; knowledge enters controlled capability only after definition, versioning, expert review, sample validation and execution boundaries.

PROBLEM TO VERIFY

知识与流程断点Knowledge-to-flow break

  • 哪些定义、SOP、版本和反馈能支持真实任务？
  • 知识缺口、过期内容与冲突规则在哪里？
  • Which definitions, SOPs, versions and feedback support a real task?
  • Where are gaps, stale content and conflicting rules?
MATERIALS

需带到现场Bring to the room

  • 授权 WIKI/UKM、SOP、术语、版本、反馈与失败样本。
  • 审核人、更新人、授权范围和目标流程 Action。
  • Authorized WIKI/UKM, SOPs, terms, versions, feedback and failure samples.
  • Reviewer, updater, access scope and target-flow Action.
VALIDATION BOUNDARY

审核与执行分开Separate review from action

  • 测检索、引用、版本命中、拒答与更新反馈。
  • 高风险 Action 不由草稿或提取结果直接触发。
  • Test retrieval, citation, version hit, refusal and update feedback.
  • High-risk Actions cannot be triggered directly by drafts or extraction output.
OUTPUT / OWNER

现场输出Room output
