MD §appendix-a · Day 0 / preparation

两日准备：真实业务、能力清单与确认责任Two-day preparation: real business, capability inventory and confirmation ownership

会前只准备能支撑讨论的四组材料；正文保留完整清单，会议现场标注已知、待确认与缺口。Prepare four evidence groups that support discussion; the full checklist stays in the MD, while the meeting marks known, pending and missing items.

A｜案例授权与运行范围A | Case authorization and run scope

  • 确认可展示、可运行、可模拟或仅可讨论的范围。
  • 明确数据/IP、录屏/视频、系统和环境的授权与留存边界。
  • Mark what may be shown, run, simulated or discussion-only.
  • Confirm data/IP, recording/video, system and environment access and retention.

B｜案例证据包B | Case evidence pack

  • 工单样本、WMS 代码/BO 样本、经授权视频或录屏。
  • 附来源、脱敏、版本、专家审核人和可验证输出。
  • Ticket samples, WMS code/BO samples, and authorized videos or recordings.
  • Attach provenance, redaction, version, expert reviewer and testable output.

C｜Hollis 成果与六业务域C | Hollis results and six business domains

  • Hollis 可分享的框架、治理样例与真实证据，范围待确认。
  • CEC、e-service、BO fulfillment、procurement、parts supply chain、UKM：各域 Owner、流程/规则/材料待确认。
  • Hollis-shared frameworks, governance examples and real evidence, subject to scope confirmation.
  • CEC, e-service, BO fulfillment, procurement, parts supply chain and UKM: confirm each Owner, process, rules and materials.

D｜四字段、依赖与确认权限D | Four fields, dependencies and authority

  • 首个共建场景/验收；联合团队/Owner；能力输入/执行边界；推进方式/下一决策门。
  • 同时登记数据、代码、接口、环境、权限、安全、预算与正式审批依赖。
  • 确认谁能提供、审核、批准、停止或转交。
  • First scene/acceptance; joint team/owners; inputs/execution boundary; path/next gate.
  • Register dependencies for data, code, interfaces, environment, access, security, budget and formal approval.
  • Confirm who may provide, review, approve, stop or hand off.
