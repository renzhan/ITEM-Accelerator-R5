WMS 验证的不只是代码生成速度WMS validation is more than generation speed

真正要验证的是生成页面能否在真实约束下运行、被业务接受，并留下可维护的工程边界。The real test is whether the generated page runs under real constraints, is accepted by the business and leaves maintainable engineering boundaries.

页面级质量证据Page-level quality evidence

SEMANTICS

字段与业务模型是否正确Are fields aligned to the business model?

INTEGRATION

接口、Action、状态回写是否真实Are interfaces, Actions and state write-back real?

ACCESS

角色权限与错误处理是否覆盖Are roles, permissions and errors covered?

TEST

修改后功能/接口测试是否通过Do functional and interface tests pass after change?

维护与迁移条件Maintenance and migration gates

  • 沉淀标准模板、组件、字段、Action 与测试用例。Capture standard templates, components, fields, Actions and test cases.
  • 代码资产与业务应用分别保留版本、责任与回滚路径。Keep versions, ownership and rollback paths for code and business app separately.
  • 先确认 ISS 软件与源码范围；代码、接口或跨部门支持按需引入 COO AI Team。Confirm ISS software and source scope first; involve COO AI Team for code, interfaces or cross-functional support only as needed.
  • 演示成功不等于上线成功，正式发布仍需工程与安全门槛。A successful demo is not a production release; engineering and security gates still apply.
