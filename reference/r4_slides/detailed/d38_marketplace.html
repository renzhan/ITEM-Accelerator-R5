Connector、Tools、Skill 与技术 MarketplaceConnector, Tools, Skill and the technical Marketplace

连接、封装和复用必须有权限、质量、版本与 Owner；Marketplace 是技术能力层，不是业务商城。Connection, encapsulation and reuse need access, quality, version and ownership; the Marketplace is technical, not a business store.

ConnectorConnector

连接授权系统与上下文，明确接口和失败处理。

Connect authorized systems and context with explicit interfaces and failure handling.

ToolsTools

封装查询与 Action，定义输入、输出、权限和审计。

Encapsulate queries and Actions with input, output, access and audit defined.

SkillSkill

表达可重复能力及业务责任，支持测试和版本化。

Express repeatable capability and business ownership for testing and versioning.

MarketplaceMarketplace

登记、发现、复用和治理技术能力，不以入驻数量替代价值。

Register, discover, reuse and govern technical capability; listings do not equal value.
