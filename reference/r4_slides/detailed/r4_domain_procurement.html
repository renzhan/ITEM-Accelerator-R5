procurement：流程建议不替代正式审批procurement: advice does not replace approval

采购场景先拆清申请、供应商、审批、交期、价格/条款和例外；任何建议、摘要或预测都不能写成批准。First separate request, supplier, approval, lead time, price/terms and exceptions; advice, summaries or forecasts never become approval.

PROBLEM TO VERIFY

流转与例外Flow & exceptions

  • 哪一段依赖重复检索、比对或人工催办？
  • 价格、条款、供应商和交期异常如何升级？
  • Which step repeats retrieval, comparison or manual chasing?
  • How are price, terms, supplier and lead-time exceptions escalated?
MATERIALS

需带到现场Bring to the room

  • 申请、供应商、合同/条款、审批流、交期和例外样本。
  • 角色权限、正式审批路径与可脱敏字段。
  • Requests, suppliers, contracts/terms, approval flows, lead time and exception samples.
  • Role access, formal approval path and redactable fields.
VALIDATION BOUNDARY

建议与审批分离Separate advice from approval

  • 可先测摘要、比对、风险提示或交期预测。
  • 供应商选择、金额、条款与提交审批始终人工确认。
  • Start with summaries, comparison, risk flags or lead-time prediction.
  • Human confirmation for supplier, amount, terms and approval submission.
OUTPUT / OWNER

现场输出Room output

输出一个低风险建议任务、材料与拒答边界。ISS Biz procurement Owner 与审批 Owner 待确认；COO AI Team 按需确认权限/接口，正式审批另行办理。Output one low-risk advice task, materials and refusal boundary. ISS Biz procurement and approval Owners to confirm; COO AI Team checks access/interfaces as needed, with formal approval separate.

讨论焦点Discussion哪个建议可以在不改变审批权的前提下先验证？正式审批、敏感权限和审计日志的证据是什么？Which advice can be validated without changing approval authority? What evidence covers formal approval, sensitive access and audit logs?
