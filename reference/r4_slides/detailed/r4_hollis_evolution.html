从项目经验到可复制、可进化的能力 From project experience to evolving capability

“可复制”不是搬运一个 Agent，而是复制证据结构，再按业务规则、数据、权限和 Owner 重新验证。 “Reusable” does not mean moving an Agent; it means reusing the evidence structure and revalidating rules, data, access and ownership.

01 · CAPTURE

沉淀Capture

  • 记录流程、判断、例外、失败与价值。
  • 保留来源、版本、专家和人工边界。
  • Record process, judgment, exceptions, failures and value.
  • Keep source, version, expert and human boundary.
02 · REUSE

复制Reuse

  • 复用方法、字段、评测方式和接口契约。
  • 不复用未经确认的业务规则或权限。
  • Reuse methods, fields, evaluation and interface contracts.
  • Do not reuse unconfirmed rules or access.
03 · EVOLVE

进化Evolve

  • 用反馈、漂移、错误和新规则触发版本。
  • 回归样本、批准和回滚必须可追踪。
  • Use feedback, drift, errors and new rules to trigger versions.
  • Regression samples, approval and rollback must be traceable.
04 · PROVE

证据Prove

  • 输出可验证做法、前提、证据和不可外推项。
  • 材料不足时，输出问题与依赖而非成熟度结论。
  • Output validated practice, assumptions, evidence and non-transferable claims.
  • When material is missing, output questions and dependencies—not maturity claims.
