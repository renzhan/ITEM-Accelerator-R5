MD §D1.2.4｜持续优化与泛化MD §D1.2.4 | Continuous improvement and generalisation

从小范围 BO 到泛化与业务价值From bounded BO to generalisation and value

先证明一个规则边界，再扩展地区、服务和例外；每次扩展都要重新测试，不能把小本体结果外推到全部 BO。Prove one rule boundary first, then expand region, service and exceptions; retest every expansion instead of generalising from a small ontology to all BO.

01 · PROVE

小范围验证Bounded proof

  • WIKI、样本、规则、专家WIKI, samples, rules, experts
  • 回答/判断/Action 分开Separate answer, judgement and Action
02 · EXPAND

扩充条件Expand conditions

  • 地区、服务、例外Region, service, exceptions
  • 补样本并做回归Add samples and regress
03 · OPERATE

进入业务回路Enter the business loop

  • 预测、方案、审批、回写Prediction, options, approval, write-back
  • 保留人工接管Keep human takeover
04 · RE-TEST

泛化重测Retest generalisation

  • 重新确认质量与风险Reconfirm quality and risk
  • 继续、收缩或停止Continue, narrow or stop

可观察的业务价值Observable business value

查询响应时间、重复查询、跨团队确认、Customer Promise、关闭周期、替换建议采纳与人工介入。Response time, repeat queries, cross-team confirmation, Customer Promise, closure cycle, substitute adoption and human intervention.

不锁唯一 Hero CaseDo not lock one Hero Case

先用现场证据选择边界，再决定哪条 BO/Customer Promise 主线值得扩大。Use live evidence to choose the boundary, then decide which BO/Customer Promise path merits expansion.

讨论焦点：Discussion: 首个 BO 边界怎样证明泛化？扩展时谁批准重测，哪些高风险错误触发收缩或停止？How will the first BO boundary prove generalisation? Who approves retesting, and which high-risk errors trigger narrowing or stopping?
