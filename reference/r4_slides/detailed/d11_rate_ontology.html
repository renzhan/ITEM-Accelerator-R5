MD §D1.2.3｜数据 → 本体 → 专家校准MD §D1.2.3 | Data → ontology → expert calibration

从数据提出本体，再由专家校准Propose an ontology from data, then calibrate with experts

本体不是数据库结构或字段说明；它把对象、关系、条件、计算规则和行动组织成共同语言，候选必须经过业务确认。An ontology is more than schema or field definitions. It organises objects, relations, conditions, calculations and Actions into shared language, then requires business confirmation.

对象Objects

  • 客户、服务、运输Customer, service, transport
  • 费率、报价Rate, quote

关系Relations

  • 服务适用哪些条件Which conditions apply to a service
  • 报价关联哪条运输Which transport a quote relates to

状态与规则States and rules

  • 有效期、例外、计算Validity, exceptions, calculation
  • 缺失条件如何处理What missing conditions mean

行动与审批Actions and approval

  • 建议与正式报价分开Separate suggestion from formal quote
  • 权限与责任清楚Make access and accountability clear

数据提出候选Data proposes candidates

从授权数据看出字段共现、关联与状态线索；不把线索当作已确认语义。Authorised data reveals co-occurrence, relations and state clues; clues are not confirmed semantics.

专家确认与审批Experts confirm and approve

业务专家补充定义、例外、决策边界与正式行动权限，形成可追溯版本。Business experts add definitions, exceptions, decision boundaries and formal-action access in a traceable version.

讨论焦点：Discussion: 哪些候选可由数据观察，哪些必须问专家？谁有权确认例外与正式报价边界？Which candidates can data reveal, and which require experts? Who can approve exceptions and the boundary of formal quoting?
