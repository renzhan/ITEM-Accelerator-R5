知识整理与 CSR 自建模型的两类坑Two traps: industry knowledge and CSR self-built models

知识多不等于可用；在质量、检索、权限和评测稳定前，训练模型往往掩盖根因。More knowledge is not more usability; training before quality, retrieval, access and evaluation stabilize can hide the root cause.

行业知识整理Industry knowledge

  • 资料汇总缺少角色、流程、更新责任和实际任务。
  • 从可验收问题收集，标来源、有效期、审核人和用途。
  • Collected material lacks roles, process, update ownership and real tasks.
  • Collect from testable questions; tag source, validity, reviewer and use.

CSR 自建模型CSR self-built model

  • 先验证知识、检索、规则和现有模型。
  • 只有明确质量/成本缺口才评估自建。
  • 模型投入必须对应可测任务与全生命周期。
  • Validate knowledge, retrieval, rules and existing models first.
  • Assess self-building only after a clear quality/cost gap.
  • Model investment must map to testable tasks and lifecycle cost.
