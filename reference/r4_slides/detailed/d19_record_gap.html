Record & Reply：做得出却说不清的流程Record & Reply: processes people can do but cannot describe

当业务能演示却难以写出完整 SOP，视频成为降低知识采集门槛的入口；offline 指线下或尚未完整记录，不等于技术全程离线。When people can demonstrate a process but cannot write the full SOP, video lowers the capture barrier. Offline means unrecorded or physical work, not necessarily fully offline technology.

视频入口的边界Boundaries for the video entry

  • 只在授权范围录制或导入可共享示例。Record or import shareable examples only within authorisation.
  • 保留角色和必要背景，标出敏感信息。Keep role and necessary context; mark sensitive information.
  • 采集与处理、原视频留存、执行权限分别决定。Decide capture/processing, raw-video retention and execution access separately.
  • 后续由实际操作人员和业务 Owner 审核。Have the operator and business Owner review the result.

本页不把视频采集写成偷监控，也不把线下流程等同于完全离线技术。Video capture is not covert monitoring, and offline work is not automatically offline technology.
