WIKI 到小本体：先定义有限边界From WIKI to a small ontology

先统一本轮真正需要的概念和规则，不追求一次覆盖全部组织；版本与业务目标必须写在边界里。Align only the concepts and rules needed for this round; do not attempt to cover the whole organisation at once. Version and business goal belong in the boundary.

从 WIKI 落到可用概念Make WIKI operational

BACK ORDER待处理订单状态Back Order state
INVENTORY库存与可用性Inventory and availability
PART / SUBSTITUTE料件与替代关系Part and substitute relation
SHIPMENT / ETA运输与预计到达Shipment and ETA
SERVICE PROMISE服务承诺Service Promise
PRIORITY / ACTION优先级与允许动作Priority and allowed Action

有限边界的四个标签Four boundary labels

  • 版本：使用哪一版 WIKI、规则和数据。Version: which WIKI, rule and data version is used.
  • 业务目标：回答、判断、预测、建议还是 Action。Business goal: answer, classify, predict, recommend or act.
  • 范围：先选一个地区、服务或规则簇。Scope: start with one region, service or rule cluster.
  • 排除项：明确暂不覆盖的组织、例外和高风险动作。Exclusions: make uncovered organisation, exceptions and risky actions explicit.

小本体是可测的起点，不是最终企业知识地图。A small ontology is a measurable starting point, not the final enterprise knowledge map.
