MD §D2.2.3 · e-service · 25 min suggested

e-service：定位重复判断、现场责任与服务结果 e-service: repeated judgment, ownership and outcomes

从服务请求到维修/诊断、派工或反馈，找出重复判断与状态断点；不假定模型、数字化成果或流程可直接接入。 From service request through repair/diagnosis, dispatch or feedback, locate repeated judgment and status breaks; do not assume models or digital results are directly integrable.

PROBLEM TO VERIFY

哪一步反复判断 Where judgment repeats

  • 请求分流、诊断、派工、反馈哪个节点耗费最多？
  • 重复判断是否来自设备、工单、知识或状态不一致？
  • Which node consumes effort: intake, diagnosis, dispatch or feedback?
  • Does repetition come from inconsistent device, ticket, knowledge or status data?
MATERIALS

需带到现场 Bring to the room

  • 设备/工单字段、维修知识、诊断路径、状态流转。
  • 脱敏历史样本、人工处理记录和例外规则。
  • Device/ticket fields, repair knowledge, diagnostic paths and status transitions.
  • Redacted history, human handling records and exception rules.
VALIDATION BOUNDARY

验证与人工边界 Validation & human boundary

  • 分别测分类、诊断建议、派工建议与状态反馈。
  • 维修安全、客户承诺和实际派工必须人工确认。
  • Test classification, diagnostic advice, dispatch advice and status feedback separately.
  • Human confirmation is required for safety, promises and actual dispatch.
OUTPUT / OWNER

现场输出 Room output

输出一个可取样的重复判断、输入字段与验收指标。ISS Biz 服务/维修 Owner 待确认；ITEM 画边界，COO AI Team 按需确认系统与接口。 Output one sampleable repeated judgment, input fields and acceptance metrics. ISS Biz service/repair Owner to confirm; ITEM draws the boundary, COO AI Team confirms systems and interfaces as needed.

讨论焦点Discussion 哪个节点可以先做建议而不直接执行？必须保留哪些状态、证据和人工接管？ Which node can start with advice without execution? Which states, evidence and human takeover must remain?
