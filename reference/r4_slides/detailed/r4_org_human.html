MD §D2.4.3–D2.4.4 · Day 2

自动、辅助、人工确认：跨业务接管与授权Automation, Assistance and Human Takeover

先按风险和动作划分接管模式，再定义跨业务 Owner、授权、升级与停止；自动化不等于自动获批。Classify takeover modes by risk and action, then define cross-business ownership, access, escalation and stop rules; automation is not approval.

AUTOMATIC · 自动

低风险、可逆动作Low-risk and reversible

  • 输入、规则、权限与停止条件清楚
  • 留日志与结果，异常自动告警
  • Inputs, rules, access and stop conditions are clear
  • Log outcomes and alert on exceptions

ASSISTED · 辅助

建议由人决定Human decides

  • Agent 生成建议、分类或候选动作
  • 业务人员确认后才进入下一步
  • Agent proposes, classifies or drafts an action
  • Business staff confirm before the next step

HUMAN CONFIRM · 人工确认

高风险保留授权Keep high-risk approval

  • 涉及外部承诺、敏感数据或不可逆动作
  • 记录确认人、依据、时间与正式审批路径
  • External commitments, sensitive data or irreversible actions
  • Record confirmer, rationale, time and formal approval path

REFUSE · ESCALATE · STOP

不会做也要可解释Refuse, escalate, stop

  • 缺证、越权、异常或超出边界时拒答
  • 转交指定 Owner，触发停止或回滚
  • Refuse when evidence, access or boundary is insufficient
  • Escalate to the named Owner; stop or roll back
授权原则：业务规则与价值判断不交给 IT；跨业务接管要有明确 Owner，且不绕过安全、EA、BIA 或生产审批。Authorization: business judgment stays with the business; cross-business takeover needs a named Owner and cannot bypass Security, EA, BIA or production approval.
