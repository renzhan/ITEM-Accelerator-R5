ISS BO：FDE 如何落地多样业务规则ISS BO: how FDE grounds varied rules

FDE 贴近业务现场，把知识、例外、样本、系统能力与可运行应用连接起来；ISS Biz 专家仍对业务规则与验收负责。The FDE connects knowledge, exceptions, samples, system capability and runnable apps close to the business; ISS Biz experts still own rules and acceptance.

规则为什么多样Why rules vary

  • 业务线、地区和服务承诺不同Business line, region and promise differ
  • 料件、库存、运输状态不断变化Part, inventory and transport states change
  • 单一 Prompt 难覆盖合理例外One prompt cannot cover valid exceptions

FDE 的工程连接FDE engineering bridge

  • 把 WIKI、事实、样本和规则整理成可运行输入Turn WIKI, facts, samples and rules into runnable inputs
  • 建立测试、调试、评测和回写路径Establish testing, debugging, evaluation and write-back
  • 把异常与缺失保留给专家Keep exceptions and gaps with experts

专家验收边界Expert acceptance boundary

  • 确认概念、规则、结果和可接受 ActionConfirm concepts, rules, results and acceptable Actions
  • 区分建议、预测与正式业务动作Separate suggestion, prediction and formal action
  • 高风险错误不能被平均分掩盖Average scores cannot hide high-risk errors

共同交付Joint delivery

FDE + 业务专家 + 事实/数据 Owner，共同定义边界。FDE + business expert + fact/data Owner define the boundary together.

不是单纯模型调用Not a model call alone

知识、规则、样本、权限、Action 与运营必须连起来。Knowledge, rules, samples, access, Actions and operations must connect.

讨论焦点：Discussion: 哪个 BO 规则边界值得先做小范围？FDE、业务专家、事实 Owner 的共同交付节奏如何安排？Which BO rule boundary merits a small start? How should FDE, business experts and fact Owners deliver together?
