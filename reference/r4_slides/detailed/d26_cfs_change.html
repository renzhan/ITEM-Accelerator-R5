自然语言调整流程，也要管理运行版本Natural-language changes still need runtime version control

“能改”必须同时意味着看得见影响、可回归、可回滚，并能照顾未完成任务。“Editable” must also mean visible impact, regression-tested, rollback-ready and safe for in-flight tasks.

变更门Change gates

  • 显示自然语言变更的差异与影响范围。
  • 业务审核后做回归测试，再发布新版本。
  • 保留旧版本、发布人和生效时间。
  • Show the diff and impact of the natural-language change.
  • Business review and regression testing precede release.
  • Keep the old version, publisher and effective time.

运行中保护Runtime protection

  • 未完成任务如何兼容，权限异常如何接管。
  • 失败重试、冲突处理和人工修正可追踪。
  • 必要时回滚，不让旧任务静默改变。
  • Handle in-flight tasks and permission exceptions explicitly.
  • Trace retries, conflicts and human corrections.
  • Roll back when needed; never silently alter old tasks.
