阶段四：Training 与普及Phase 4: Training & Adoption

Training 不只是用户演示：业务要会运行与反馈，专业人员要会建模、测试、发布与维护。Training goes beyond demos: users learn to run and give feedback; specialists learn to model, test, release and maintain.

输入Inputs

  • 已验证的流程、Agent/Workflow 与失败边界
  • 用户角色、FDE/Senior User/Developer 和 DT 支撑需求
  • Validated flow, Agent/Workflow and failure boundaries
  • User roles, FDE/Senior User/Developer and DT needs

工作输出Work outputs

  • 用户培训、使用材料、反馈路径
  • 运营责任、采用计划，以及何时确认或接管
  • User training, usage material and feedback path
  • Operations ownership, adoption plan and takeover rules

验收门Acceptance gate

用户知道何时使用、确认、拒答或接管；运营责任已有人签认。

Users know when to use, confirm, reject or take over; operations ownership is signed off.

本轮边界This round

不承诺全员培训或全面采用；采用计划取决于验证结果和责任确认。

No promise of universal training or adoption; the plan depends on validation and ownership.
