MD §D1.2.5｜视频 → SOP → Agent → 执行MD §D1.2.5 | Video → SOP → Agent → execution

视频到 SOP，再到 Agent 与执行From video to SOP, Agent and execution

先提取事实和不确定点，再由业务审核决定哪些步骤能进入 Agent、Tool 或 Workflow；执行前必须经过沙盒验证。Extract facts and uncertainties first. Business review decides what enters an Agent, Tool or Workflow; sandbox validation comes before execution.

01 · EXTRACT

视频观察Observe video

提取顺序、输入、判断、系统切换、人工动作和结果。Extract sequence, inputs, judgement, system switches, human actions and result.

02 · DRAFT

SOP 草稿SOP draft

标出不确定步骤、缺失信息和可能自动化点。Mark uncertain steps, missing information and automation candidates.

03 · REVIEW

业务审核Business review

实际操作者与 Owner 确认规则、例外与责任。Operator and Owner confirm rules, exceptions and accountability.

04 · VERIFY

沙盒执行Sandbox run

验证正常/异常路径，再按权限进入受控执行。Verify normal and exception paths, then execute under access control.

演示要看到The demo should show

  • 哪些步骤被系统明确识别为不确定Which steps are marked uncertain
  • 业务修改如何回写 SOP 与知识How business correction writes back to SOP and knowledge

持续优化Continuous improvement

  • 失败、人工修正与结果返回知识Failures, human corrections and results return to knowledge
  • 不把一次视频直接当成上线流程Do not treat one video as a production workflow
讨论焦点：Discussion: 哪一段流程适合首个视频演示？谁有权把某一步从“建议”升级为可执行 Action？Which process segment is suitable for the first video demo? Who can upgrade a step from “suggestion” to executable Action?
