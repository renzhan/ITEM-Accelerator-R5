MD §D2.2.4–D2.2.6 · 六域收敛

跨域复用：已知事实、待验证事实与依赖 Cross-domain reuse: facts, unknowns and dependencies

收敛不是把六页拼成一个产品；只抽取经多域材料支持的共用输入、输出、Owner、权限与评测，其余保留为域内特有。 Convergence is not merging six pages into one product; extract only cross-domain inputs, outputs, ownership, access and evaluation supported by evidence.

分层记录Layered record

  • 已知事实：来源、版本、责任人和材料可追溯。
  • 待验证事实：需样本、专家、授权或接口确认。
  • 缺口与依赖：数据/代码/软件/接口、正式审批、资源或决策。
  • 场景比较：首场景、备选与排除项只凭证据排序。
  • Known: source, version, Owner and material are traceable.
  • To validate: needs samples, experts, authorization or interfaces.
  • Gaps/dependencies: data/code/software/interfaces, formal approval, resources or decisions.
  • Compare scenes: rank first, backup and exclusions by evidence only.
现场输出Room output 六域事实表、跨域能力复用候选表、首场景/备选比较依据；未确认项明确写“待确认”。 Six-domain fact log, cross-domain capability candidates and first/backup comparison basis; label every unknown “to confirm.”
