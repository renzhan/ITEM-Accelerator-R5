多维决策与稀缺样本：本体不能补足事实Multi-dimensional decisions and sparse samples: ontology cannot add facts

本体能统一概念和约束，但不能凭空补足事实、样本、目标和专家判断。An ontology unifies concepts and constraints; it cannot invent facts, samples, goals or expert judgment.

缺口一：维度Gap 1: dimensions

  • 决策同时受目标、背景、风险和责任影响。
  • 静态知识难覆盖动态经营背景。
  • Decisions combine goals, context, risk and accountability.
  • Static knowledge cannot cover dynamic operating context.

缺口二：样本Gap 2: samples

  • 历史样本少，难以独立检验。
  • 合成样本须明确标注，不能冒充事实。
  • Few historical samples make independent testing difficult.
  • Synthetic samples must be labeled, never treated as facts.

正确用法Right use

  • 用本体约束概念与条件。
  • 把缺失事实和专家判断显式留给人。
  • Use ontology to constrain concepts and conditions.
  • Make missing facts and expert judgment explicit for humans.
