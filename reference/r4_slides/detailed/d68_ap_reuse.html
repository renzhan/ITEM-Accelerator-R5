AP 复用：分类接入，不一次性重写AP Reuse: Classify, Integrate, Don’t Rewrite All

按成熟度和场景决定处理方式；Hollis 阶段治理支持先评估、再标准接入。Choose treatment by maturity and scenario; Hollis stage governance supports assessment before standard integration.

类别处理方式
能力清楚、质量/权限满足尽量直接复用，登记接口与 Owner。
价值明确、接口/元数据不统一适配后接入，保留原运行环境。
规则或范围不匹配明确改造，重新评测，不跨场景外推。
CategoryTreatment
Clear capability, quality/access fitPrefer direct reuse; register interface and Owner.
Value clear, interface/metadata inconsistentAdapt and connect; retain runtime.
Rules or scope mismatchDefine change and reevaluate; no cross-scenario extrapolation.
类别处理方式
多个 Agent 功能重复比较实际质量、采用和成本，再合并或保留互补。
缺 Owner/权限/质量不明/风险高先登记待评估，不直接进入生产编排。
CategoryTreatment
Overlapping AgentsCompare actual quality, adoption and cost; merge or retain complementary items.
Missing Owner/access, unclear quality or high riskRegister for assessment; do not put directly into production orchestration.

验收与边界Gate & boundary

输出试复用清单、缺口、Owner、评测条件和本轮不接入项；是否统一托管由成熟度和场景决定。

Output reuse candidates, gaps, Owners, evaluation conditions and exclusions; unified hosting depends on maturity and scenario.

不要做的事Do not

不因数量多就全部迁移，不因接口相似就跳过业务规则、权限、质量和维护确认。

Do not migrate everything because the count is large or skip rules, access, quality and maintenance checks because interfaces look similar.
