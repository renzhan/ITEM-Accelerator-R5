港口案例如何验证和迁移How the port case is validated and migrated

证明任务不是 Agent 数量，而是一次任务是否可追踪、可回放、可纠错，并能在 ISS 找到真实样本与清晰责任。The proof is not the number of Agents, but whether a task is traceable, replayable and correctable with real ISS samples and clear ownership.

演示与运行证据Demo and runtime evidence

01 · COVERAGE高频问题覆盖率Coverage of recurring issues
02 · HUMAN一次任务的人工介入Human interventions per task
03 · TIME处理时长与等待点Handling time and wait points
04 · RECOVERY错误、重试与异常回收Errors, retries and exception recovery

任何历史效率或人员变化都必须带背景、时间和统计口径；不直接承诺 ISS 会获得相同结果。Any historical efficiency or staffing change needs context, period and measurement definition; it is not an ISS promise.

迁移边界与 OwnerMigration boundary and Owners

  • 样本：ISS 可优先提供 CSR、物流、BO 或服务链中的重复问题。Samples: ISS may start with recurring CSR, logistics, BO or service-chain issues.
  • 审核：业务 Owner 审核分类、优先级、异常和允许动作。Review: the business Owner approves classification, priority, exceptions and allowed actions.
  • 运行：正常路径可后台，缺失、冲突和高风险动作需人工确认。Run: normal paths may run in background; missing, conflicting or risky actions need confirmation.
  • 优化：新工单和运行错误返回分类、规则与知识。Improve: new tickets and runtime errors update classification, rules and knowledge.
讨论焦点：Discussion: ISS 的最小演示样本是什么？谁签收分类和指标？什么结果足以继续，什么异常必须暂停迁移？What is the smallest ISS demo sample? Who signs off classification and metrics? What supports continuation, and what pauses migration?
