MD §D1.2.9 · Day 1

法规、幻觉与模型投资不能后补Regulation, hallucination and model investment cannot be afterthoughts

技术可行不等于可上线；安全、法规、来源校验和可替换性必须前置。Technical feasibility is not launchability; safety, regulation, source checks and replaceability come first.

HR 与法规HR and regulation

  • HR、法务、安全和业务 Owner 共同确认规则。
  • 个人信息、员工权益和决策责任不能绕过审批。
  • HR, legal, security and the business owner confirm rules together.
  • Do not bypass review for personal data, employee rights or decision accountability.

幻觉控制Hallucination control

  • 强制来源、检索、结构化校验和工具验证。
  • 信息不足时拒答、澄清或转人工。
  • Require sources, retrieval, structured checks and tool verification.
  • Refuse, clarify or hand off when information is insufficient.

短效投资Short-lived investment

  • 评估全生命周期质量、维护和迁移成本。
  • 不把模型所有权本身当业务成果。
  • Assess lifecycle quality, maintenance and migration cost.
  • Do not treat model ownership itself as business value.
