MD §D1.2.8 · Day 1

iCEO：问答可行，不代表决策合格iCEO: workable Q&A does not mean decision-ready

把问答、辅助建议和正式决策分开评价；演示流畅不能替代业务合格。Evaluate Q&A, assisted recommendations and formal decisions separately; a smooth demo is not business qualification.

可行：受控问答Viable: controlled Q&A

  • 在有权限、可检索的知识范围内回答。
  • 明确来源、可见范围和敏感信息。
  • 证据不足时拒答、澄清或转人工。
  • Answer within authorized, retrievable knowledge.
  • Make sources, visibility and sensitive information explicit.
  • Refuse, clarify or hand off when evidence is insufficient.

失败：复杂决策Failed: complex decisions

  • 多维约束、动态背景与少量样本使决策不准。
  • 当前不交付自动经营决策。
  • 保留失败条件与样本，不外推到所有 Agent。
  • Many constraints, dynamic context and sparse samples made decisions unreliable.
  • Do not deliver automated operating decisions now.
  • Preserve failure conditions and samples; do not generalize to every Agent.
