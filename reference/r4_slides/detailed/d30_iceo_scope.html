MD §D1.2.8 · Day 1

明确该做、缩小、停止或转交Decide what to do, narrow, stop or hand off

把失败变成范围管理：按责任、数据与授权决定下一步，而不是泛化为“AI 不行”。Turn failure into scope management: use accountability, data and authorization to choose the next step.

该做Do now

权限内问答、证据引用、辅助整理和可回退建议。

Authorized Q&A, cited evidence, assisted organization and reversible recommendations.

先缩小Narrow first

限定一个角色、任务、数据集和人工确认点。

Limit one role, task, dataset and human approval point.

停止Stop

没有责任授权、真实样本或可验证目标时，不进入建设。

Stop when accountability, real samples or a testable objective are missing.

转交Hand off

需要完整经营背景或法规判断的任务交给相应团队。

Hand off tasks requiring full operating context or regulatory judgment.
