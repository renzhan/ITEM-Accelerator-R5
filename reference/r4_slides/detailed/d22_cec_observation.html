CEC：业务和坐席共同发现自动化机会 CEC: business and agents find opportunities together

不只依赖 IT 判断价值：观察真实坐席如何跨知识库、订单、服务与沟通系统操作，再由业务与坐席共同分类。 Do not rely on IT alone: observe how agents work across knowledge, order, service and communication systems, then classify tasks with business and agents.

观察真实工作Observe real work

  • 高频查询与重复切换Frequent queries and repeated switching
  • 标准回复与信息补充Standard replies and information capture
  • 升级步骤与隐性判断Escalation and hidden judgement

共同分类Classify together

  • 可以自动Can automate
  • 需要辅助Needs assistance
  • 必须由人决定Must remain human-decided

授权观察Authorised observation

  • 录屏范围与个人信息Recording scope and personal data
  • 电话/屏幕授权与日志Call/screen consent and logs
  • 原材料留存与停止条件Raw material retention and stop condition

后台自动Background automation

标准、低风险、可回放的查询或任务。Standard, low-risk, replayable queries or tasks.

坐席辅助Agent assistance

敏感沟通、例外处理和高风险动作保留坐席责任。Keep agent responsibility for sensitive communication, exceptions and high-risk actions.
