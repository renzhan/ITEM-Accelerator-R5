Data Hub：可信事实与可追溯上下文Data Hub: trusted facts and traceable context

互联不是完整，也不等于可训练；每个输入都要保留来源、时间、版本、权限和质量。Connected does not mean complete or trainable; every input keeps source, time, version, access and quality.

可用输入Possible inputs

  • CRM/订单、维修、物料库存、物流 ETA。
  • UKM/EKP/EKF、SOP/PRD、邮件/历史记录。
  • 授权代码与多模态提取结果。
  • CRM/orders, field service, materials/inventory, logistics ETA.
  • UKM/EKP/EKF, SOP/PRD, email and history.
  • Authorized code and multimodal extraction results.

可信上下文Trusted context

  • 映射统一业务定义，保留来源和有效时间。
  • 接入前确认用途、权限、质量和责任 Owner。
  • 按场景开放，不向所有 Agent 默认开放。
  • Map to shared business definitions while keeping provenance and validity.
  • Confirm purpose, access, quality and owner before connection.
  • Expose by scenario, not to every Agent by default.
