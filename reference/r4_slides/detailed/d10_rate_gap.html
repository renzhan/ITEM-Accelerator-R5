Rate & Quote：业务与开发的语言断点Rate & Quote: where business and code diverge

业务说费率、报价、运输条件与可执行方案；开发看到系统、表格和字段。数据能提供结构线索，却不能自行提供全部业务语义。Business speaks in rates, quotes, transport conditions and executable options; development sees systems, tables and fields. Data offers structural clues, not the full business meaning.

断点发生在哪里Where the break occurs

业务语言Business language

客户、服务、运输、费率、报价、条件、例外。Customer, service, transport, rate, quote, condition, exception.

↔

数据语言Data language

不同系统、表格、字段、状态和计算实现。Different systems, tables, fields, states and calculations.

同一个字段、规则或计算条件可能被双方理解成不同含义。The same field, rule or calculation condition may mean different things to each side.

解决不是再写一层 PromptThe answer is not another prompt layer

  • 从授权真实数据识别对象、字段、关联与状态候选。Use authorised real data to identify candidate objects, fields, relations and states.
  • 让业务专家补充数据看不到的例外、定义与边界。Let business experts add exceptions, definitions and boundaries absent from data.
  • 再把审核过的语义连接到分析页面与应用表单。Connect reviewed semantics to analysis pages and application forms.
