MD §D2.5.2 · Day 2

近期共建、中期资产化、后续复制的能力路线图Capability Roadmap: Build, Productize, Replicate

路线图只表达建议顺序：先把真实场景和证据做实，再沉淀资产，最后讨论复制与产品化。The roadmap expresses a recommended sequence: prove a real scenario and evidence, then build assets, then consider replication and productization.

NEAR TERM · 近期共建

锁定事实与小范围交付Prove a narrow scenario

  • 锁定首场景、备选、排除项与验收证据
  • 登记材料、样本、规则、Owner 与执行边界
  • 按条件进入样本验证或适配，不假设上线
  • Lock first scenario, alternatives, exclusions and acceptance evidence
  • Register materials, samples, rules, Owner and execution boundaries
  • Enter validation or adaptation conditionally; do not assume launch

MID TERM · 中期资产化

可观测、可回归、可复用Make it observable and reusable

  • 补齐能力卡、权限、接口、日志、成本与版本
  • 用 Marketplace 技术层和 Dashboard 业务层观察
  • 形成回归、回滚、Training、运维和退出责任
  • Complete asset card, access, interfaces, logs, cost and versions
  • Observe through technical Marketplace and business Dashboard
  • Define regression, rollback, Training, operations and exit

LATER · 后续复制

成熟后再扩展与产品化Replicate after maturity

  • 依据真实结果判断相邻业务是否适合复制
  • 保留差异、授权、正式审批和业务 Owner
  • 服务化、平台化和未来路线均待确认
  • Use real outcomes to judge adjacent-business replication
  • Keep differences, authorization, formal approval and Owners
  • Service, platform and future roadmap remain to be confirmed
不要混线：七阶段是项目推进路径；这里是能力从共建到复用的建议路线图。Keep distinct: seven stages are a project path; this is a recommendation from co-build to reuse.
