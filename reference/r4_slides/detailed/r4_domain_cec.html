CEC：在哪一条坐席流程验证 AI CEC: where to validate AI in the agent flow

先定位坐席任务与承诺节点，再判断知识检索、分类、分派或 Action 是否值得验证；不预设 CEC/Genesys AI 已成熟或必须替换入口。 Locate the agent task and promise point first, then test retrieval, classification, routing or Action; do not assume CEC/Genesys AI is mature or must replace the entry.

PROBLEM TO VERIFY

流程断点 Process breakpoint

  • 客服、承诺、分派、知识检索中哪一步重复或失真？
  • 现有入口、SOP、文本与处理结果如何记录？
  • Which step in service, promise, routing or retrieval repeats or fails?
  • How are current entry, SOP, text and outcome recorded?
MATERIALS

需带到现场 Bring to the room

  • 脱敏通话/工单文本、SOP、意图与分派规则。
  • 坐席、主管、客户承诺和权限定义。
  • Redacted call/ticket text, SOPs, intent and routing rules.
  • Agent, supervisor, customer promise and access definitions.
VALIDATION BOUNDARY

验证与人工边界 Validation & human boundary

  • 用正常/异常/边界样本测准确性、周期、转人工率。
  • 承诺、敏感查询和外呼 Action 先人工确认。
  • Test accuracy, cycle time and handoff rate on normal, abnormal and boundary samples.
  • Human confirmation first for promises, sensitive queries and outbound Actions.
OUTPUT / OWNER

现场输出 Room output

输出一个待验证坐席任务、基线与拒答/升级条件。ISS Biz 坐席/运营 Owner 待确认；ITEM 记录，COO AI Team 提供方法，COO AI Team 按需查接口。 Output one agent task, baseline and refusal/escalation rules to validate. ISS Biz agent/operations Owner to confirm; ITEM records, COO AI provides method, COO AI Team checks interfaces as needed.

讨论焦点Discussion 首个任务是知识检索、意图分类、分派还是承诺辅助？证据与人工接管点是什么？ Is the first task retrieval, intent classification, routing or promise assistance? What evidence and takeover point apply?
