CFS＋Depot：小而杂的流程快速上线CFS + Depot: launch small, messy workflows fast

先选一个可控的小流程，把 SOP 变成可运行的 Workflow；正式名称、范围和系统归属由 ISS Biz 确认。Start with a bounded workflow and turn the SOP into a runnable workflow; ISS Biz confirms names, scope and system ownership.

拆解 SOPDecompose the SOP

  • 步骤、角色、状态和输入。
  • 校验、分支、异常与后续任务。
  • Steps, roles, states and inputs.
  • Validations, branches, exceptions and follow-up tasks.

连接运行面Connect the runtime

  • 必要表单、数据与允许动作。
  • 明确谁可启动、确认和接管。
  • Required forms, data and permitted actions.
  • Make start, approval and takeover roles explicit.

证据而非流程图Evidence, not a flowchart

  • 观察状态回写和异常接管。
  • 记录上线时间、错误、返工与采用。
  • Observe state writeback and exception takeover.
  • Record lead time, errors, rework and adoption.
