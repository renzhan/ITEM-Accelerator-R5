业务价值：基线、成本与实际结果Business Value: Baseline, Cost and Outcomes

价值不能用历史收益或毛节省代替；先建立同口径基线，再核算新增运行、维护和人工审核成本。Value cannot be replaced by historical benefits or gross savings; establish a common baseline and include new run, maintenance and review costs.

效率与净成本Efficiency & net cost

  • 处理时间、人工确认、返工
  • 单位任务成本、Token/算力、维护成本
  • 净成本须扣除新增运行与必要人工审核
  • Handling time, human confirmation and rework
  • Unit-task cost, tokens/compute and maintenance
  • Net cost includes new run and required human review

业务结果Business outcomes

  • Customer Promise、BO 关闭、物流异常
  • 维修/流程周期、客户体验
  • 必须有基线、同一统计范围与业务 Owner 验证
  • Customer Promise, BO closure and logistics exceptions
  • Repair/process cycle and customer experience
  • Require a baseline, same scope and business-owner validation

复用与表达Reuse & framing

优先讲减少重复工作、提升服务、扩大同等团队处理能力；不把价值只讲成减员。

Lead with less repetition, better service and more capacity for the same team—not only headcount reduction.
