MD §D1.2.4｜样本、审核与评测MD §D1.2.4 | Samples, review and evaluation

补全样本与专家审核形成可测规则Complete samples and review into measurable rules

规则质量来自样本边界和专家判定，而不是把调试数据当成证明；合成样本必须标识来源。Rule quality comes from sample boundaries and expert judgement, not treating debug data as proof; synthetic samples must be labelled.

样本要覆盖什么What samples should cover

NORMAL正常路径与常见输入Normal paths and common inputs
EXCEPTION合理例外与冲突状态Valid exceptions and conflicts
MISSING缺失字段和不完整上下文Missing fields and incomplete context
BOUNDARY应拒答、转交或暂停的边界Boundaries for refusal, transfer or pause

调试与独立评测分开Separate debug from evaluation

  • 专家先确认规则、结果与可接受动作。Experts first confirm rules, results and acceptable Actions.
  • 调试/补全样本用于修正，不计入独立评测分母。Debug and completion samples fix the system; they do not enter the independent denominator.
  • 独立样本按版本记录正确、失败、拒答和人工接管。Independent samples record correct, failed, refused and human-taken-over outcomes by version.
  • 错误回写 WIKI、规则、样本与本体，再回归。Feed errors back to WIKI, rules, samples and ontology, then regress.
讨论焦点：Discussion: 哪位专家判定 BO 结果？哪些样本只能用于调试？ISS 能否准备独立、带版本的评测集？Which expert judges BO outcomes? Which samples are debug-only? Can ISS prepare an independent, versioned evaluation set?
