Rate & Quote 的验证与迁移条件Rate & Quote: validation before migration

先用边界清晰的小范围证明语义、规则和页面共用同一本体，再谈向相邻流程或地区迁移；项目证据不能混同。First prove shared semantics, rules and pages in a bounded scope; only then consider adjacent flows or regions. Evidence from different projects must not be conflated.

一条可审计的证据链An auditable evidence chain

01 · SOURCE授权数据与来源版本Authorised data and source version
02 · EXPERT专家确认定义与例外Expert confirms definitions and exceptions
03 · SMALL SCOPE一个可复现的业务边界One reproducible business boundary
04 · FEEDBACK分析、表单和结果回馈Feedback from analysis, form and result

没有完整实测结果时，标为验证计划，不写成已实现成效。Without complete measurement, label it as a validation plan—not an achieved result.

迁移候选，不是自动同项目Candidates are not the same project

  • UKM / PEI：可作为后续最佳实践候选，分别确认业务目标。UKM / PEI: candidates for later practice transfer, with goals confirmed separately.
  • BNP：仅作本体与应用迁移的补充材料，不与本次 UNIS Rate & Quote 混同。BNP: supplementary material for ontology/application transfer, not the same as this UNIS Rate & Quote case.
  • 门槛：语义、规则、权限、接口、Owner 和可回放结果都能说明。Gate: semantics, rules, access, interfaces, Owners and replayable results are explainable.
讨论焦点：Discussion: 首个小范围由谁签收？UKM、PEI 与 BNP 哪些只是相关证据，哪些具备可迁移条件？Who signs off the first bounded scope? Which parts of UKM, PEI and BNP are related evidence, and which meet transfer conditions?
