MD §D2.3.2 · 能力资产生命周期 I

能力生命周期 I：草稿 → 审核 → 样本 → 认证Capability lifecycle I: draft to certification

合成资产来自真实流程和审核，不等同于七阶段项目路径；本页只覆盖发布前四状态，后四状态由下一页继续。Synthesized assets come from real process and review, separate from the seven-stage project path; this page covers only the four pre-release states.

01 · CANDIDATE
候选 / 草稿Candidate / draft
  • 问题、来源、范围、初步 Owner、排除项与风险。
  • 交付：候选卡；推测与合成样本须标记。
  • Problem, source, scope, initial Owner, exclusions and risks.
  • Output: candidate card; mark inferred and synthetic samples.
02 · EXPERT REVIEW
专家审核Expert review
  • 核对知识、流程、规则、例外、有效期与边界。
  • 交付：审核意见、版本与 Owner。
  • Check knowledge, process, rules, exceptions, validity and boundary.
  • Output: review comments, version and Owner.
03 · SAMPLE VALIDATION
样本验证Sample validation
  • 用正常/异常/边界样本区分回答、分类、预测、优化、执行。
  • 交付：指标、错误、判定与人工接管记录。
  • Use normal, abnormal and boundary samples across answer, classify, predict, optimize and execute.
  • Output: metrics, errors, decisions and takeover record.
04 · REGISTERED
登记认证Registration & certification
  • 补齐权限、接口、成本、部署、日志、版本、责任与回滚。
  • 交付：能力卡与认证结论；未满足项保持待确认。
  • Complete access, interfaces, cost, deployment, logs, version, accountability and rollback.
  • Output: capability card and certification conclusion; open items stay to confirm.
讨论焦点Discussion选择一个 D2.2 候选能力，现场确定进入哪一状态、缺什么证据、谁批准；不把“演示”写成“认证”。Choose one D2.2 candidate: decide its state, missing evidence and approver; do not turn a demo into certification.
