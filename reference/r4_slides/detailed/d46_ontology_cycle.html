共建闭环Co-building loop

采集Collect→提取Extract→业务/专家审核Review→对象·关系·规则·ActionModel→样本验证Validate→发布与反馈Release & feedback

按复杂度选规模Choose scale by complexity

  • 简单、稳定任务：轻量规则或结构化模板。
  • 跨系统、状态和约束：小范围本体与授权数据。
  • 复杂任务：逐步扩展，不以“全组织本体”为起点。
  • Simple, stable tasks: lightweight rules or structured templates.
  • Cross-system states and constraints: bounded ontology and authorized data.
  • Complex tasks: expand gradually, not from an enterprise-wide ontology.
