MD §D2.3.3–D2.3.4 · Day 2

持续复用与版本进化，不是一刀切搬迁Reuse and Evolve, Do Not Migrate Blindly

先把已有 Agent、模型、代码和环境登记清楚，再按成熟度选择复用、适配或服务化；七阶段项目路径不是资产生命周期。Register existing Agents, models, code and environments first; then choose reuse, adaptation or service based on maturity. The seven-stage project path is not the asset lifecycle.

01 · REGISTER

登记与复用Register and reuse

  • 保留来源、当前版本、Owner、边界与依赖
  • 已有能力先接入评测标准，不假设已认证
  • 可复用的输入、规则和反馈进入能力卡
  • Keep source, current version, Owner, boundaries and dependencies
  • Apply evaluation standards before assuming certification
  • Move reusable inputs, rules and feedback into the asset card

02 · ADAPT

按成熟度适配Adapt by maturity

  • 样本、权限、日志或接口不足时先缩小范围
  • 明确人工确认、拒答、升级和回滚边界
  • 只补真正缺口，不把项目交付物当资产认证
  • Narrow scope when samples, access, logs or interfaces are incomplete
  • Define human confirmation, refusal, escalation and rollback
  • Fill real gaps; project delivery is not asset certification

03 · EVOLVE

版本进化与复制Evolve and replicate

  • 知识、规则、模型、接口变化触发回归
  • 成熟后再服务化或复制到相邻业务
  • 每次扩展保留差异、批准与退出条件
  • Knowledge, rule, model or interface changes trigger regression
  • Service-ify or replicate only after maturity
  • Keep differences, approval and retirement conditions
两条线并行：七阶段项目管理交付节奏；能力资产生命周期管理长期可用性。Two tracks: the seven-stage project path manages delivery; the asset lifecycle manages long-term usability.
