AI 生成流程与视频观察维修：下一步验证AI-generated workflows and video maintenance observation: next validation

Workflow 草案和视频识别都是后续验证项；它们不能跳过授权、复核和业务责任。Workflow drafts and video recognition are future validations; neither bypasses consent, review or business accountability.

草案来源Draft source

  • 从 SOP 与已授权观察材料提出流程草案。
  • 先由业务确认关键动作与例外。
  • Propose workflow drafts from SOPs and authorized observations.
  • Business owners confirm key actions and exceptions first.

视频验证Video validation

  • 单独测关键动作的识别偏差。
  • 记录误报、漏报和复核决定。
  • Test recognition error on key actions separately.
  • Record false positives, misses and review decisions.

明确边界Explicit boundary

  • 不直接当维修质量证明。
  • 不未经审核用于人员评价或高风险控制。
  • Do not treat it as proof of maintenance quality.
  • Do not use it for people evaluation or high-risk control without review.
