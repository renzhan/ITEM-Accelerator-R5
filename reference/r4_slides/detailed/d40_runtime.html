Runtime：使用、标准化与后台执行Runtime: use, standardization and background execution

让业务人员用标准入口表达目标；重复任务可后台运行，但必须保留异常接管。Let business users express goals through standard entry points; repetitive work may run in background with exception takeover.

使用Use

  • 基于业务术语、字段、模板、权限和 Action。
  • 服务坐席、仓库、现场服务和普通用户。
  • Use business terms, fields, templates, access and Actions.
  • Serve agents, warehouses, field service and business users.

标准化Standardization

  • 创新使用标准组件和边界。
  • 避免无限个人定制和无法维护的孤岛。
  • Innovation uses standard components and boundaries.
  • Avoid unmaintainable personal customization and silos.

Robotics / Token SavingRobotics / Token Saving

  • 状态监控、整理、派发、同步和提醒可事件驱动。
  • 简单任务先用规则/小模型，复杂任务再升档。
  • Monitoring, organization, dispatch, sync and reminders can be event-driven.
  • Use rules/small models first; escalate complex tasks.
