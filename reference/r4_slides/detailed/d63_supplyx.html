SupplyX：先确认范围，再比较复用边界SupplyX: Confirm Scope Before Comparing Reuse

SupplyX 的具体产品、内部版本、责任团队、能力范围与成熟度当前未知；不按名称臆测功能，也不预设替代。The SupplyX product, internal version, team, scope and maturity are unknown; do not infer function or assume replacement.

先补事实Confirm first

  • 具体产品/版本、责任团队、用户入口
  • 已有数据、本体、模型、Agent/Skill 与成熟度
  • Product/version, responsible team and user entry
  • Existing data, ontology, models, Agents/Skills and maturity

同场景比较Compare in one scenario

  • 流程覆盖、接口与 Action、规则与评测
  • 权限、治理、部署、运营成本与产品责任
  • Flow coverage, interfaces/Actions, rules and evaluation
  • Access, governance, deployment, operations cost and product ownership

边界分类Boundary categories

直接复用｜标准接入｜补充开发｜确有重复｜暂不涉及

Direct reuse | Standard integration | Add-on build | True overlap | Out of scope

决策输出Decision output

形成能力边界表，选择一个同场景验证；不是先比功能清单再决定迁移。

Create a capability boundary table and validate one shared scenario; do not decide migration from feature lists.
