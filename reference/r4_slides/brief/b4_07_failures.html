失败经验：知道该缩小、停止或转交什么Failure Lessons: What to Narrow, Stop or Hand Off

失败不是证明 AI 一概不可用，而是把问答、辅助分析、建议与正式决策分开评价，保留条件、样本与责任。Failure does not prove AI is useless; separate Q&A, assisted analysis, recommendations and formal decisions, keeping conditions, samples and ownership.

iCEO：问答 ≠ 决策iCEO: Q&A ≠ Decision

可验证的问答，不能替代复杂经营决策Verifiable Q&A cannot replace complex business decisions
  • 有权限且可检索的知识范围内，问答可继续验证；证据不足要拒答或转人工。Continue validating Q&A within authorized, retrievable knowledge; refuse or escalate when evidence is missing.
  • 当前决策表现未达到业务门槛；本体不能补足缺失事实、样本、目标和专家判断。Current decision performance is below the business bar; an ontology cannot supply missing facts, samples, goals or judgment.

其他坑：把失败变成门槛Other traps: turn failure into gates

  • 知识多不等于可用：要有来源、有效期、审核人与真实任务。More knowledge is not usability: keep source, validity, reviewer and real task.
  • 先稳住检索、规则、权限与评测，再谈自建模型；避免短效投资。Stabilize retrieval, rules, permissions and evaluation before building models.
  • HR、敏感信息与高风险动作不能以试验绕过法规、审批或人的责任。Do not bypass law, approval or human accountability for HR, sensitive data or high-risk actions.
  • 形成四类：该做｜先缩小｜暂不做｜需要其他团队处理。Classify into do, narrow first, do not do yet, or hand off.
会中要问　ASK IN THE ROOM　哪个候选场景应缩小？什么证据让它停止？哪个问题必须转交正式业务、数据、安全或架构责任人？Which scenario should narrow? What evidence stops it? Which issue must go to formal business, data, security or architecture owners?
