共享底座、业务工具与双向回写Shared foundations, tools and write-back

让可信输入连接受控行动，再把业务结果与经审核的知识回到平台；统一治理，不是一刀切迁移。Connect trusted inputs to governed actions, then return business results and reviewed knowledge to the platform.

能力底座：按任务协同Task-oriented foundations

Data Hub 管事实、来源和权限；Model Hub 协同理解、分类、预测与优化。Connector 连接系统，Tools 封装查询与行动，Skill 组织复用能力。Data Hub governs facts and access. Model Hub routes understanding, classification, forecasting and optimization. Connectors link systems; Tools package actions; Skills support reuse.

三类工作面：共用治理Three working surfaces

ITEMGPT Runtime 供业务人员使用 Agent、动态表单与流程；Agent Factory 供专业人员建设、评测和治理；Ontology Studio 共建对象、关系、规则与 Action。Runtime runs agents, forms and workflows. Factory supports professional building, evaluation and governance. Ontology Studio aligns objects, relations, rules and actions.

两类回写：结果与能力Two write-back paths

业务平台接收授权任务结果、状态与确认记录；知识能力体系接收日志、失败和经审核的人工作业修正。建议、调用成功、业务完成必须区分。Write authorized task results to business systems; return logs, failures and reviewed corrections to knowledge and capabilities. Separate suggestions, successful calls and completed actions.

技术目录与业务观测分开Technical discovery, business value

Marketplace 属于技术能力层，管理发现、复用、版本、权限与质量；Dashboard 面向人员与管理者，呈现采用、质量、风险、成本与业务结果。Marketplace governs discoverable technical capabilities, versions, access and quality. Dashboard tracks adoption, risk, cost and outcomes for business users and managers.
