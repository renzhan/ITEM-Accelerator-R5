从观察到执行：Record & Reply、CEC、CFS / DepotFrom Observation to Execution: Record & Reply, CEC, CFS / Depot

先让真实操作变成可审核知识，再决定哪些可自动、需辅助或必须由人负责；视频处理与原始留存是不同决定。Turn real work into auditable knowledge first, then decide what can be automated, assisted or human-owned; processing and raw-video retention are separate decisions.

Record & ReplyRecord & Reply

视频 → SOP → Agent → 执行
  • 授权采集操作、判断、切换、例外与结果；业务人员和 Owner 审核 SOP 草稿。With authorization, capture steps, judgments, switches, exceptions and outcomes; operators and owners review the SOP draft.
  • 沙盒验证正常/异常路径，失败与人工修正回写知识。Validate normal and exception paths in a sandbox; feed failures and corrections back to knowledge.

CEC＋GenesysCEC + Genesys

经授权录屏 → CEC SOP → Agent → 受控集成Authorized recording → CEC SOP → Agent → controlled integration
  • 区分可自动、需辅助、必须人决策；Connector/Tools 接入允许的查询与回复。Separate automate, assist and human-decision tasks; connect permitted queries and replies with Connector/Tools.
  • 无头化不等于取消坐席；敏感沟通、例外和高风险动作保留责任。Headless operation does not remove agents; sensitive communication, exceptions and high-risk actions retain human responsibility.

CFS＋DepotCFS + Depot

SOP → Workflow → 自然语言调整 → 回归发布SOP → workflow → natural-language change → regression release
  • 验证状态回写、版本、未完成任务、权限、异常接管与回滚。Verify status write-back, versions, unfinished tasks, permissions, hand-off and rollback.
  • AI 生成流程与维修视频观察仍是后续验证项，不当作已交付。AI-generated workflows and repair-video observation remain validation directions, not delivered capabilities.
