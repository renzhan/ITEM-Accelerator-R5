MD §appendix-a / D2.6.5 · TWO-DAY PREPARATION

参会准备：真实材料、权限与 OwnerPrepare evidence, access and owners

准备能让现场判断基于真实证据，区分可运行、样例和未来方向，而不是把下一步能力写成已交付。Bring evidence for real decisions; distinguish runnable capabilities, sample demonstrations and future validation.

六个业务域的材料与分享人Six domains, real evidence

CEC、e-service、BO fulfillment、procurement、parts supply chain、UKM：各确认分享 Owner，至少准备一份可审核流程、SOP、工单或脱敏样本。CEC, e-service, BO fulfillment, procurement, parts supply chain, UKM: identify sharing owners and bring at least one reviewable flow, SOP, ticket or redacted sample per domain.

Hollis 与 COO AI 已有成果Hollis and existing AI capabilities

准备可共享企业 AI 案例、方法框架、已有成果、架构与治理样例；明确实际成熟度、开放范围、接口和能力 Owner，用于企业实践及资产讨论。Bring shareable enterprise cases, methods, existing results, architecture and governance examples. Clarify actual maturity, access, interfaces and capability owners.

案例能运行到哪一步Runnable, sample or future?

确认工单、WMS 代码与本地环境、BO 样本、视频授权和接口。标注真实／脱敏／合成；自有模型、视频维修观察和推广仍按证据标明“待验证”。Confirm tickets, WMS code and environment, BO samples, video consent and interfaces. Label real, redacted or synthetic inputs; identify future model, video and commercialization validation.

四字段、权限与后续责任Four fields, authority and follow-up

准备场景与验收、团队与 Owner、能力与执行边界、推进方式与下一门。确认数据、源码、录屏、模型、日志和部署权限；带具备确认职责的参与人。Prepare scenario and acceptance, team and owners, inputs and execution boundaries, and the next gate. Confirm data, source, video, model, log and deployment permissions.

会前关注　Pre-read focus　哪些材料和审批仍待补齐，由谁在何时补齐？Which evidence and approvals are still missing, and who will provide them by when?
