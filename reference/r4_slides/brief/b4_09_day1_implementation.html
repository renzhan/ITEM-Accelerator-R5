先对齐风险，再形成 ISS 实施初步判断Align Risk First, Then Form a Preliminary ISS Delivery View

风险决定验收与边界；实施讨论只形成候选与输入，最终决定留到 Day 2 的真实材料与责任确认。Risk defines acceptance and boundaries; Day 1 delivery is a candidate view, with final decisions reserved for Day 2 evidence and ownership.

先问四类风险Ask four risk questions first

  • 准确性：Accuracy:任务、分母、独立样本、高风险错误与人工接管是什么？task, denominator, independent samples, high-risk errors and human hand-off?
  • 融合：Integration:与 SupplyX、Copilot、AP 的边界是复用、接入、补充还是暂不涉及？reuse, connect, complement or not address SupplyX, Copilot and AP?
  • 价值：Value:基线、任务完成、质量、成本、采用与业务结果如何关联？how do baseline, completion, quality, cost, adoption and outcomes connect?
  • 复用：Reuse:现有 Agent 先登记、评测、标准接入，再按成熟度服务化。register, evaluate and standardize existing agents before service based on maturity.

再谈七阶段共建Then discuss seven-stage co-build

盘点 → 底座 → 首场景 → Training → 优化 → 产品化 → 复制Map → foundation → first scenario → training → optimize → productize → replicate
  • Day 1 只写首个候选、范围/排除项、输入、评测、Owner 与下一门。Day 1 records only candidate, scope/exclusions, inputs, evaluation, owners and next gate.
  • Open → Transform → Engineer：探索可保留；真实流程中补齐权限、部署、监控与评估。Open → Transform → Engineer: keep exploration, then add permissions, deployment, monitoring and evaluation.
  • 模型、接口、Marketplace、服务化和未来路线均是建议/待确认，不当作已交付。Models, interfaces, Marketplace, service and future roadmaps are proposals/to confirm—not delivered.
Day 1 交接　DAY 1 HANDOFF　把风险转成四字段里的验收条件、依赖、人工边界和证据清单；Day 2 用真实业务域与能力材料深化。Turn risks into acceptance conditions, dependencies, human boundaries and evidence in the four fields; deepen with real domains and capability material on Day 2.
