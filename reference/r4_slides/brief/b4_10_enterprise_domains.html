D2.1 · Hollis 的分享D2.1 · Hollis’s share

60 分钟 · 方法与证据60 min · method and evidence
  • 业务 know-how、流程、数据治理、模型、IT 集成与跨团队协作如何沉淀。How know-how, process, data governance, models, IT integration and collaboration are codified.
  • 样本、专家、评测、版本、权限与反馈如何形成 Skill、Workflow、模型或 Agent。How samples, experts, evaluation, versions, permissions and feedback form reusable assets.
  • 输出可验证做法、前提、证据与不可外推项；不自动等同于 ISS 已具备能力。Output practices, prerequisites, evidence and non-transferable items; not proof that ISS already has them.
