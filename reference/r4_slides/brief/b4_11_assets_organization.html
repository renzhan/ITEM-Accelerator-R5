沉淀能力资产，连接业务与组织Capability assets and business ownership

演示不是认证：能力要进入可追踪生命周期，业务判断、建设与持续运行也要有责任人。A demo is not certification: capabilities need a traceable lifecycle, accountable business decisions and sustainable operations.

沉淀什么、记录什么Assets and evidence

知识、SOP、本体、数据／模型、Tools／Skill、Agent、Workflow 与评测资产。记录来源、范围、Owner、权限、版本、指标、依赖、日志、成本和退出条件；原始、推测及合成材料需标注。Knowledge, SOPs, ontology, data/models, Tools/Skills, Agents, Workflows and evaluations. Record source, scope, owner, access, version, metrics, dependencies, logs, cost and exit criteria; label inferred or synthetic material.

发布前后八个状态An eight-state lifecycle

候选／草稿 → 专家审核 → 样本验证 → 登记认证。
发布接入 → 运行观测 → 回归／版本更新 → 退出替换。Candidate/draft → expert review → sample validation → registration/certification.
Release → observation → regression/versioning → retirement/replacement.

三方各自承担的责任Complementary ownership

ISS Biz：目标、规则、例外、价值、验收与采用。goals, rules, exceptions, value and acceptance.

COO AI / Hollis：方法、已有成果与能力治理。methods, proven capabilities and governance.

ITEM / FDE：建模、编排、评测与工程交付。modeling, orchestration, evaluation and delivery.

复用、采用与持续运行Reuse, adoption and operations

Marketplace 管技术能力发现；Dashboard 看业务采用和结果。专家审核，业务保留接管，运维负责版本与监测；COO AI Team 按需提供数据、代码、接口和基础设施。Marketplace supports technical discovery; Dashboard tracks business adoption and outcomes. Experts review, business retains hand-off, operations maintain versions, and COO AI Team provides data/code/interfaces/infrastructure when needed.

会前关注　Pre-read focus　谁审核、谁维护、谁接管？会议共识不替代正式审批。Who reviews, maintains and takes over? Workshop consensus does not replace formal approval.
